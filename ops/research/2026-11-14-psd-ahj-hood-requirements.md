# PSD IFB 27-701-002: what the three fire authorities require of hood cleaners (worker, 2026-11-14)

Why: IFB 3.1 says the vendor "must hold, and provide proof of, all licenses, certifications, registrations, and approvals
required by each applicable AHJ ... before the first service", and 7.1 asks us to certify our people meet them. HOODZ
(Larry Moss, 2026-11-14, 1a1dba2a12da814e) will finish NFPA-mirroring franchise training by Feb 28 but will NOT get IKECA.
So the question is: does any of the three AHJs require IKECA, or a registration HOODZ lacks?

Method: workflow wf_758ddfad-210 (3 finders + 1 skeptical verifier). **Limit: every AHJ website is blocked from the cloud
session** (poudre-fire.org, lfra.org, wfpd.org, wellingtoncolorado.gov, fcgov.com, thecomplianceengine.com). Everything
below is from search-result snippets the verifier reproduced ("SNIPPET-ONLY"); no page was read live. Do not quote any of
this in a bid document until the pages are read.

## Bottom line

Read-out for the 7.1 statement: nothing found says IKECA is required at any of the three. That is consistent with
HOODZ doing NFPA 96 work with trained, named technicians (Carlos Ahedo as inspector). **It is not yet proof**: PFA's two
contractor-qualification documents are unread. Hood SUPPRESSION is a separate licensed trade at PFA and LFRA, and the IFB
excludes fire-suppression service, so it is out of our scope.

## Must read before Luke signs 7.1 (all blocked from the cloud; HQ's VM or Luke's browser can fetch)
1. PFA **Notice 2026-02, "Minimum Contractor Qualifications for Required Inspection, Testing, & Maintenance (ITM) of Fire
   Protection Systems"**: poudre-fire.org, Inspection Services Division page.
2. PFA **Minimum Contractor Qualifications Guidebook**: poudre-fire.org, Contractors / Plan Reviews and Permits page.
3. PFA **Brycer - The Compliance Engine Notices** page (which report types file in TCE, vendor fee, how to register).
4. Fort Collins Ordinance No. 051, 2025 (2024 IFC amendments), local text of section 606.3.3 (cleaning; who may clean).
5. LFRA 2024 IFC amendments (lfra.org DocumentCenter/View/808): confirm no change to commercial kitchen hood cleaning.

If item 1 or 2 names IKECA (or any third-party certification) for exhaust cleaning in PFA's area, HOODZ cannot carry
7.1 and an alternate (Aqua Strong / Elite / Brotherhood, cert copies asked by Mon 11/16) must.
