# Insurance coverage by bid: check before signing (2026-11-13)

For Luke, before he buys the Brown Bison policy today (Fri 2026-11-13). Every limit below is quoted from the bid's own
documents; the page refs are in the last column of the matrix. Nothing here is a quote or price, and nothing here says a
policy is in force. On almost every bid, proof of insurance is due **at award or before work starts, not with the bid**.

## 1. Summary: the highest limit each coverage needs

1. **CGL:** $2,000,000 each occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate / $2,000,000 products-completed ops. Driver: **Widefield
   RFP 26-27-013 (GO)**. Every other GO/REVIVE bid needs $1,000,000 per occurrence, with aggregates up to $2,000,000 (PSD,
   Lafayette). Long shot: CCC Stout LED needs $5,000,000 per occurrence (NO-BID unless Luke overrules by today 17:00).
2. **Auto:** $1,000,000 CSL, any auto (owned, hired, non-owned). Drivers: BVCC, COS janitorial/snow, PSD, Widefield, La Plata. No bid asks for more.
3. **Workers comp:** statutory on every bid that has an insurance clause (Pagosa and BOR have none). **Employers liability:** highest is $1,000,000. Drivers: COS R26-080JW/082JW
   ("minimum limit of $1,000,000") and Canon City ($500,000 accident / $1,000,000 disease policy / $1,000,000 disease each employee).
4. **Umbrella/excess:** no GO/REVIVE bid requires one. Widefield marks it "Waived". PSD and Weld let an umbrella count toward the limits. Only CCC ($5M CGL) would force one.
5. **Professional/E&O:** $1,000,000 per occurrence or wrongful act / $3,000,000 annual aggregate, plus proof of an
   extended reporting period at project end. Driver: **PSD IFB 27-701-002 (GO)**. PSD will look at an exception if it comes
   in with the bid. Also: Canon City $600,000/$1,000,000; Widefield $1,000,000; BVCC and Lafayette only if their conditions apply.
6. **Employee crime / third-party fidelity:** $1,000,000 per loss. Driver: **COS janitorial R26-080JW (REVIVE)**. BVCC asks
   for $1,000,000/$1,000,000 only if staff can get at state money, securities or inventory, and that list names janitors.
7. **Cyber:** $1,000,000 each occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate. Driver: **Widefield (marked Req)**. BVCC needs the same only if we touch protected offender information.
8. **Pollution:** no GO/REVIVE bid requires it (Widefield waived it). **Master key + care-custody-control:** CGL endorsements for COS janitorial; no separate limit is stated.
9. **Priority (GO/REVIVE):** Widefield, COS janitorial, COS snow, PSD hood, BVCC, Canon City boiler, Lafayette,
   Pagosa (no insurance clause at all), La Plata. **Long shots (EVALUATE / REVIVE-IF / AT RISK / default NO-BID):** Canon
   City janitorial, CPW heater, Arapahoe 26-64 and 26-57, Trails, COS parks irrigation, Lakewood, Weld cabling, Fort Collins doors, Adams County, Westminster, Aims, CCC Stout LED, BOR Alamogordo.
10. **Proof due WITH the proposal (not at award):** Lafayette 12/18 (a COI *or* a statement that we can meet the
    requirements, plus a loss run and OSHA 300A), COS 11/23 (signed and initialed Exhibit 1 with the insurer's name),
    Arapahoe 26-64 and 26-57 (a "Certificate of Liability Insurance" with the bid). BVCC conflicts with itself: DQ 12.B lists a COI in the quote contents.
