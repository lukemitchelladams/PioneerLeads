Douglas County posted Q&A 1 on the UPS bid (IFB 039-26), attached. The key answers:

Emergency service is required, with a technician on site within 3 hours. PM-only bids won't meet it.

Three technicians must be based within 75 miles of Douglas County. Manufacturer training or industry certifications count in place of a state license.

Equivalent replacement batteries are acceptable. The bid schedule's "Monthly Maintenance Cost" column is the cost of one PM visit per site.

Pricing by Monday, November 2 at noon still works.
