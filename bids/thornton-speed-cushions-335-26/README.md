## Scope summary (Exhibit A, Exhibits 1-2 to Exhibit A)

"Thornton requires one (1) or more firms to perform installation, up-keep and/or maintenance for speed cushions and speed
bumps." (p.3). Agreement II.A (p.13): "Contractors shall provide all material, equipment and labor necessary to install Asphalt
Speed Cushions on an "On-Call" basis." Exhibit A.B (p.27): "provide, maintain, and/or remove asphalt speed cushions as described
in a given Task Assignment Scope of Work in accordance with Exhibit 1, Details and Exhibit 2, MGPEC Form 9"; remove and dispose
of all waste. Nothing in the 88 pages mentions rubber or modular cushions; "speed bumps" appears only in the p.3 description.

- **Types (Exhibit 1, pp.31-33, City Traffic Engineering details dated 1-9-2020):** all "have a height of 3.25"", tolerance
  "(+/-) 0.25"" checked with a straightedge at six points per cushion; length and width "(+/-) 3"". Type 1: 78" x 78" overall,
  54" x 30" top. Type 2: 78" wide x 120" long, 54" x 72" top. Type 3: drawn 78" wide (15" ramps, 48" top) x 144" long (36" ramps,
  72" top), but Exhibit C calls it "144"x84"" (ask, Q4). Installed in sets of 3-4 across the street.
- **Markings (p.34, "Typical Speed Cushion Pavement Markings"):** 12" white stripes in preformed plastic tape ahead of the
  cushions and a 12" x 18" white diamond (preformed tape) centered on each cushion. Exhibit C has no markings line (ask, Q4).
- **Mix (Exhibit 2, MGPEC Form #9, p.35):** Street classification "Residential / Collector"; application "Other: Speed Cushion";
  "Grading SX (3.5" Speed Cushion)"; RAP maximum 20%; Ndesign = 75 (0 to <3 million ESALs); binder PG 64-22. "The Contractor shall
  include a copy of this form with each Mix Design submittal after the contract is awarded."
- **Field conditions (Exhibit E):** MUTCD traffic control, one lane each way open or flaggers (XXXIII); 24-hour advance notice
  to owners if driveway access is cut (XXXI); no stockpiling in the ROW beyond the day's use (III.G); safe access by ramping
  (XXX); City water by meter only (XXXIV); warranty "no time limit except those prescribed by law" (XXIX.B).

## Rule 8 check (no excavation / earthwork / heavy civil)

Rule 8 names roadwork. The CEO ruled on 2026-11-20 08:45 MT that trade alone is not a NO-BID reason here (same as Westminster
roofing and Hyland Hills concrete): the work is sub-performed and Brown Bison primes. The documents keep it small: surface-placed
hot-mix cushions 3.25" tall on existing asphalt, and removal of the same. No excavation, grading, drainage or base work is in
Exhibit A; the 811 "excavator" clause (XIV) is general-conditions boilerplate.

## Pricing from documents (Exhibit C; no prices in this file)

The proposal prices no project; it fills Exhibit C: the 9-line unit schedule (material-only and all-other-costs per cushion
type, removal per type), hourly rates for T&M, and the three markup percentages. Method: sub cost x 1.15 (Brown Bison's fee in
the unit prices; "Overhead and Profit" plus "Insurance costs" markups total 15% on T&M; bonds "at actual cost"). HQ's
documents-based worksheet (HMA tonnage computed from the Exhibit 1 dimensions, crew-day production, removal by cold planer) is in
