## Scope summary
Part A: replace the roof-mounted heating and cooling at the PURA office (115 E Riverwalk, Unit 410). Part B: diagnose and repair a leaking
rooftop unit at 125 Riverwalk Pl, with a replacement alternate. In trade (HVAC, NAICS 238220). The RFQ gives no tonnage, make, model or
square footage ("not known", p.2) and has no price sheet or bid form; it asks for a lump sum per part with a breakdown (p.3). No bond,
prevailing-wage or Davis-Bacon language in the RFQ (pp.1-5). Sizes in the private price come from Pueblo County Assessor records (labeled
assumptions there), not from the RFQ; the walk or the sub's survey confirms them.

## Key requirements (quote the document, cite section)
- Part A (PURA office, 115 E Riverwalk, Unit 410): "replace the heating and cooling system"; contractor recommends system type; Manual J load calc;
  removal/disposal incl. refrigerant recovery; ductwork/line sets/venting/gas/electrical/condensate as needed; state whether new equipment works
  with the existing "WattMaster Controls Auto-Zone Control System" (~five zones) and price replacement controls separately; permits and
  inspections with Pueblo Regional Building Department; start-up, test and balance; closeout docs (pp.1-2). Equipment is "roof-mounted"; make,
  model, age and square footage "not known".
- Part B (125 Riverwalk Pl): find and fix a leaking rooftop unit; written findings with photos; repair as a lump sum incl. curb/flashing;
  alternate: replacement unit; recommendation; coordinate roof work so the roof warranty is not voided; "state how quickly you can begin" (pp.2-3).
- Quote contents (p.3): equipment make/model/capacity/efficiency; lump sum with breakdown (equipment, labor, permits, duct/electrical); optional
  alternate; exclusions and unit prices for extras; lead time and working days; manufacturer + contractor labor warranty ("minimum one year
  labor"); rebates; "Three references for similar commercial or office projects completed in the last three years"; "Proof of license and
  insurance"; separate Part A / Part B pricing.
- Qualifications (p.4): Pueblo County mechanical license; three years commercial HVAC; "EPA Section 608 certification is required for anyone
  handling refrigerant".
- Insurance before work starts (p.4), PURA additional insured: CGL "$1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate"; auto "$1,000,000
  combined single limit"; workers' comp "Colorado statutory limits". Carrier approval for Brown Bison is still PENDING: never claim coverage.
- Tax: "PURA is a public entity and is exempt from Colorado sales tax; do not include sales tax." (p.4)
- Contact: Chris DeLuca, Business and Development Director, cdeluca@puebloura.org (questions in writing or through BidNet).
