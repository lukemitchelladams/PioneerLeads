## Scope summary (quoted, Amd 0002 PWS)
- *"inspect, assess, and perform corrective maintenance and repair of fire door assemblies at healthcare facilities"* (6.1.2).
  *"Replacement shall be limited to instances where repair or adjustment cannot achieve compliance."*
- Counts (6.2, *"approximate and provided for planning purposes only"*): AIHC *"Approximately 21 fire door assemblies"* (an
  August 2025 report found deficiencies in about 21), SFIH *"Approximately 18"*, TPIHC *"Approximately 11"*, MIHC
  *"Approximately 14"*. RFI 16: counts are *"fire door assemblies/openings, which may be single or paired"*.
- 6.3.1: within 15 days of NTP, verify every fire- and smoke-rated assembly *"including cross-corridor and hazardous area
  doors"*, and report repair / partial / full replacement and costs per door *"utilizing Appendix A"*; label each door with a
  unique ID; flag ACM-impacted assemblies.
- 6.3.2 / 6.4.2: within 15 days of assessment approval, shop drawings, door schedules, product data, listings, installation
  plans. *"No procurement or installation shall occur prior to Government approval."*
- 6.3.3-6.3.4: approved repairs/replacements with listed/labeled components, functional testing, fire alarm interface check,
  and *"a comprehensive, passing fire door inspection report for each facility"* plus an Excel door inventory (6.4.4).
- 6.6: ACM and lead present. *"The Contractor is not authorized to disturb, remove, or perform work that impacts ACM under this
  contract."* Abatement *"will be performed by the Government under a separate contract or action"*. Lead: EPA RRP practices.
- 6.5: occupied healthcare facilities, PCRA/ICRA approval before corrective work, ILSM where life safety is impaired, work
  *"Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM"*, badges, sign in/out.
- 6.11: *"minimum one (1) year warranty on all labor and materials"*.
- Codes (6.8): NFPA 80, NFPA 101, IHS Technical Handbook, OSHA 1910/1926, OEM. The 2025 AIHC report (DH Pace FireCheck)
  used *"NFPA 80 - 2010 for Healthcare"* and failed all 19 openings it inspected (15 critical, 4 immediate); the most common
  failures were clearances (FC5) and missing frame/hardware labels (FC13.F/FC13.H), 14 openings each.

## Gaps / risks
- **Inspector credential:** neither Albuquerque sub's site states IQP or FDAI. If neither has one, the lead inspector must
  come from a certified third party under our sub (ask in the first email; it is asked).
- **Pricing structure:** corrective quantities are blank; the Government evaluates unit prices. Unbalanced pricing can be
  rejected (Volume 3), so keep each line priced to its own cost.
- **Travel:** all rates are all-inclusive. Mescalero is about 3 h and Taos about 2.5 h from Albuquerque; travel sits inside
  the inspection, final and corrective unit prices for those sites.
- **DH Pace** did the 2025 AIHC inspection and has NM offices: likely competitor.
