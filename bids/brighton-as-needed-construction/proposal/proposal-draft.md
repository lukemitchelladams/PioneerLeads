### 3.1 Understanding of the work

The City wants a contractor it can call for small, as-needed building work: "small office remodels, drywall repairs,
shelving installation, door replacement (excluding storefront), and minor building additions/alterations" (RFP p.6).
Historically "an annual not to exceed amount of $80,000 - $100,000" with "The average task order amount ... around
$8,000" (p.6). The RFP is explicit that this is not major construction: "The majority of the task orders under this
agreement will involve smaller, less complex projects, and will likely not require project managers, superintendents,
foremen, construction trailers, engineering, etc." (p.6). Our approach is sized to that: one point of contact, one
standing crew, quotes in days, work scheduled around building occupants, no layers of overhead.

### 3.8 Change order approval and monitoring

RFP p.3: "Outline an approval and monitoring process that the Respondent plans to follow related to potential change
orders."

1. Every task order starts from a written quote with a stated scope, hours, materials and not-to-exceed amount.
2. If a condition is found that is outside the scope (hidden damage, rot, code item, City-requested addition), the crew
   stops that portion of the work, photographs it, and Luke calls the City Representative the same day.
3. Luke sends a written change request within one business day: what was found, options, added hours and materials at
   Fee Schedule rates, and schedule effect.
4. No change-order work starts without the City Representative's written approval (email is sufficient). Unapproved work
   is at Brown Bison's cost.
5. Each invoice shows the original quote, approved change orders by number and amount, and the total, so the City's
   task-order record stays reconcilable.
6. Luke tracks hours against each quote daily; if a task will exceed 80 percent of its approved amount, the City
   Representative is told before, not after.

**Training.** [LUKE/SUB: list only what is held — OSHA 10/30-hour cards by name; first aid/CPR; aerial lift; EPA RRP;
