## E. Ambiguities (decide before submitting)

1. **Gen AI disclosure.** The form asks whether the Response "does / does not contain content derived from a Generative
   Artificial Intelligence model". The narrative was drafted with AI assistance, so the DRAFT form is pre-checked "does"
   and lists the narrative sections. Falsification "could subject this Response to disqualification". Luke confirms.
2. **Fee schedule structure.** Option A (drafted): each hourly rate is the fully burdened rate the City pays Brown Bison
   for that labor class, whoever performs it (Ceres cost + Luke's markup baked in); the "Subcontractor markup" line then
   applies only to specialty subs quoted per task order. Option B: list Ceres's raw rates and rely on the subcontractor
   markup line. Option A matches Littleton and Thornton. The Subcontractor list form will still show Ceres at
   "[LUKE] %" of the contract; the City may ask how the two fit together. Luke decides.
3. **Trip charge.** Ceres quoted a trip charge. RFP p.7: "The City will not pay trip charges or service call fees under
   this master price agreement." It has to be folded into the hourly rate or dropped.
4. **"Standard work hours" are not defined** in the RFP (p.7: "plan and schedule work during standard work hours"). The
   draft defines regular hours as Monday-Friday 7:00 am-5:00 pm, City-requested work outside that at the after-hours
   rate. Luke confirms the window with Ceres.
5. **Professional liability $1M at award** (Q&A). Confirm with Will that it is placeable for a GC/handyman operation and
   what it costs; price it into the markup now ("fully burdened to account for things like bonds and insurance").
6. **Sub insurance.** Sample MPA §6(b) p.4: "Professional shall cause any subconsultant or subcontractor of the
   Professional to procure and maintain the minimum insurance coverages listed herein." Ceres would need WC, GL
   $1M/$1M, Auto $1M, and the same $1M professional-liability line. Ask Ceres for a COI now; if they cannot meet it,
   Brown Bison's own policies have to cover the work.
7. **Addendum 1 says "2 attachments".** We hold the Q&A. Confirm on BidNet whether the second attachment is the Fee
   Schedule, the Required Solicitation Documents, or something new.
8. **Procurement contact name.** Cover sheet says Joe Hostetler; pipeline/README say Kelsey Archuleta. Address any
   question to procurement@brightonco.gov with the subject format the General Terms require.
9. **Disciplines.** The draft proposes carpentry, drywall/finish, painting, flooring, doors/windows/cabinets, demolition
   and general repair, and excludes asbestos, mold, masonry, fencing and water backfill. If Ceres or another sub covers
   masonry or fencing, add them before Monday.
10. **Years in business / prior names / bankruptcy-litigation questions** on the Qualification Statement are Luke's to
    answer; the draft leaves "[LUKE]" and does not assume "No".
