## Pre-bid checklist for the attendee (send with Ex. A on GO)

1. Sign in as "[name], [firm], for Brown Bison Holdings LLC". Photograph the sign-in sheet if CPW allows.
2. Each of the 6 existing units: nameplate photo (make, model, energy source, rating, voltage), the pipe connection, location.
3. Conduit system: pipe size and material at each heater, flow, the water temperature CPW must hold and how tightly, existing
   controls.
4. Changeover: may all 6 units be off at once, or one at a time; is temporary heat needed; how long may a unit be off.
5. Electrical: photos of the main and sub-panel (schedule, main breaker, service size, voltage and phase, spare spaces);
   distance from the panel to each heater and to the garage; wet-area routing.
6. Where CPW wants the GFCI protection and the emergency cutoff switches.
7. Garage: size, ceiling height, insulation, existing heat, heater and thermostat location.
8. Removed units: disposal by us, or does CPW keep them.
9. Access: work hours and days, parking and staging, any biosecurity rules in the aquatic areas.
10. Who CPW expects to obtain the electrical permit and inspection.
Write down every answer CPW gives; the important ones are confirmed in writing through Q1-Q13. After the meeting, no contact
with CPW staff: questions go to the buyer by email (p.3).
