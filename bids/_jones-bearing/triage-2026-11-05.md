## 4. How Brown Bison quotes a SAM.gov product buy as dealer, Jones Bearing as supplier

Everything below is taken from the notices read today unless marked **not verified**.

**4.1 Who quotes.** Brown Bison Holdings LLC (CAGE 20VU2, UEI EV9QPAPM4C47) is the offeror on every quote. The USCG notice:
"Companies must have valid DUNS numbers and be registered with SYSTEMS FOR AWARD MANAGEMENT (SAM) and shall provide the company Tax
Information Number (TIN) with their offer." (DUNS is retired; UEI replaces it. Whether USCG still wants a DUNS line is **not verified**.)
Jones Bearing needs no registration to be the supplier on a Brown Bison quote. It needs SAM + CAGE only to quote in its own name or to
register on DIBBS (section 5 and 6).

**4.2 What the quote contains (USCG 70Z08026Q-AK017 as the template).**
1. Unit and extended price for 60 EA, F.O.B. destination Baltimore, with the delivery date (no later than 3/10/27).
2. A line that packaging will be per MIL-DTL-197M Method 33B Level B and marking per MIL-STD-129R with Code 39 bar codes, priced in.
   The notice says quotes that cannot meet this "WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED".
3. "Offeror(s) must include a completed copy of FAR 52.212-3 with their quote" (Reps and Certs, Alternate I). Brown Bison's SAM reps
   cover most of it; the Buy American paragraph needs the manufacturer and country of origin of the SKF part.
4. The HSAR 3009.104-70 inverted-domestic-corporation disclosure (check the first box).
5. TIN, CAGE, UEI, SAM status, quote validity period, Brown Bison POC. Send by email to the named contracting officer.

**4.3 DLA quote forms.** DLA Maritime's SF1449 instructions (SPMYM227Q1115) spell out the dealer fields: "Page 1 BLK 17a. Provide
Contractor's info and cage code. Section A. A1 – 1. Enter The Contractor's POC. Section B. – Enter your Quote ... Section B. K21 –
Check the appropriate box and provide the Manufacturer's Name, Address and the Country of Manufacturing. Section F. – Provide the Lead
Time and/or Delivery Date. Section K. - 52.204-24 (d)(1) and (2) Check the applicable boxes. Section K. - 52.209-11 (b)(1) and (2) ...
Page 1 - Read box 28 and sign boxes 30 a, b, and c." Automated DLA RFQs (the pulleys in section 2) are quoted on the DIBBS web quote form
instead; the Utah APEX DIBBS deck (Jan 2024) says "MUST BE LOGGED IN TO BID" and the quote form has no save, so have price, lead time,
manufacturer CAGE and part number ready before opening it.

**4.4 Dealer traceability (what replaces a "letter of supply").** None of the 83 notices uses the term "letter of supply" (that is a
GSA Schedule concept; **not verified** as applying to open-market SAM buys). What the notices do require of a non-manufacturer:
- DLA: "DLAD Proc Note C03 Contractor Retention of Supply Chain Traceability Documentation (JUN 2023)": "By submitting a quotation or
  offer, the contractor is confirming it currently has, or will obtain before delivery, and shall retain supply chain traceability
  documentation ... demonstrating the item conforms to the technical requirements, and, for part numbered items, is from an approved
  manufacturer." It must show "part number; drawing or specification; national stock number; manufacturing source; manufacturing
  source's Commercial and Government Entity (CAGE) code; and clear identification of the name and location of all supply chain
  intermediaries between the manufacturer to the contractor to Government acceptance", available "within three business days upon the
  contracting officer's request". So: Jones Bearing's purchase invoice from SKF (or SKF's distributor) plus Brown Bison's invoice from
  Jones Bearing, kept on file.
- NAVSUP (authorized-distributor buys, which we are NOT pursuing): "proof of being an authorized distributor shall be provided on
  company letterhead signed by a responsible company official and sent with your offer".
- USCG sheave (brand-name buy): "Offerors must be able to provide necessary certification including traceability to the manufacturer,
  manufacturer's COC and its own certificate of conformance." Expect the same for the SKF bearing even though that notice does not say it.

**4.5 Nonmanufacturer rule.** DLA Maritime's clause list checks "[X] (30) 52.219-33, Nonmanufacturer Rule". On a small-business set-aside a
dealer must normally supply the product of a small-business manufacturer; SKF is not small. Whether the rule applies to a buy under the
simplified acquisition threshold (13 CFR 121.406(d)) is the question for the USCG bearing; **not verified here**, ask the contracting
officer in writing before pricing.

**4.6 Buy American / trade agreements.** USCG cites "FAR 52.225.1 Buy American Act-Supplies" and "FAR 52.225-3, Buy American

## 5. DLA DIBBS path (CAGE 20VU2 today; Jones Bearing later)

Sources: DLA's "Doing Business with the Defense Logistics Agency: DIBBS" pocket guide (DLA Small Business, Jamie Miles; fetched via an
archive copy because dla.mil returns 403 to this session), the Utah APEX Accelerator "DLA DIBBS, January 2024" deck, DLA's Vendor
Information Hub article "DLA Internet Bid Board System (DIBBS) Vendor Registration" (search summary only; page not fetchable from here,
so its wording is **not verified verbatim**), and DIBBS FAQ #45 as recorded in `docs/bidwatch.md` on 2026-10-20.

1. **Prerequisite.** Pocket guide: "To Register - must have prior SAM registration ... yearly update required" and "Registration is
   required for - submitting electronic quotes on RFQs and to register email addresses for solicitation and award notifications NOT
   to search". DLA article (summary): "A Vendor will need to be registered on SAM.gov and receive their company Commercial and
   Government Entity (CAGE) code to proceed any further in the registration process." Brown Bison qualifies now; Jones Bearing does
   not until section 6 is done.
2. **Steps** (pocket guide screens + DLA article summary): DIBBS home page > Vendor Registration > "Enter your CAGE" > "Data pulled
   from SAM" > "Super User - enter name, phone number and email" > "Your Temporary PIN # will be sent here" / "Enter the temporary ID
   sent to your Email" > "Read carefully and understand agreement ... Check 'I accept terms box'" > set password (15+ characters, four
   character classes, valid 60 days) and three challenge questions > quote defaults ("Days Quote Valid for – standard 90 days",
   "Discount terms – standard net 30 days") > award/solicitation notification POCs ("ONLY ONE Primary and Alternate POC") > "Vendor
   Directed Solicitation Notification" by FSC, NSN or approved-manufacturer CAGE ("Can enter up to 3000 values per category") >
   Submit. The DLA article adds a "Physical mailing address verification (post card mailed to address entered in SAM)" step.
3. **Super User.** "Shows your DIBBS Super User ID – always your CAGE CODE followed by 01" (Brown Bison: 20VU201). One Super User per
   CAGE; the Super User "creates other User accounts, edits accounts or deletes user accounts". DLA's July 2026 Super User Terms say
   DLA must approve sub-user accounts before they are added (search summary; **not verified**).
4. **Quoting.** Utah deck: "MUST BE LOGGED IN TO BID"; open the RFQ package, check NSN/part number, AMSC code, drawings (drawings live
   in cFolders, which "requires separate password from DIBBS home page"; restricted drawings need a DD Form 2345 JCP certification);
   fill the quote form in one sitting ("Saving is NOT an option"), review, Submit. Pocket guide: "At least 85% of our solicitations are
   AUTOMATED via DIBBS ... identified by having a T in the 9th position of the solicitation number", evaluated and awarded by the
   system. Two of the three MAYBE pulleys are T-buys.
5. **Set up FSC notifications** for 3110, 3130, 3020, 3910, 3920 under the Super User profile so DLA emails new RFQs instead of us
   scraping.
6. **The rule we obey: no cloud, no proxy.** DIBBS FAQ #45 (verified verbatim 2026-10-20): "the DIBBS and cFolders User Agreements

   "...usage/access to DIBBS or interfere with DLA's ability to identify, authenticate, and geolocate users. This includes, but is not
   limited to, the use of Virtual Private Networks (VPNs), other proxies, and hosting services (for example, AWS, Google..." (snippet
   cut off). Registration, searching, drawings and every quote happen on Luke's own computer and home/office internet. The cloud
   scanner never touches DIBBS; the local runner with `BIDWATCH_ALLOW_DIBBS=1` is the only automated reader.

## 6. SAM.gov registration packet for Jones Bearing Co.

Source for the steps: GSA "Quick Start Guide for Contract Registrations" (official GSA document, read today). Notarized-letter status:
conflicting public sources, see 6.4.

**6.1 Before starting.** The person doing it needs a personal Login.gov account with two-factor sign-in ("If you do not already have a
Login.gov account, please create an account"). Suggest Luke does the keying with dad on the phone; dad's company officer signs anything that
needs signing.

**6.2 What dad must supply** (GSA guide, Core Data > Assertions > Reps & Certs > POCs):
- Legal business name and physical address as they appear on IRS records (the registration validates the UEI against the legal name and
  address, then takes "Business Information (TIN, etc.)" and an "IRS Consent to Disclosure of Tax Information" signed with the MPIN).
  Directory listing says Jones Bearing Company, 1062A Highway 72, Pelham, AL 35124; **confirm with dad**. No PO box.
- EIN (TIN). Entity type and state of incorporation ("Enter General Information (business types, organization structure, etc.)").
- "U.S. bank Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Information for federal government payment purposes": routing number, account number,
  account type, and the name on the account.
- Executive Compensation and Proceedings answers (usually "no" for an 11-person company).
- NAICS and PSC codes: 332991 ball and roller bearing mfg; 333922 conveyor and conveying equipment mfg; 333613 mechanical power
  transmission equipment mfg; 423840 industrial supplies wholesalers. PSC 3110, 3130, 3020, 3910, 3920. "Enter your entity's size
  metrics" (employee count, 3-year average receipts).
- Points of contact: "Electronic Business POC" and "Government Business POC" (name, email, phone); plus whoever is the Entity
  Administrator (the SAM user who owns the record). Decide whether that is dad, someone at Jones Bearing, or Luke.
- CAGE: "If you do not have a CAGE Code for the UEI you are registering, do not worry; we will assign one after your registration is
  submitted." Nothing for dad to do.

**6.3 Timeline.** GSA: "If you have all the necessary information ready, this should take approximately 45 minutes to complete" and
"Allow up to 12-15 business days after you submit before your registration is active in SAM.gov." Then DIBBS registration (postcard to
the SAM address, PIN by email) adds days. Plan on Jones Bearing being able to quote in its own name about four weeks after dad sends the
items. Renewal every 365 days.

**6.4 Notarized Entity Administrator letter: status unclear, plan for it.** GSA's Federal Register notice of 2025-01-27 ("Notarized
Document Submittal for System for Award Management--New Entity Administrator Appointments") says GSA "will only employ this method to
establish an Entity Administrator to an existing entity ... where the previous administrator is no longer on staff", refining the earlier
rule that collected the letter "for all new entities". Several 2025-2026 third-party guides still say new registrations are not
activated until FSD receives the notarized letter. Treat it as "may be required"; if SAM asks, the FSD template letter goes on company
letterhead, signed by an officer, naming the administrator, and is uploaded to an FSD.gov ticket (FSD 866-606-8220). **Not verified against
FSD.gov directly** (fsd.gov did not render from this session).
