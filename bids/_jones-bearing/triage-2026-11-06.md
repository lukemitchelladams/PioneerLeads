## 1. Army MICC Fort Drum W911S227U0132, "Bearings" (SAM id 21f8910c3dc44615956327770976b6c3) = NO for Jones Bearing

### 1.1 What the Army wants (quoted from the notice)
"Required Product Characteristics, brand-name-or-equal as follows: The contractor shall furnish new, unused, commercially available steel
mill products meeting the material specification/grade, product form, nominal dimensions, length, and quantity stated for each Contract
Line-Item Number (CLIN)." Six CLINs:

| CLIN | Item (notice wording) | Reference part (notice wording) | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0001 | "Thin-section bearing" | "Kaydon Bearings JG180XP0" | "6 each" |
| 0002 | "Right-hand female rod end", "Self-lubricating PTFE/Teflon-lined spherical bearing surface" | "QA1 Precision CFR8T ... same as CTFD-8" | "3 each" |
| 0003 | "Rod end" | "QA1 Precision CMR8T ... same as CTMD-8" | "3 each" |
| 0004 | "Retaining ring", "[STZD RET RING]" | "Rotor Clip SE-98" | "6 each" |
| 0005 | "Roller chain", "Single-strand", "Nominal chain series/size: No. 60", "100-foot rolls, with one compatible master/connecting link included with each roll" | "Diamond Chain DMD-60-1R" | "600 feet" |
| 0006 | "Stainless-steel swivel joint", "Nominal connection size: 1-1/2 inch NPT" | "OPW 3750-0151" | "6 each" |

No NSNs are given. Bearing type: CLIN 0001 is a Kaydon thin-section bearing (Kaydon's JG prefix is a Reali-Slim thin-section series and the
XP0 suffix a four-point-contact open bearing, **from Kaydon's catalog naming, not from the notice**; the 180 reads as an 18 in bore). Nothing
on the list is a mounted spherical roller bearing, a take-up or a tapered-roller pillow block.

### 1.2 Terms the notice does state

  with each salient characteristic." "Failure to provide sufficient information to establish compliance with all salient characteristics may
  render the quotation ineligible for award."

  manufacture when required."
- "used, refurbished, remanufactured, surplus, gray-market, or counterfeit items are not acceptable unless expressly authorized in writing".
- Set-aside (SAM record): "Small Business Set Aside - Total". NAICS 333613, PSC 3020. Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation, posted 2026-11-06.

### 1.5 Verdict: NO for Jones Bearing; at most a thin Brown Bison-only dealer play
- Jones Bearing, American Pulley and ConveyMor make none of the six items. The one bearing line is a Kaydon thin-section bearing, a
  different product class from Jones's mounted spherical roller bearings, take-ups and tapered pillow blocks. Rod ends, retaining rings,
  roller chain and swivel joints are catalog MRO parts. The profile matched on the title word "Bearings" and PSC 3020, not on fit.
- As a dealer buy for Brown Bison alone: brand name or equal, total small business, no approved-source, QPL or first-article language, so
  it is quotable in principle. Against it: a one-off six-line MRO order (6 bearings, 6 rod ends, 6 rings, 600 ft of chain, 6 swivels) with
  five distributor quotes to collect in under a week, a certificate of conformance per CLIN, the open MTR question, and the
  nonmanufacturer-rule question on a total-SB set-aside (11-05 triage, section 4.5, still not verified). CLAUDE.md rule 9 (small-margin
  product supply is not worth Brown Bison's time unless volume is proven) applies. Default NO; Luke can overrule.
