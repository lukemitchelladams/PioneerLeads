### What the document itself tells us about competitiveness
- Lump-sum PM + hourly emergency rate means the **PM lump sum is the headline number**. Unbalanced bids (very low lump sum, very high hourly) are explicitly rejectable.
- 100% performance and payment bonds on a maintenance contract add real cost. If the Town waives bonds, the bond cost comes off the price. Bid both ways on the form if it allows.
- Estes Park is 1.5+ hours from the Denver metro. Travel is a cost driver on 19 sites. A Front Range contractor needs a local or Loveland/Fort Collins-based tech plan to be competitive against Estes/Loveland shops.
- Town Business License is required for every sub too. Budget it.
- The Town is tax exempt. Do not carry sales tax on materials in the price.
