Section 1 Introduction and Background 
 
1.1 Introduction 
 
This RFP is to solicit proposals from Vendors capable of satisfying the needs of Metro Water 
Recovery (Metro) as stated herein. In addition to soliciting written responses, this document 
provides information to assist Vendors in preparing their responses and facilitates the evaluation 
process.  
 
1.2 Summary of Project Objective 
 
Metro is seeking qualified and experienced firms capable of providing  preventive maintenance, 
inspection, testing, and servicing of critical electrical distribution equipment at the Robert W. Hite 
Treatment Facility and the South Thornton Lift Station. The expected outcome is reliable, properly 
maintained electrical equipment supported by consistent testing and maintenance documentation 
throughout the 2027–2031 contract term. 
 
1.3 About Metro Water Recovery 
 
Metro is the wastewater treatment authority for most of Metropolitan Denver and parts of Northern 
Colorado. Metro is a Metropolitan sewage disposal district formed in 1961 and is a political 
subdivision of the State of Colorado.  
 
Metro is committed to protecting the environment and providing for the community’s future needs.  
 
• Metro operates and maintains two treatment plants – the Robert W. Hite Treatment Facility 
(RWHTF) and the Northern Treatment Plant (NTP) – the METROGRO Farm, and its 251-
mile Interceptor System.  
• Metro serves more than 2 million people within the 817-square mile service area. 
• Metro Water Recovery treats on average 130 million gallons of wastewater per day from 
neighborhood sewer lines and removes more than 95% of the pollutants before discharging 
it into the South Platte River. 
 
Metro Water Recovery’s mission is  to protect the region’s health and environment by cleaning 
water and recovering resources. 
 
Visit at www.MetroWaterRecovery.com

6.4 Proposed Team Resources 
 
SECTION 4 of the proposal shall describe the organizational structure and personnel proposed to 
support Metro’s Switchgear and Protective Relay Maintenance program. The proposal shall identify 
the roles, responsibilities, qualifications, certifications, and relevant experience of all key personnel 
who will perform, supervise, manage, or support the services. 
At a minimum, the proposed team shall identify, as applicable: 
• Program/Project Manager responsible for overall coordination, scheduling, communication, 
reporting, and contract performance; 
• Field Supervisor or Lead Technician responsible for directing and overseeing field 
activities; 
• Qualified electrical technicians and testing personnel who will perform inspections, 
preventive maintenance, testing, troubleshooting, and related services; 
• Safety personnel or other individuals responsible for compliance with applicable electrical 
safety requirements and Metro site-specific safety procedures; 
• Engineering or technical support resources available to evaluate identified deficiencies, 
equipment condition, test results, and other technical issues; and 
• Subcontractors or other third -party resources proposed to perform any portion of the 
services. 
Resumes or qualifications shall be provided for key personnel proposed for primary roles and shall 
identify experience relevant to the inspection, testing, preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and 
servicing of electrical distribution systems and associated equipment. 
 
The proposal shall identify all applicable professional licenses, technical certifications, safety 
training, manufacturer-specific training, and other qualifications held by proposed personnel. As 
applicable to the services being performed, these qualifica tions should include NETA Certified 
Electrical Testing Technician credentials, Qualified Electrical Equipment Maintenance Worker 
(QEMW) qualifications, or equivalent industry -recognized credentials. NETA certification 
establishes competency levels for elec trical testing technicians based on examinations, training, 
continuing education, and experience requirements.  
 
Personnel performing electrical maintenance, testing, or other work involving electrical hazards 
shall have documented training and qualifications appropriate to the work being performed. 
Proposers shall identify personnel with applicable electrical safety  training, including NFPA 70E 
training where required for the assigned work, and shall describe how required qualifications and

For specialized testing or maintenance activities, the proposal shall identify the certification or 
qualification level of the personnel proposed to perform the work. Metro may require

A clear distinction shall be made between Vendor personnel, subcontractor or third-party personnel,

Metro reserves the right to require background checks for individuals performing services on
