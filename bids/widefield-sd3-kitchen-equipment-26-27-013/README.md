## Scope summary
"Comprehensive diagnostic, repair, emergency response, and preventive maintenance services for commercial kitchen equipment"
(T&C 2). Included per the scope section: "ovens, ranges, and warmers, refrigerators, freezers, milk coolers, holding cabinets
and specialty preparation equipment". Excluded: "Steamers, dishwashers, and sanitation equipment". Emergency refrigeration:
"mandatory technician response time within two (2) hours". Standard work orders start "within two (2) business days". PM every
12 to 48 months. Personnel follow District background-check practices (T&C 10).
