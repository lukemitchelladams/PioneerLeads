**d. "Proposed approach, methodology, and understanding of the District's needs."**
1. Request intake: Nutritional Services or Facilities emails or calls dispatch; Brown Bison logs the work order with school,
   equipment, symptom, and whether it is refrigeration-emergency or standard.
2. Response: refrigeration emergencies (refrigerators, freezers, milk coolers) dispatched for a technician on site "within
   two (2) hours of the initial request"; standard work orders started "within two (2) business days".
3. Site protocol (p.7 item 4.2): technician checks in with the designated Nutritional Services or Facilities representative
   "prior to commencing any work onsite and immediately upon completion of the service call".
4. Diagnosis and NTE control (p.7 item 4.1): root-cause finding reported same day; any work, labor or parts "expected to exceed
   $1,000.00" stopped at diagnosis and quoted in writing to the Nutritional Services Director or Facilities Department for
   "prior written authorization" before proceeding.
5. Repair with "Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) or equivalent parts" and operational test before return to service.
6. PM program: inventory of included equipment per kitchen at contract start; schedule by manufacturer interval (12 to 48
   months), during school breaks where possible; each PM includes "Inspection ... Lubrication ... Cleaning ... Operational
   Testing ... Reporting & Recommendations" (p.6 item 3.3), delivered as a written report per unit.
7. Invoicing (p.7 items 4.3-4.5): one invoice per work order, itemized with work performed and diagnostic findings, labor
   hours by rate, and the complete parts list.
8. Data privacy (p.3 T&C 10): no student data is received or handled under this service; if the District classifies Brown
   Bison as a "School Service Contract Provider", we comply with the Student Data Transparency and Security Act.
9. Sustainability (p.3 T&C 9): refrigerant recovery per EPA 608; recyclable packaging; Green Seal cleaning products where
   applicable.
