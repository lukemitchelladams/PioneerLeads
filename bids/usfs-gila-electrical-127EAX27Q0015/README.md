## Scope summary (quoted, Attachment A SOW)
- *"furnishing and installing a complete, code-compliant electrical distribution system to support a designated concrete
  pad area at the tanker base/fire cache, and the upgrade of an existing electrical/motor control panel serving the
  retardant pumping system"* (SOW intro). *"suitable for continuous outdoor service in a high-use industrial environment"*.
- Panel / motor control: *"Provide a single, clearly identifiable disconnecting means"* for the connected pump motor;
  *"Circuit breaker-based disconnecting means (in place of or equivalent to existing fused disconnect, where
  appropriate)"*; *"Motor controller (contactor or equivalent) for operation of the pump"*; *"Motor overload protection in
  accordance with NEC Article 430"*; *"Replace existing panel with a complete, functional, new system"*. Panel photos in
  the SOW appendix.
- Concrete pad: *"Two (2) duplex receptacles total"*, GFCI (*"NEC 210.8"*); *"lockable, weatherproof disconnect switch
  within sight of pad"*; *"one (1) spare empty conduit stubbed to pad"*; *"Maximum 3% voltage drop at each receptacle"*;
  service *"routed from the new service pole, which shall be installed as part of the receptacle/stub installation"*;
  routed distance *"approximately 30–50 feet"*.
- Conduit: above ground *"EMT or Rigid Metal Conduit (RMC)"*; pull strings in all conduits; *"Minimize trenching where
  feasible"*; restore disturbed surfaces.
- Design: *"Perform NEC-compliant load calculations (Article 220)"*; SOW 5.7 *"Contractor Design Responsibility"*.
- Codes: NFPA 70 (NEC), NFPA 70E, NEC Article 250 grounding, Forest Service engineering standards as applicable.
- Trenching is short (30-50 ft) and incidental to the electrical work; this is not heavy civil (rule 8).
