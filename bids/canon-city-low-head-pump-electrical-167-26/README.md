## Rule 8 check (no pump-station civil work)
The title is electrical only, and the CEO's 11/18 note allows electrical alone. Until the documents are read, rule 8 is open:
if the scope includes excavation, trenching beyond conduit runs, concrete pads or structures, or pump/motor mechanical work,
that part is outside our trades and the bid goes NO-BID unless the City accepts an electrical-only bid.

Context only (third-party aggregator summaries of an earlier City bid, not this solicitation): the City bid the removal and
replacement of "a vertical turbine pump and 100 hp motor at the Low Head Pump Station" (Bid 65-26, due 2026-06-30), described
as a raw-water pump station. Do not quote this as a 167-26 requirement.
