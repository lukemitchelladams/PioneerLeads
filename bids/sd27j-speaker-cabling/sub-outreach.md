## Scope the sub prices (RFP Scope of Proposal §§1-12)
- New **18/2 shielded speaker cable**, one continuous home run per classroom from the existing Bogen CSD 1X2 speaker to the
  nearest MDF/IDF on Exhibit A drawings; no splices.
- Remove/abandon/modify the existing daisy-chain wiring as the District directs.
- Furnish dedicated supports (J-hooks, slings, straps, tray); never tied to ceiling grid wires, pipes, ducts or conduit.
- Fire-stop and sleeve every penetration; keep rated assemblies intact.
- Label both ends with the room number; test and verify; leave termination slack at the MDF/IDF.
- **Not included:** connection to the Bogen PA head end (District or its PA contractor does that).
- Work on **Mondays** (no classes), phased by building wing; clean up at the end of each Monday.
- Pricing template has three lines: Labor, Materials, Total.
