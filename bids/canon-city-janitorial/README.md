- "The Contractor shall provide all labor, tools, equipment (including industrial vacuum and buffer) and transportation" (sec. I) and "all cleaning supplies, chemicals, tools, equipment, and materials ... All associated costs shall be included in the monthly janitorial service pricing" (sec. G).
- Hours: "normally work between the hours of 7:00 PM and 6:00 AM, or at times agreed to that does not interfere with City staff" (sec. H).
- City check-off sheets signed and dated by Contractor staff; "periodic face-to-face meetings with the Contractor's staff actually performing the services" (p.19).
- Background checks on every assigned employee, "at the Contractor's expense and shall be reimbursable by Contractor to the City" (sec. VI).
- Extra paid work on request: carpet cleaning, strip/scrub and refinish VCT, machine-clean tile (sec. D). "Special or emergency janitorial services on an hourly reimbursement basis. A separate line item quotation shall be provided" (sec. F): construction clean-up, body fluids, flooding.
- End of contract: 30-day pre-termination walk-through; uncorrected deficiencies "$30 per day ... not to exceed a total of $900" as liquidated damages (sec. E).
- Optional consumables (sec. C, p.17): multifold towels 69 cases/yr, single-fold 9, roll towels 14, standard toilet tissue 23, jumbo 2, large liners 30, medium 20, small 2, foam soap 21, lotion soap 18, air freshener refills 57 each, diaper disposal refills 2. "Estimated annual quantities are provided for bid comparison purposes only and do not guarantee minimum purchase quantities." Supplies may be awarded with the janitorial contract, never separately.

## Risks / open questions
- **PSA IV.D: "Because the City has hired Consultant for its professional expertise, Consultant agrees not to employ subcontractors to perform any of the work required under the Scope of Services."** The same PSA then requires "any subcontractor of Consultant" to carry insurance (VII.B) and indemnifies for subcontractor acts (VIII), and the RFP evaluates "experience with the responder or any subcontractors" (GI 8). Ask on BidNet (by 2026-12-03 14:00) whether a janitorial subcontractor is allowed under the service agreement. Until answered, treat the sub model as at risk.
- PSA VII.B.3 professional liability $600,000/$1,000,000: ask whether it applies to a janitorial contract.
- Fremont County 5% local preference (up to $10,000) favors Cañon City firms; a local sub does not make Brown Bison a Fremont County bidder.
- No square footage, restroom counts or current spend in the RFP; get them at the 11/30 walk or by BidNet question.
