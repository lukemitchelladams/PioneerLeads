## Scope summary
Full outsourced custodial for the whole district: "all supervision, labor, equipment, tools, cleaning chemicals,
consumable supplies ... quality control systems, and administrative support" (RFP §1.2). Exhibit D lists 146 rows
across 90 site codes: 7,547,412 sq ft total, 21 day-porter lines, 36 mobile-unit lines. Largest: Cherokee Trail HS
391,000 sq ft; Smoky Hill HS 373,536; Grandview HS 368,500; Eaglecrest HS 353,334; Overland HS 331,530.
Contractor determines staffing; "Failure to maintain adequate staffing shall not excuse nonperformance" (Exhibit B §5).

## Why NO (quoted from the document)
- Exhibit A §2 Additional Minimum Qualifications: "Minimum seven (7) years providing managed custodial services";
  "Minimum five (5) years serving K-12 educational clients"; "Experience supporting at least three (3) school
  districts exceeding 3,000,000 square feet"; "Preferred experience supporting at least one (1) educational
  portfolio exceeding 5,000,000 square feet"; "Demonstrated APPA Level 2 compliance history"; "Existing Colorado
  presence and client base". (The RFP adds "Failure to meet every listed qualification will not automatically
  disqualify a proposer", but every qualification is missing.)
- §5.2: "at least three (3) references of similar clients ... of similar size and scope to this requirement."
- Exhibit A §5: award weighs "industry leadership, innovative practices, risk mitigation, workforce stability".
- MSA §15(d): professional liability "$3,000,000 per each occurrence/$3,000,000 + aggregate on a claims-made
  basis" plus E&O $1M/$2M; CGL with sexual-molestation endorsement; MSA §8.6 10% retention; §2.6.2 SLA credits.
- A firm that can staff 7.5M sq ft (ABM, Aramark, HES, SSC) primes this itself; none would sub to us.

## Key requirements (for the record)
- Performance standard APPA Level 2 at building opening every school day (Exhibit B §2); morning inspection
  score ≥ 90% monthly average, no facility below 85% (Exhibit C "Performance Enforcement").
- SLA: biohazard/major spill acknowledge 15 min, respond 30 min, resolve 2 h; safety hazard resolve 1 h (Exhibit B §7).
- District provides equipment at elementary schools and all paper products; contractor provides liners, chemicals,
  soap, and all equipment at other sites (Exhibit B §6).
- Background checks on every employee at contractor expense (MSA §2.9); subcontractors only with CCSD written consent (MSA §2.7).
- Proposal must be valid 365 days (§2.4); MSA signed "with no redlines" or redlines scored against you (§3.0).
