SPECIFICATIONS/SCOPE OF WORK  
 
 
 
I. SPECIFICATIONS/SCOPE OF WORK 
 
This work is to address the streetlights repairs or replacements on Front Range Parkway at Colorado Air 
and Space Port. 
 
PHASE #1 
 • Perform (3) directional bores to establish new underground conduit pathways between pole locations, 
minimizing surface disruption across the facility  
• Pull new #6 THWN-2 copper conductors through existing underground conduit in all (13) viable sections, 
ensuring conductor fill does not exceed 40% per NEC 2026 Chapter 9 Table 1  
• Install a listed ground rod with listed clamps at each existing caisson per NEC 2026 Section 250.52, and 
run #6 solid bare copper bonding conductor to establish a continuous equipment grounding path throughout 
the lighting circuit  
• Install (14) new commercial-grade LED luminaire heads, selected for wet location listing and rated for 
exterior pole-top mounting per NEC 2026 Section 225.25  
• Install new vertical branch circuit wiring from grade level to each new LED fixture using listed weatherproof 
fittings and conduit, in accordance with NEC 2026 Section 300.5 and 225.10 clearance requirements 
 
PHASE #2 
• Excavate and install (2) new concrete caissons for new pole bases, designed to support specified pole 
loading and meet local wind/frost depth requirements  
• Set and plumb (2) new poles supplied by the owner; contractor to coordinate delivery staging, set poles 
using telehandler or boom equipment, and level to true vertical.

19.12. Miscellaneous: Security Breaches and Personal Information. 
 
 
 
If Contractor obtains personal identifying information, as that term is defined in C.R.S. 24-73-
101, from the County during the course of this Agreement, Contractor shall destroy or properly 
dispose of the information in a manner that is compliant with C.R.S. 24-73-101 when that 
information is no longer needed for the performance of this Agreement. Contractor shall also 
implement and maintain reasonable security procedures and practices that are appropriate to 
the nature of the personal identifying information obtained; and reasonably designed to help 
protect the personal identifying information from unauthorized access, use, modification, 
disclosure, or destruction. 
In the event of a security breach, as defined in C.R.S. 24-73-103, that compromises computerized 
data that includes personal information subject to this Agreement, Contractor shall notify the County 
of the security breach in the most expedient time and without unreasonable delay following discovery 
of the security breach, if misuse of personal information about a Colorado resident occurred or is 
likely to occur; and cooperate with the County, including sharing with the County any information 
relevant to the security breach, except that such cooperation does not require the disclosure of 
confidential business information or trade secrets.
