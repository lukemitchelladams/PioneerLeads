## A. Access and security

- §12.2 (p.4) is all the IFB says: "Contractor shall take all necessary precautions with going through secure facility, i.e. tools, camera, masks, etc." It is silent on ID, badge, escort, PPE, and whether photos are allowed.
- Bring (our list, not the IFB's): photo ID, a mask, notebook, a printed aerial map of Front Range Parkway to mark poles on, tape or laser measure, charged phone, boots, hi-vis vest. Leave tools in the vehicle until told. At check-in, before you shoot or measure, ask: Photos and video allowed, and where? Tape or laser OK? Escort needed? What is off limits? If photos are barred: notes, sketch, paced distances, marked map, and send question 1 in writing.
- §32.3 (p.11): "Any communications in regards to this Solicitation must go through the Adams County Purchasing Division only. Any contact with other County personnel or County Contractors may be cause for disqualification." Ask in the open meeting. No side talks with airport staff or County contractors.
- §15 (p.4): "The County shall not be responsible for interpretations offered by employees of the County who are not agents of the County's Purchasing Division." So walk answers from airport or other County staff are not safe to price on. Note each answer and who gave it, then get it in writing (section C).

## B. Photo and measurement checklist (IFB Scope of Work, p.12)

One wide photo with a landmark, then close-ups with a tape for scale. Name photos by ID (P07-head, S04-handhole): poles P01-P14 from the power source (IFB: 14 luminaires, so confirm the count), new sites N1-N2, bores B1-B3, sections S01-S13. Do not open panels or handholes. Ask staff.

**Quick numbers:** poles ___ | caissons ___ | sections ___ total, ___ viable | bores B1 ___ B2 ___ B3 ___ ft | panel ___ V, ___ phase, ___ A

**Phase 1**
- [ ] **"(3) directional bores ... between pole locations"** (B1-B3). Photo both ends and each crossing. Note: pole pair, length (ft), surfaces, crossings (road, driveway, ditch, fence), rig and pit room, water source, locate paint.
- [ ] **"(13) viable sections" of "existing underground conduit"** (S01-S13). Photo pole bases, handholes, stub-ups, conduit stamps. Note: pole-to-pole ft, conduit size and type, condition (water, dirt, crushed), which look NOT viable.
- [ ] **"ground rod ... at each existing caisson"**. Photo each caisson, base plate, anchor bolts, ground lug. Note: caisson count, surface around it, whether a rod could be driven, route for the bare copper bond.
- [ ] **"(14) new commercial-grade LED luminaire heads ... exterior pole-top mounting"**. Photo each pole whole, head, head label (zoom), tenon or arm, pole tag. Note: pole height (ft), shape and material, existing head make and model, watts and volts, mount size, photocell, lift access (soft ground, slope, overhead lines).
- [ ] **"new vertical branch circuit wiring from grade level to each new LED fixture"**. Photo base cover, handhole (staff opens it), conduit entries. Note: handhole size, wire inside or outside the pole, fuse at the base, grade-to-head ft.

**Phase 2**
- [ ] **"(2) new concrete caissons"** (N1, N2). Photo a wide view with the nearest pole, road edge and fence, then the ground. Note: ft to nearest pole, surface, slope, soil (rock, clay, sand, wet), obstacles above and below, nearest power.
- [ ] **"(2) new poles supplied by the owner ... delivery staging ... telehandler or boom"**. Photo the poles if on site (tags, base plate), the haul route, the lay-down area. Note: length, weight, base plate and bolt pattern, gate and route widths, soft ground, overhead lines.

**Everywhere**
- [ ] **Surfaces and obstacles:** asphalt, concrete, gravel, turf, dirt; fences and gates (width), ditches, culverts, signs, trees, overhead lines.
- [ ] **Utility locates:** photo paint and flags. Ask who calls 811 and whether the airport has maps of its private lines.
- [ ] **Power source and panel:** wide shot, door label, nameplate (volts, phase, amps), street light breaker, contactor, photocell, meter. Sketch the circuit through P01-P14.

## C. Questions: ask at the walk AND send in writing by Tue 2026-10-27, 5:00 pm MT

Send to jlachermeier@adcogov.org (cover p.1; §26.1.1 p.7: use "the Point of Contact listed on the first page") and post in BidNet (§14 p.4). Two other domains appear for Joe: @adamscountyco.org in §14 and @adamscountyco.gov on the No-Bid form (p.16). §14: "The Contractor submitting the question(s) shall be responsible for ensuring the question(s) is received by the County". Confirm receipt.

1. **Photos.** May bidders photograph and measure on site? If not, will all bidders get as-builts and photos?
2. **Bores.** For each of the 3 bores: pole pair, length (ft), depth, conduit size, type, count? Any road, driveway or taxiway crossing?
3. **Sections and conduit.** p.12 says "all (13) viable sections". How many exist? Which are not viable, and are the 3 bores for them? Who decides? Existing conduit size, type, condition? As-builts, pull boxes, pull strings? Who pays if a pull fails?
4. **Soil.** Geotechnical report? Frost depth and design wind speed for the caissons (p.12: "local wind/frost depth requirements")? Sample Agreement §1.3 (p.19) puts subsurface conditions on the Contractor.
5. **Luminaires and poles.** p.12 gives only "commercial-grade", "wet location listing", "exterior pole-top mounting". Make, model, watts, lumens, color temperature, distribution, voltage, controls? Approved equals, and how submitted? Existing pole height, type, tenon, condition? Any pole or base repair in scope?
6. **New poles.** Spec of the 2 owner-supplied poles (height, weight, base plate, bolts)? Who supplies anchor bolts and rebar? Engineered caisson drawing? Where stored, when delivered? p.12 lists only caissons and setting: is wiring, a ground rod or a luminaire for them in scope?
7. **Outages.** Can the circuit be shut off? When, how long, how much notice? Temporary lighting?
8. **Escorts and badging.** Escort every work day? Badges, background checks or fingerprints for crews and subs? Cost and lead time? Work hours, vehicle permits, restricted areas, boom or telehandler height limits?
9. **Schedule.** Start and completion date or days? Sample Agreement §3.1 (p.20) leaves the term blank ("XXXXX"). Bid Instructions §5.5 (p.2): "Liquidated Damages will not apply." Sample Agreement §5.3 (p.21) has a daily damages table. Which applies?
10. **Missing documents.** Cover p.1 lists a "Prevailing Wage Vendor Form". §8 (p.3): "Wage Determinations for this construction project are attached as Appendix." Sample Agreement §1.8 (p.20) cites Exhibits B and C. None is in our PDF. Please post them.
11. **Subs.** Sample Agreement §8.1 (pp.22-23) needs written approval before subcontracting. When and how do we submit subs? May the Reference Form (p.18) show our subcontractor's similar projects?
12. **Attendance.** §12.1 says "recommended"; the heading and cover say mandatory. Confirm attendance is required and that Brown Bison Holdings LLC is recorded.
