Proposal Schedule lines (p.9), each an annual price: 128 Main St boilers incl. snow melt (annual); 128 Main St McQuay chiller (quarterly); 128 Main St CRAC unit (annual); 161 Justice Center Rd boilers (annual); 161 Justice Center Rd Carrier chiller (quarterly); 612 Royal Gorge Blvd boilers (annual); TOTAL ANNUAL BASE PROPOSAL.

- Chillers: "Quarterly inspection of chillers, pumps, and related components. Testing and inspection to determine operating status; preventive maintenance to support proper operation, equipment life, reliability, and energy efficiency." (p.6)
- Boilers: "Annual cleaning and inspection of boilers, pumps, and related components. Testing, inspection, and preventive maintenance, including applicable cleaning and adjustments." (p.6)
- CRAC: "Annual cleaning and inspection of CRAC unit indoor and outdoor coils." (p.6)
- "The proposal price shall include jobsite and travel labor, equipment inspection and testing, preventive maintenance labor, and routine maintenance materials such as oil, solvents, cleaning materials, and lubricants." (Proposal Requirements 2). Long "as applicable" inspection and predictive/preventive task lists on pp.6-7 (vibration, combustion air ratios, refrigerant charge, calibration, lubrication, tube cleaning, etc.): "apply only where appropriate to the installed equipment".
- "Responders shall identify any proposed exceptions, excluded repairs, additional charges, or optional services separately." (PR 4) and "shall provide qualifications and relevant experience servicing the specified equipment." (PR 5)
- Special/emergency service "on an hourly reimbursement basis. A separate line item quotation shall be provided" (sec. E).
- Background checks on every assigned employee at the Contractor's expense (sec. VI, p.8). End-of-term walk-through with $30/day deficiency penalty, max $900 (sec. D).

Ask on BidNet (by 2026-12-03 14:00) whether a mechanical subcontractor is allowed. Until answered, the sub model is at risk.

- Snow-melt boiler (CHN501) is on the 128 Main St boiler line; confirm it is one annual PM like the others.
