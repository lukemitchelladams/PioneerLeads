**Q1. Exhibit A, service location list (p.2; pp.6-45).** Page 2 states "The full list of service locations is attached in
Exhibit A." No page in the posted 52-page bid package is labeled Exhibit A. Please confirm whether the maps on Bid Package pages
6 through 45 are Exhibit A, or post the list. Please also confirm: (a) the addresses of Country Park (p.8) and Prairie Winds
Park (p.19), which show "?"; (b) which two of the "20 developed parks" (p.1) are not among the 18 parks mapped, and whether
they are in scope; (c) whether Stanford Hills and Open Space (part 1) is in scope, since only part 2 (p.41) is mapped; (d)
where Trails Skate Park (p.1) is shown and what landscape areas it includes; (e) Cherokee Trail Park (p.7) and Piney Creek
Trail Part 1 (p.32) show the same aerial and boundary: please confirm it is one service area, to be priced once.

**Q2. Quantities and pricing format (p.4; p.5).** Please provide, by location, the acreage or square footage of turf mowing,
shrub beds and native mowing, the linear feet of hardscape to be edged, and the length of each native trail mow strip, or the
GIS or KMZ files behind the maps. Is there a District price form for "Base pricing broken down by location and service
category," or may proposers use their own format? Under Evaluation Criteria (p.5), are there scored criteria or weights for
price, qualifications and references?

**Q3. Growing season and policing (Exh B II.A.1, II.A.5, II.D.2).** For pricing weekly mowing "during the growing season,"
what season start and end dates, or how many mowing visits, should proposers assume? For policing "at least four (4) times per
week during summer," what dates does the District consider summer? Please list the "priority facilities designated by the
District, including the Trails Recreation Center and other designated high-use sites" that are policed daily and whose trash
receptacles are checked daily, and confirm whether daily includes weekends and holidays.

**Q4. Irrigation (Exh B II.C).** Please provide the number of irrigation controllers and their make and model or central
control platform, the number of zones or systems at each location, and the water source (potable or non-potable). Is backflow
preventer testing in the contractor's scope? Which other parties have access to the controllers (II.C.4)?

**Q5. Snow removal (p.2; Exh B III.B and III.C).** For the five priority routes in III.C, please provide the length and width
of the paved surfaces to be cleared at each, and the required clearing standard (full paved width or a set width). After the
District authorizes service at two inches (2") or greater, what response time does the District expect? Are there restrictions
on de-icing products near creeks, wetlands or trail bridges? How many snow events did the District authorize in each of the
last two winters?

**Q6. Chemical and fertilizer program (Exh B I.I, II.A.6, II.A.7, II.E.5).** Does the District have an approved product list,
no-spray areas (playgrounds, water features, wetlands) or notification requirements for herbicide and pesticide applications?
May the "commercial pesticide applicator licensing where applicable" required by I.I be held by a licensed subcontractor
performing the applications? Is there a required fertilizer analysis or slow-release content for the
three turf fertilizations?

**Q7. Sample agreement (p.4; p.5).** The Exceptions and Deviations requirement (p.4) refers to the "sample agreement." No
sample agreement is included in the posted bid package. Please post it, and confirm whether it contains insurance, indemnity,
bonding, payment or termination terms beyond those stated in the RFP. Are any District forms required with the proposal
(signature page, price form, W-9, affidavit or certification) beyond the items listed under Bid Submittal Requirements
(pp.3-4)?

**Q8. Statement of Qualifications and references (p.4).** May a proposer demonstrate the "at least three (3) years of
continuous commercial landscape maintenance operations" through its proposed team, including the subcontractors performing the
landscape work and key personnel, or must the proposing entity itself show three years of operations? May the "Three Client
References" include accounts of the subcontractor that will perform the landscape work, labeled as such?

**Q9. Contract history (p.2; Exh B III).** Please provide the District's annual base contract value for landscape
maintenance and its annual spend on time-and-materials extra services and on snow removal in 2025 and 2026, and the prior
bid tabulation if one is public.

**Q10. Restrooms and trash receptacles (Exh B II.D.2 and II.D.4).** For weekly restroom cleaning at Piney Creek Hollow Park,
Spring Creek Park, Village Park and Tallgrass Park, what months are the restrooms normally open, and how many fixtures does each
building have? How many trash receptacles are within the contracted scope at each location?
