### 1E. Year-round maintenance plan

Planned timing is Brown Bison's (HQ default; the sub confirms). The Exhibit B requirement behind each task is cited.

> **Contract start, January to February.** In the first weeks of the term we inspect every location
> (Exh B I.F: "The Contractor shall inspect the properties at the beginning of the contract term and promptly identify
> material pre-existing conditions") and deliver a photo log of pre-existing conditions, irrigation controllers and tree
> wraps in place. Winter service runs from day one: policing at least twice a week and daily at priority facilities (II.A.5),
> trash receptacle checks (II.D.2), monthly native-area tree inspections (II.E.3), snow removal on District authorization
> (Part 3), and the weekly check-in. We prepare the 2027 seasonal maintenance calendar with the Parks Manager (p.3, "Seasonal
> Planning").
>
> **Early spring, March to April.** One spring cleanup at every location (II.A.8); tree and shrub pruning, first round
> (II.B.2); tree wraps removed (II.B.2); crabgrass pre-emergent where agronomically appropriate (II.A.6); irrigation activation
> scheduled with the District, with a written list of repairs needed to place each system in operating condition (II.C.1); first
> aeration (II.A.4) and first fertilization (II.A.7).
>
> **Growing season, May to September.** Weekly mowing with trimming (II.A.1-2); edging twice a month (II.A.3); policing at least
> four times a week in summer (II.A.5); broadleaf weed control and bed weeding (II.A.6, II.B.1); second fertilization; mid-summer
> pruning (II.B.2); irrigation checks, arc adjustments and controller programming for season, events and watering restrictions
> (II.C.2); native mowing on six cycles and the wall-to-wall pass at Spring Creek and Piney Creek Hollow (II.E.1); disc golf
> fairway paths and basket beds at Tall Grass Park (II.B.3); weekly restroom cleaning while open (II.D.4); crack weed spot
> treatment (II.E.5); service adjusted ahead of special events and large sporting events (II.D.3).
>
> **Fall, October to November.** Second aeration; third fertilization; one fall cleanup (II.A.9); irrigation winterization at
> every system completed before October 31 (II.C.3); fall tree wrapping (II.B.2); policing returns to at least twice a week.
>
> **December.** Winter service and snow standby; year-end condition report and the renewal-year plan and budget projection for
> the District (p.3, "Seasonal Planning").
>
> **Weather.** When weather makes work unsafe or impractical we reschedule and tell the Parks Manager the same day
> (Exh B I.J); missed recurring services are made up at the next workable window.
