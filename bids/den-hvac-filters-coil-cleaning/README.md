## Scope summary
Filter replacement and coil cleaning on DEN's HVAC fleet: terminal, concourses A/B/C, AOB, hotel/transit center and
outlying buildings ("outlying and other airport support buildings", Exh A 1(b)). DEN supplies the filters; the contractor
supplies labor, supervision, vehicles, equipment, cleaning materials, inventory handling, disposal and documentation.
Appendix II workload: **5,774 filter changes and 1,699 coil cleanings a year** on about 1,684 units (Exh A p.14-15 of 16).
The Pricing Form uses 1,795 units (6,170 filter services, 1,795 coil cleanings). Appendix III lists 1,288 work orders with
about 8,403 filter pieces per full service (2-inch: 6,656; 6-inch: 1,299). Counts and the Pricing Form layout are in
QUANTITIES.md. HVAC service is in our trades (CLAUDE.md rule 8). Rule 9 (supply bids) does not apply: this is a service
contract, and Appendix II states quantities. Brown Bison proposes and holds the contract; an HVAC service sub does the
field work (sub-outreach.md).

### Scope (Attachment 1, Part 4, Exhibit A)
- "The Contractor shall provide all certified and experienced labor, equipment, materials (not including filters) and
  expertise necessary to successfully fulfill this scope of work" (Exh A 1(c), p.2).
- "Replace filters and clean coils at intervals determined by the PM ... Tasks will be assigned via monthly work orders"
  (Exh A 3(1)(a), p.3). Also: "Manage inventory for air filters and maintain cleanliness of mechanical rooms"; "Provide
  on-site supervision, coordination, and safety oversight"; LOTO and access control; "Identify any deficiencies ... and
  immediately notify the PM" (3(1)(b)-(f)).
- Unit estimates: "40 – Ventilation Smoke Control Fans; 150 – Air Curtains; 405 – Air Handling Units (AHUs); 125 – MAU /
  DOAS Units; 95 – Rooftop Units (RTUs); 30 – Computer Room Air Conditioning (CRAC) Units; 200 – Air Conditioning Units
  (ACUs); 430 – Fan Coil Units (FCUs); 225 – Fan-Powered VAVs (FP VAVs); 150 – Passenger Loading Bridge PCA Units*"
  ("*not anticipated at this time but may be added later") (Exh A 3(2), p.3-4).
- Filters: contractor stores filter inventory at DEN, stages filters in mechanical rooms, "shall work collaboratively with
  DEN's provider of air filters", and "is responsible for disposing of used filters" (Exh A 3(3)(a),(b),(d),(f), p.4).
- Vehicles "at the time of contract execution": "a vehicle capable of transporting filters from WorldPort" and "an
  electric vehicle, such as a golf cart, to drive through the airport tunnel system" (Exh A 3(3)(c),(e), p.4).
- Unlisted work: "For any service or unit type not listed on the price sheet, the contractor will provide a written quote"
  (Exh A 3(4), p.4).
- Task list per filter change: LOTO via DEN HVAC team; replace filters; date the filter; before/after photos; close panels;
  dispose in PM-identified containers; "HEPA-vacuum filter bank, frames, and immediate plenum area"; "All Annual and
  Semi-Annual filter change work orders require coil cleaning inspection ... Coil cleaning shall be completed at the
  discretion of the PM. The Contractor shall provide all labor and materials necessary to clean coils"; "ensuring zero air
  bypass in accordance with NAFA guidelines"; document in work tracking software; clean floor drains and mechanical rooms
  (Appendix I items 1-11, Exh A p.13).
- Documentation per unit in "an approved online work tracking software/program": filter sizes and quantities, MERV, labor
  hours, dates, location, asset number, pre/post photos, "magnehelic gauge readings, where gauges are present" (Exh A 4(1),
  p.4). Monthly reports (4(5)); monthly equipment inventory list (4(4)); status meetings (4(6)).
- "Contractor's work must not interfere with airport operations and may require off-hours or phased work." (Exh A 4(9), p.5)

  preventative maintenance services" (SC p.1). Whether PM beyond Appendix I is included: asked (Q20).

return for the prior two (2) years and prepare a Core Staff Labor Rates Sheet" (III-15.G.3).
