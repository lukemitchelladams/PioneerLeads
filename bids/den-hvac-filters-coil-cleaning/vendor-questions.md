# DEN RFP DEN-2026-020-10021: written questions for BidNet

Deadline: **2026-11-23 14:00 MST** on BidNet ("Last Date to Submit Written Questions November 23, 2026, by 2:00 PM MST",
RFP p.2). "The City requires all questions to be submitted individually on this site" and may "respond to only the first
question posed in that group" (RFP p.2-3). So: one block below = one BidNet question box. Paste only the text under
"Paste:". Answers come back as an addendum after 11/23; acknowledge every addendum on Attachment 1, Part 1.

## Tier 1: changes the unit price

**Q1. Which unit counts govern**
Source: Exh A Sec 3(2) (p.3-4 of 16), Appendix II (p.14-15 of 16), Pricing Form D9:D19, Appendix III. See QUANTITIES.md section 1.
Paste:
> Exhibit A Section 3, Appendix II and the Pricing Form list different unit counts (for example RTUs 95 / 91 / 120, CRAC units 30 / 27 / 50, FP-VAVs 225 / 225 / 250, tunnel FCUs 15 / 20). Please confirm that the "# of Units" and "Service/Year" already entered on the Pricing Form are the quantities used for evaluation, and that proposers should not change them.

**Q2. Complete asset list**
Source: Appendix III (sheet "completed work orders", 1,288 rows); RFP p.3 ("more than 1700 HVAC assets").
Paste:
> Appendix III lists 1,288 work orders, while the RFP describes more than 1,700 HVAC assets, and 198 Appendix III rows have no filter type. Will DEN post a complete asset list with filter sizes and quantities for every unit to be serviced?

**Q3. Coil cleaning table formulas**
Source: Pricing Form, cells G28:G37 and C41.
Paste:
> On the Pricing Form coil cleaning table, only cell G28 has a formula; G29 through G37 are blank, and the Year 1 Total (C41) sums G28:G37. Should proposers enter the formula (# of Units x Service/Year x Cost) in G29:G37, or will DEN issue a corrected Pricing Form?

**Q4. Firm pricing vs. 5% a year**
Source: Exh A Sec 13(4) (p.12 of 16); Pricing Form notes ("Total increase for years 2-5 must not exceed 5% per year").
Paste:
> Exhibit A Section 13(4) says pricing shall be firm for the term of the Contract, while the Pricing Form allows Year 2-5 increases of up to 5% per year. Which governs? Should proposers fill in the Year 2-5 Total cells with escalated amounts?

**Q5. Who supplies filters**
Source: Exh A Sec 1(c) (p.2 of 16) "materials (not including filters)"; Sec 3(3)(d) "DEN's provider of air filters"; Pricing Form note ("equipment and materials" not allowed).
Paste:
> Please confirm that DEN furnishes all replacement filters, including 6-inch final filters and any bag or specialty filters, and that the contractor only receives, transports, stages, installs and disposes of them and does not purchase filters.

**Q6. Coil cleaning scope and method**
Source: Appendix I item 8 (p.13 of 16); Exh A Sec 11(6) (chemical approval).
Paste:
> For pricing coil cleaning, does it cover only the coils inside the air handler (cooling and heating coils), or also condenser coils on RTUs, ACUs and CRAC condensers? Does DEN require a specific method (foaming chemical, low-pressure water, steam), and will water and drain access be available at each unit?

**Q7. Expected coil cleaning volume**
Source: Appendix I item 8 ("Coil cleaning shall be completed at the discretion of the PM"); Appendix II (1,699); Pricing Form (1,795).
Paste:
> Appendix I item 8 says coil cleaning is done at the PM's discretion after inspection. Should proposers staff for all 1,699 coil cleanings per year listed in Appendix II (1,795 on the Pricing Form), or does DEN expect a smaller number to be ordered each year?

**Q8. Prevailing wage classification**
Source: Sample Contract Sec 6.C (contract p.8); Attachment 6 (2026 Building General Wage Decision CO20260020); Pricing Form note "All rates must include Prevailing Wage Rates where required."
Paste:
> Which Denver prevailing wage classification will DEN and the Auditor apply to filter replacement and coil cleaning labor (for example Sheet Metal Worker, Pipefitter, Laborer: Common or General, or Building Engineer from Attachment 6)? Or does only the City Minimum Wage apply to this work?

**Q9. Badging time, fees and escorts**
Source: Exh A Sec 1(k) (p.2 of 16), Sec 9(1)(a) (p.8 of 16: "all required security escorting, access coordination, and badging"), Sec 12(2) (p.11 of 16).
Paste:
> How long does DEN badging typically take from application to badge issue, and what are the badge and fingerprinting fees per person? While badges are in process, may badged contractor staff escort unbadged contractor staff?

**Q10. Airside driving and the $10,000,000 auto limit**
Source: Exh A Sec 1(j) (p.2 of 16: "Airfield and LAR Driving privileges"); Attachment 4 C.2.a (Exh C p.1-2 of 6).
Paste:
> Exhibit A Section 1(j) requires Airfield and LAR driving privileges. Will any assigned work require unescorted airside driving, which triggers the $10,000,000 auto liability limit in Attachment 4? If so, may the contractor use DEN's Airside Unescorted Excess Auto Liability Program?

**Q11. Builder's Risk and Professional Liability**
Source: Attachment 4 C.5 and C.6 (Exh C p.2-3 of 6). The exhibit is DEN's "Construction Agreement (non-ROCIP)" form.
Paste:
> Attachment 4 is the construction insurance form and includes Builder's Risk / Installation Floater and Professional Liability of $1,000,000. For this filter replacement and coil cleaning service contract, are Builder's Risk and Professional Liability required?

**Q12. Weekend and holiday work**
Source: Exh A Sec 1(l) (p.2 of 16: Mon-Thu day and night crews), Sec 10(1) (p.10 of 16: work "at any time per direction of the Project Manager").
Paste:
> Exhibit A sets Monday-Thursday day and night crews but allows work at any time at the PM's direction. Will weekend or holiday shifts be required? If so, should that cost be built into the unit prices or billed from the hourly rate table?

**Q13. Washable filters**
Source: Appendix III (68 units list washable, metal or mesh filters).
Paste:
> About 68 units in Appendix III have washable, metal or mesh filters. Is cleaning a washable filter billed as one filter change on the Pricing Form, and does DEN require a specific cleaning method or location?

**Q14. Tunnel FCUs**
Source: Appendix II (p.14 of 16: about 15 tunnel FCUs, bi-monthly filters, semi-annual coil cleaning); Pricing Form rows 17 and 36 (20 units, coil cleaning 1 per year).
Paste:
> Appendix II lists about 15 baggage-tunnel FCUs with bi-monthly filter changes and semi-annual coil cleaning, but the Pricing Form shows 20 tunnel units with one coil cleaning per year. Which is correct, and which Appendix III rows are the tunnel units?
