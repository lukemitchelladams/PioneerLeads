## 4. Narrative contents the RFP requires (quoted, IV-3, p.21-24)

1. **Cost Effectiveness & Pricing**: "How will you help DEN meet the project budget without compromising quality?"; "Please
   provide a project specific pricing sheet for the evaluation of the cost-effectiveness of your proposal"; "Explain how you
   calculated your cost per unit/service"; "Please fill out and submit Exhibit B - Price Sheet".
2. **DEN EDI Plan**: "The Proposer shall describe what they have done to engage with historically underutilized businesses in
   their ongoing operations." Plan "should include, but is not limited to": A Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion Strategies; B
   Technical Assistance & Support Services; C Procurement Process; D Communication and Proposer Management; E Past
   Performance; F Proposer's Culture; G Future Initiatives ("a roadmap of the work Proposer intends to do over the next five
   (5) years").
3. **Understanding the Project**: "Understanding of the Scope of Work"; "Complexity, challenges, and problems involved in
   planning and performing scope of work".
4. **Proposed Work Plan and Approach**: "Describe the Proposer's project management and organizational approach, including:
   Proposed effort for completing the work on schedule"; "Describe the Proposer's existing project management control methods
   and progress reporting systems."
5. **Key Personnel and Ability to Respond**: "Provide an organizational chart which identifies the Proposer's key personnel
   and applicable subcontractors who would perform work under the Contract"; "Describe the qualifications of each
   subcontractor planned to perform work"; "Describe the type of work which will be assigned to each subcontractor".
6. **Company Experience and Qualifications**: "Include information on previous projects where these services have been
   provided"; "At a minimum, the following information shall be included as appropriate to the Scope of Work: Project name,
   Project description, Contract Value, Scope of Work, Location, Owner name, address, current contact person, and telephone
   number, List any subconsultants and percentage of work performed, Gross fees, Outcome/result".

Cover letter (p.21): "The letter shall include the full name of the company or joint venture members, and all proposed
subconsultants. ... The cover letter must include a statement committing the availability of the key personnel, identified
in Section 5 below, to perform the work for the duration of the contract term. The letter must be signed by a person who is
authorized to sign a contract with the City. This signatory shall be the same person identified in the Proposal
Acknowledgement Letter as the authorized representative."

Evaluation (p.26, V-5): Cost Effectiveness & Pricing 25%; DEN EDI Plan 15%; Understanding the Project 15%; Proposed Work Plan
and Approach 20%; Key Personnel and Ability to Respond 10%; Company Experience and Qualifications 15%. Also V-1: "The
Proposer's ability to present its proposal in writing in a clear, concise, and organized manner will be considered."
