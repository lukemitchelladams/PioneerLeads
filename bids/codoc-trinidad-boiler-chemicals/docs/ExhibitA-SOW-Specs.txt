              COLORADO DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS (CDOC)
       DQ CAAA 2027000037 – Boiler Chemicals & Treatment Services at TCF

1.   STATEMENT OF WORK: This Statement of Work (SOW) describes the services and
     deliverables sought through this DQ and the scope of what the awarded Bidder(s) will be expected
     to offer through the Purchase Order resulting from this DQ. The SOW is intended to provide
     interested Bidder(s) with sufficient basic information to submit a Quote. It is not intended to limit a
     quote’s content or exclude any relevant or essential data. CDOC is seeking Quotes from qualified
     bidder(s) that can provide Boiler Chemicals and Treatment services on site at Trinidad Correctional
     Facility.
          Boiler Water Treatment services to include: Maintain the appropriate water chemical levels for
          optimal performance of steam boiler system, condenser, condensate, and chiller water of
          closed system. Implement a treatment program which protects boilers from water scales and
          corrosion to prevent any malfunction or system issues. Monthly testing of water chemical
          composition, adding or adjusting water concentration levels, inspection of boiler functions and
          controls.
          Consult with facility staff on operational practices, provide technical guidance and support of
          chemical handling, and make recommendations for system care.
          The awarded Bidder(s) will be expected to provide all necessary chemicals, materials,
          equipment, testing supplies, and tools to perform treatment services on site.
          Monthly schedule of services: Services must be prescheduled, one day per month, based on
          Trinidad Correctional Facility Physical Plant staff agreed upon date and time. Additional calls
          for testing and treatment may be requested prior to the regular monthly schedule.
          Urgent service calls: Must be able and prepared to respond within 24 hours for urgent or
          emergency service calls. It is critical for the daily operations of the facility that the Boiler
          System be fully operational at all times.
          Please be advised that all bidder(s) interested in submitting a quote for these services must attend
          the Mandatory Site Visit scheduled for 10:00am Wednesday, October 21, 2026. See section 4
          of the Document Quote for additional instructions and information.
2.   SPECIFICATIONS OF BOILERS:
          Trinidad Correctional Facility currently has two (2) Cleaver Brooks model #CBW700-150 in
          the program’s unit and T Building, built in 1998.
          Steam capacity is 5,277,000 BTU’s providing heat to 38,900 square feet.
          There are two (2) additional Cleaver Brooks model #CBW700-100 in the admin building, built
          in 1998.
          Steam capacity is 4,200,000 BTU’s providing heat to 37,590 square feet.
          The system is non-continuous with blowdowns and condensate, hydronic closed loop.
          Natural gas is the heat source for the water tubes.

        needed in the future.
