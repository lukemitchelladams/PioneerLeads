The City of Thornton, Colorado, (“Thornton”) respectfully requests separate sealed Proposals 
for Landscape Construction Services , Project No. 324-26 (hereinafter referred to as 
“Project”).  Proposals will be received until  3:00 p.m., local time, October 28, 2026  in the 
Contract Administration Office, 9500 Civic Center Drive, 2nd Floor City Hall, Thornton, CO 
80229-4326. 
 
Approved Methods for Submission of Proposals:  Electronic proposals shall be

Project Description : Thornton is seeking Proposals from Service Provider s to provide  
Landscape Construction Services  on an on -call, as-needed basis under an open-ended 
General Service Agreement.  Thornton reserves the right to award to multiple Service 
Providers.  Nothing herein guarantees any minimum amount of Service to any Service Provider.  
Thornton requires one or more firms to perform projects such as streets, traffic signals, curb and 
gutter, sidewalk, water line installation or repair, sewer line installation or repair, irrigation ditch,

Termination Notice.  Rate adjustments may be re-negotiated on an annual basis upon request.

Consumer Price Index as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
