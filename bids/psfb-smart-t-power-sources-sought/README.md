## Scope summary
PWS "Bridge Redundant Power from Generator Supply to Panel RP15 in Building 1470", dated 19 October 2026 (POCs Mr. Timothy Ahlers 554-6721, Mr. Jeff Simon 554-4992):
- "run dedicated electrical feeds approximately 100 feet from the PDU in Room 126 to Electrical Panel RP15 located in building 1470 hallway" delivering "Two (2) x 20-Amp (A) circuits and One (1) x 30-Amp (A) circuit" (PWS 1.1).
- Primary power from the existing generator in Building 1471; secondary from the red PDU / automatic transfer switch in Building 1470 Room 126 (PWS 1.1, 3.5).
- "The PDU's Automatic transfer switch ... will, however, need to be verified for capacity to support the additional (2) 20A and (1) 30A requirements" (PWS 3.1).
- Final cutover "must be completed within a single, scheduled outage window" (PWS 3.1); commissioning test with a simulated utility outage and automatic transfer to generator (PWS 3.4, 3.6, 3.7).
- Standards: OSHA, UFC 3-520-01, NEC / NFPA 70 and 70E, Peterson SFB Design Guide (PWS 1.2); labels per TIA-606-C (PWS 3.2).
- Outages: 14 days' notice (7 business days for the terminal) through Customer Service 719-556-4330 (PWS 3.3, 4.1). Hot work: 7 days' notice, one-time Fire Department training (PWS 5). AF Form 3952 hazmat request 10 business days before work (PWS 6.3).
