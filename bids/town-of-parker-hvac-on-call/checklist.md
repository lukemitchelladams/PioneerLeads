# Submission Checklist — Town of Parker HVAC On-Call

## Before bidding
- [ ] Registered as a vendor on BidNet / RMEPS (free for Colorado agencies' basic access)
- [ ] Solicitation + every addendum downloaded and acknowledged
- [ ] Pre-bid meeting / site visit attended (if mandatory, this is disqualifying if missed)
- [ ] Questions submitted before the Q&A deadline
- [ ] Vendor pricing in hand (York / JCI chiller parts & support — Rich)

## Document package (confirm against the solicitation's checklist)
- [ ] Signed bid form / pricing schedule (hourly rates, OT, holiday, parts markup, trip charge)
- [ ] Addenda acknowledgment
- [ ] References (usually 3, similar municipal or commercial on-call work)
- [ ] Certificate of insurance meeting stated limits; Town named additional insured
- [ ] W-9
- [ ] Licenses: Colorado SOS good standing; Town of Parker / Douglas County mechanical contractor license; EPA 608 techs
- [ ] Bid bond, if required
- [ ] Illegal alien affidavit / E-Verify (Colorado public contracts, CRS 8-17.5-102)
- [ ] Non-collusion affidavit
- [ ] Response-time commitment matching or beating what the scope requires

## Submission
- [ ] Submitted via portal (electronic) or sealed hard copy, per solicitation — check which
- [ ] Submitted before the due time (portal time, not our clock)
- [ ] Confirmation saved to `docs/`

## After
- [ ] Request bid tabulation from the buyer on the cover sheet after award
- [ ] Update `memory/MEMORY.md` with outcome
