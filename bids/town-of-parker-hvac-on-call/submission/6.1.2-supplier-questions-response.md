## 5. Preventive-maintenance approach for rooftop and other units

*Summarize your preventive-maintenance approach for rooftop and other HVAC units, including coils, condensate
systems, mechanical and electrical components, refrigerant circuits, and controls.*

Every applicable task in RFP §5.4 is performed and documented at each cooling-season visit:

- **Coils:** evaporator and condenser coils cleaned with cleaners compatible with the coil, the roof and the
  occupants; drains protected; no overspray.
- **Condensate:** pans, traps, drains, pumps and lines cleared, traps primed, drainage verified with no leaks.
- **Refrigerant circuits:** visual leak check; pressures, superheat and subcooling recorded when conditions permit;
  every addition and leak finding documented (see question 9 and RFP §5.8).
- **Electrical:** disconnects, fuses, contactors, relays and wiring inspected; connections tightened to manufacturer
  requirements; voltage and amperage recorded for motors and compressors and compared with nameplate.
- **Mechanical:** belts, blowers, sheaves, pulleys, bearings and motors checked for wear, vibration, alignment and
  tension; lubricated only where the manufacturer requires.
- **Performance:** supply and return temperatures and temperature differential recorded; abnormal readings
  diagnosed and documented.
- **Economizers and dampers:** operation and positioning verified.
- **Controls:** thermostats, sensors, alarms, safeties and BAS setpoints verified; no change to sequences or
  occupied setpoints without the Town's authorization.
- **Air filters:** ⟪not in the visit prices. When the Town wants filters changed at a visit, we supply them at cost
  plus 15% and install them at no added labor charge⟫. Filter sizes and counts for 42 of the 46 assets are
  already on file from the Town's current filter supplier; the remaining four are confirmed at the first visit and
  recorded in the baseline record.

## 6. Heating-system safety: combustion analysis, gas-leak checks, CO testing

*Explain your heating-system safety inspections, combustion analysis, gas-leak checks, and carbon-monoxide testing.*

At each heating-season visit (RFP §5.5), for every gas-fired asset:

- Gas piping, fittings, valves and regulators checked with an electronic gas-leak detector; inlet and manifold gas
  pressure measured and recorded.
- Burners and pilot assemblies cleaned; ignition, flame sensors, inducer motors, venting and safeties inspected and
  tested.
- Heat exchangers inspected for cracks, corrosion, separation and flame disturbance.
- Combustion analysis with a calibrated analyzer: O₂, CO₂, CO, stack temperature, draft and
  efficiency recorded per unit.
- Carbon-monoxide testing at the appliance and in occupied space near the unit's supply.
- Any condition that may present a carbon-monoxide or combustion-safety risk is reported to the Town by phone
  immediately, the unit is made safe, and it is not left running in an unsafe condition.

## 9. Non-routine repairs: diagnosis, estimates, authorization, parts, downtime

*Describe how you diagnose non-routine repairs, submit written estimates, obtain Town authorization, source parts,
and limit equipment downtime.*

1. Diagnose on site and document readings, photographs and the cause.
2. Send a written estimate (labor hours at the contract rates, parts at the stated markup, lead times, options) to the
   Town's representative. No non-routine work proceeds without written authorization, except action needed to
   protect life, safety or property.
3. Parts: new, genuine or manufacturer-approved unless the Town approves an alternative. ⟪Parts come from Denver-area distributors for each brand; common belts, filters,
   capacitors, contactors and igniters ride on the service trucks⟫.
4. Downtime: temporary measures offered when parts are delayed; lead times and warranties stated in the estimate.
5. Work is closed out only after the unit is back in its proper operating state, alarms and setpoints are confirmed,
   and the Town has been told.
6. Warranty: 12 months on labor; parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, passed through to the Town and
   administered by Brown Bison (RFP §5.12).
