## How the work is managed

- **One point of contact.** Luke Adams schedules every visit with the Town's representative, receives every
  emergency call notice, reviews every report before it is sent, and invoices the Town.
- **Response.** A technician on site within 4 hours of the Town's call, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- **Reports.** Searchable digital reports within five business days after each scheduled visit and one business day
  after an emergency call (RFP §5.10).
- **Quality control and callbacks.** Brown Bison reviews each report for completeness and accuracy; callback work
  caused by our workmanship is corrected at no cost to the Town (RFP §5.11).
- **Warranty.** 12 months on labor; parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, passed through and administered by
  Brown Bison (RFP §5.12).
