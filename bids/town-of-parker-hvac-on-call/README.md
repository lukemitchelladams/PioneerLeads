## What we need from the solicitation (in priority order)

1. **Solicitation PDF + all addenda.** Save to `docs/` in this folder.
2. **Scope of work section.** Look for: "response time", "emergency", "hours", "within X hours",
   "call-back", "after-hours". That answers Rich's response-time question.
3. **Equipment schedule / facility list / attachment.** Look for: "chiller", "Exhibit", "Attachment",
   "Equipment List", "Asset List", tonnage, manufacturer, model, serial, year. Transcribe into
   `equipment-schedule.md`. If the bid has no equipment list, submit a written question through
   the portal before the Q&A deadline asking for make/model/tonnage of chillers by building, or
   request a site visit.
4. **Bid form / pricing schedule.** Hourly rates (regular, OT, holiday), markup on parts, trip charge.
5. **Insurance, bonding, licensing requirements.** Douglas County / Town of Parker mechanical license?
6. **Term and renewals.** Typical: 1 year + up to 4 one-year renewals.
7. **Evaluation method.** RFB = low bid; RFP = scored. Changes how we write it.
