# Town of Parker RFP 26-028: air-filter schedule for the 46 Appendix A assets

Source: Air Force Filter and Supply (AFF), Denver, master filter lists for the Town of Parker: quotes QN107827 to
QN107836 dated 2026-10-19, sent to Rich Carroll (Air Comfort York), forwarded to Brown Bison 2026-10-20. AFF: "master
lists per location for Town of Parker that we have on file from this year". Rich: AFF supplies the Town these
filters on a quarterly basis. Sizes are nominal inches, W x H x depth. M8 = MERV 8 pleated (1", 2" or 4" deep);
M15 = MERV 15 rigid filter with plastic header, which AFF marks ANNUAL. Prices are kept off this public repo.

RFP 26-028 and Appendix A never mention air filters (the word does not appear in either document).
