# CCSD RFP 27-014 District Pool Operations: written questions for BidNet

Deadline: **2026-12-08 14:00 MST**: "Written Inquiry Deadline December 8, 2026 @ 2:00 PM MST"; "Response to Written
Inquiries December 11, 2026 Estimated" (RFP Sec 2.1, p.5).

Method (RFP Sec 2.8, p.7, quoted): "No inquiries will be accepted after the date and time indicated in the Schedule of
Activities and NO phone calls will be accepted." "When making inquiries through BidNet you must ask one question at a
time and may not ask multiple questions in one entry. Asking multiple questions with one entry may result in your answers
being delayed." "The District requires that all inquiries be sent through BidNet". "Suppliers should not contact any
other District office or individual regarding this RFP." Answers "may be published as an amendment on BidNet"; every
amendment is acknowledged in the cover letter.

So: one block below = one BidNet question entry. Paste only the text after "Paste:". Posting is a portal post (Luke, or
HQ if it holds the BidNet login). Post Tier 1 first. Answers land 12/11, after the 12/09 sub pricing date, so subs price
on the stated assumptions and we adjust after the answers.

## Tier 1: changes the weekly price

**Q1. Pools at each school**
Source: Exh A 2 (p.32) lists six facilities; no basin count or volume anywhere.
Paste:
> For each of the six high schools, please list the number of pool basins (for example competition pool and diving well) and the approximate volume of each in gallons.

**Q2. Operating schedules**
Source: Exh A 2 (p.32) "Pool usage, schedules, and operating conditions may vary by location and season"; Exh A 3 (p.33) seven days per week.
Paste:
> Please provide each pool's typical operating days and hours during the school year, summer, and winter and spring breaks.

**Q3. Equipment counts and systems**
Source: Exh A 3.C, 3.D, 3.F (p.34-36); no equipment list is posted.
Paste:
> Please list, for each facility, the number of ADA pool lifts, the number of diving boards, and the sanitizer and pH control system in use (for example liquid chlorine, calcium hypochlorite, CO2 or acid feed, UV).

**Q10. Holiday visits**
Source: Exh A 3 (p.33) "seven days per week, including weekends and holidays when required"; MSA 7.1.3 (p.15) holiday work paid only "if work on such day has been authorized in writing by CCSD prior to work commencement".
Paste:
> Should the weekly price on the Facility Pricing tab include visits on CCSD holidays when a pool is open, or will holiday visits be billed separately under the "Holiday or premium service surcharge" line after written authorization per MSA 7.1.3?

**Q11. Weeks when a pool is closed**
Source: Exh A 10.A (p.41) "Annual pricing shall be based on 52 weeks of service unless otherwise identified"; MSA 7.1.2 (p.14) pro-rata for days not served.
Paste:
> When a pool is closed for a planned shutdown, drain or repair, will the weekly rate stop for those weeks, or should proposers price all 52 weeks for every pool?

**Q12. Deck and locker room cleaning**
Source: Exh A 3.B (p.34) "Removing debris from pool areas"; workbook Cleaning Frequency Schedule row "Pool deck debris removal".
Paste:
> Does "removing debris from pool areas" cover only loose debris on the pool deck, or also deck scrubbing or hosing, bleachers, and locker rooms or showers?

**Q13. Spacing of the three daily visits**
Source: Exh A 3.A (p.33) "three separate onsite water-testing visits per day at each pool".
Paste:
> Is there a required time window or minimum spacing for the three daily water-testing visits at each pool (for example morning, midday and evening)?

**Q14. Chemical deliveries**
Source: Exh A 5 (p.38) "The District will coordinate the ordering and delivery of its provided supplies."
Paste:
> Will the District's chemical deliveries go directly into each pool's storage tanks or chemical room, or is the contractor expected to receive, move or transfer bulk chemicals?

**Q18. Emergency response time**
Source: Exh A 7 (p.40) bidders identify "Proposed response times".
Paste:
> Does the District have a minimum on-site response time for after-hours emergencies such as a chemical feed failure or low chlorine, or should proposers state their own?

## Tier 2: eligibility, insurance and scoring

**Q4. Certificate of insurance timing**
Source: Sec 5.6(3) (p.31) COI "if applicable: This is required if the supplier will be on district property"; MSA 15.0 (p.21) certificate "within fifteen (15) days of the Effective Date".
Paste:
> Section 5.6 lists a Certificate of Insurance with the proposal "if applicable," while MSA Section 15 requires the certificate within fifteen (15) days of the Effective Date. Will the District accept the certificate after award and before work begins, with the proposal confirming the proposer will carry the MSA coverages?

**Q5. Professional liability**
Source: MSA 15.d (p.22) professional liability on a claims-made basis plus E&O; Insurance Requirements sheet (p.2) ties professional liability to licensed professions (accountants, attorneys, engineers ...).
Paste:
> Is the professional liability coverage in MSA Section 15.d required for pool operations and maintenance services, given that the District's Insurance Requirements sheet applies professional liability to licensed professions such as accountants, attorneys and engineers?

**Q6. Sexual molestation endorsement**
Source: MSA 15.a (p.21) "Suppliers with direct contact with students shall provide an endorsement"; Insurance Requirements sheet (p.1).
Paste:
> Will pool service technicians who work on deck or in mechanical rooms while students are in the building be considered to have "direct contact with students" for the sexual abuse and molestation endorsement in MSA 15.a?

**Q7. Subcontractor references and CPO staff**
Source: Sec 5.2 (p.31) three references "of similar size and scope"; Exh A 4 (p.37) CPO; MSA 2.7 (p.12) subcontractors with prior written consent; Sec 5.4 (p.31) sub resources identified.
Paste:
> May a proposer meet the Section 5.2 reference requirement and the Exhibit A Section 4 CPO requirement through its named subcontractor's pool service references and CPO-certified technicians, with the subcontractor identified in the proposal for CCSD's consent under MSA Section 2.7?

**Q8. Term and start date**
Source: MSA 3.1-3.2 (p.13): term runs from the Effective Date until terminated; SOW term set in the SOW. No start date or renewal count in the RFP.
Paste:
> What are the intended service start date, the initial term, and the number of renewal periods for this contract?

**Q9. Evaluation weights**
Source: Sec 4.1 (p.29) "price, past performance, financial stability, and technical capability"; Sec 3.0 (p.8) MSA redlines scored.
Paste:
> Please provide the evaluation criteria and their weights or point values for this RFP.

## Tier 3: site visit and logistics

**Q15. Site visit route**
Source: Sec 2.1 (p.5) "Site visits will begin at Overland High School Pool"; Exh A 11 (p.42) "Site visits shall be coordinated through the District contact".
Paste:
> Will the December 3 site visit tour all six pools that day, or only the Overland High School pool?

**Q16. Insurance for site visit attendees**
Source: Insurance Requirements sheet (p.2) "Vendor/partners are not allowed on site unless proof of insurance is provided".
Paste:
> Do site visit attendees need to present a certificate of insurance to enter the pools on December 3?

**Q17. Background screening method**
Source: MSA 2.9 (p.12-13); Exh A 4 (p.37) "District background screening, identification, building access".
Paste:
> What background screening method does the District require for contractor personnel, for example a CBI name check or a fingerprint-based check?

**Q19. Site visit registration**
Source: Exh A 11 (p.42) "Site visits shall be coordinated through the District contact identified in this RFP"; no RSVP line in the RFP.
Paste:
> Do attendees need to register in advance for the December 3 site visit, and if so, by what date?
