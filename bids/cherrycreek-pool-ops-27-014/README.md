## Scope summary (Exhibit A, quoted)
- **Facilities: six high school pools**: "Eaglecrest High School / Smoky Hill High School / Cherry Creek High School /
  Grandview High School / Cherokee Trail High School / Overland High School"; "Bidders shall provide pricing and
  proposed service frequencies for each facility individually, as well as a combined District-wide total." (Exh A 2,
  p.32). Number of basins per school, volumes and equipment lists: not stated (Q1-Q3).
- **Service days**: "comprehensive swimming pool service seven days per week, including weekends and holidays when required
  by the facility operating schedule" (Exh A 3, p.33). Hours: not stated. "Pool usage, schedules, and operating
  conditions may vary by location and season" (Exh A 2, p.32). No lifeguarding anywhere in the RFP.
- **Water testing**: "three separate onsite water-testing visits per day at each pool", each testing "Free available
  chlorine" and "pH"; "Three readings taken during one visit do not satisfy this requirement." Total/combined chlorine,
  alkalinity, calcium hardness, cyanuric acid, temperature "at the frequencies required by applicable regulations"
  (Exh A 3.A, p.33). That is 18 site visits a day, 126 a week, before cleaning and PM.
- **Cleaning**: vacuuming, brushing, waterline tile, skimming, gutters/overflow channels, skimmer baskets, debris;
  "Cleaning shall be performance-based"; bidder sets the frequency per item on the Cleaning Frequency Schedule, and "The
  proposed frequencies will become part of the contractor's service expectations upon award" (Exh A 3.B p.34, 3.G p.37,
  12.A p.43).
- **Preventative maintenance**: pumps/motors, filters, chemical controllers, feed pumps, tubing, flow meters, heaters,
  surge tanks, backwash, main drains, UV "where installed" (Exh A 3.C, p.34-35); diving boards (3.D, p.35); pump strainers
  (3.E, p.35-36); ADA pool lifts "at the manufacturer's recommended frequency", bidder states frequency (3.F, p.36).
  "Major repairs, replacement parts, or equipment replacement shall require prior written approval" (p.35).
- **Chemicals: the District supplies them.** "The District will provide routine pool treatment chemicals and commonly
  used chemical feed replacement parts and components." (Exh A 5, p.38). Contractor "shall not independently purchase
  chemicals ... without prior written authorization" (p.38). Contractor supplies "routine testing reagents and supplies
  necessary to perform the required water quality testing" (Exh A 10, p.41).
- **Reporting**: daily electronic records per visit, weekly summary per facility, monthly report with ADA lift records
  (Exh A 6, p.38-39).
- **Emergency response time: not stated; we propose it.** Bidders identify "Normal business-hour response procedures",
  "After-hours emergency contact information", "Proposed response times", "Emergency service rates and additional
  charges" (Exh A 7, p.40); "immediately notify the District when an issue is identified that could affect safe pool
  operation or require closure" (p.39).
- **Staffing**: account manager and alternate, staffing levels, weekend/holiday coverage, routes and visit schedules,
  CPO, substitutes (Exh A 4, p.37). CCSD may "approve or reject personnel" (MSA 2.4, p.11).

**Verdict: no disqualifier.** Risks that cost points, not eligibility: (1) three similar references from the last five
years: Brown Bison has no pool work, so the sub's references carry it (Q7); (2) insurance: COI "if applicable" at
submission, and Brown Bison's policy is still pending (Q4); professional liability conflict (Q5); molestation endorsement
(Q6); (3) capacity: 18 testing visits a day, 7 days a week, across six schools; a one-truck pool shop cannot staff it,
so the sub must have routes and substitutes; (4) "no redlines" MSA with 10% retention and 365-day price hold.
