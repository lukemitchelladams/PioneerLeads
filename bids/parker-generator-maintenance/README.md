## Scope summary

Seven standby diesel generators plus their automatic transfer switches at six Town facilities (RFP §3):

| Facility | Generator(s) | Fuel |
|---|---|---|
| Town Hall, 20120 E. Mainstreet | Rolls-Royce LSA 49.3/L10 | 1,515 gal |
| PACE Center, 20000 Pikes Peak Dr | Onan 125 kW | 200 gal |
| Fieldhouse, 18700 Plaza Dr | Onan 400 kW | 800 gal |
| MOTS Operations, 12010 Motsenbocker Rd | Detroit 150 kW and Detroit 250 kW | 196 gal; 249 gal |
| Police Department, 18600 Lincoln Meadows Pkwy | Cummins 750 kW | 1,550 gal |
| Public Works Operations, 9045 Tammy Ln | Kohler 500 kW | 900 gal |

Work (RFP §4): initial electronic inventory within 30 calendar days of notice to proceed; coordinated annual PM
schedule (Police Department quarterly: "three inspections and one annual"); ATS inspection and testing; annual
resistive load-bank test on every generator; oil and coolant lab analysis; 24/7 emergency response with a
guaranteed response time we state; corrective repairs with written estimates; digital service reports.

## Key requirements (quoted, with section)

- "Submit proposals electronically through the Rocky Mountain E-Purchasing System and Bonfire before the date and
  time stated in the official posting." Emailed proposals "may be rejected" (§1.3).
- "Proposal pricing shall remain valid for at least 120 calendar days after the proposal deadline" (§1.3).
- Proposal order: cover letter; executive summary + addenda acknowledgment; technical response incl. the eight
  §5 questions; team/technician qualifications, "manufacturer authorizations, and local service coverage";
  implementation plan; emergency response plan "and guaranteed response times"; sample service report; pricing
  schedules (§7); references, disclosures, exceptions, licenses, insurance evidence, safety (§1.4).
- "Provide 24-hour, seven-day-per-week emergency response, including after-hours, weekends, and holidays. The
  contractor shall state and honor a guaranteed response time, prioritize critical public safety facilities" (§4.9).
- "Provide genuine or manufacturer-approved parts" (§4.9).
- "No additional charge, surcharge, travel fee, fuel fee, environmental fee, or other cost will be paid unless
  specifically identified in the accepted pricing" (§2.2).
- Insurance: certificates and endorsements "meeting the Town's then-current requirements" before work starts
  (§2.4). **Limits are not in the RFP; not in hand yet.**
- "The selected contractor may not assign the agreement or subcontract material responsibilities without prior
  written Town approval" (§8). Brown Bison is the bidder; name the generator sub in the proposal and ask approval.
- §7 Pricing Schedules: "Complete all applicable fields in attached pricing sheets & proposal forms."
  **Those sheets/forms are NOT in hand** (GovTribe only carries the 8-page RFP).
