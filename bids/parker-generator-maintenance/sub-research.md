- **Factory names for the odd units:** Smith Power Products or Stewart & Stevenson for the Rolls-Royce/MTU and Detroit sets; the Cummins branch for Onan/Cummins.

## Notes

- **Why the top 3.** Generator Source: widest named brand list (Onan, Cummins, Kohler, Detroit, MTU), load bank, 24/7 line, Parker named in its service area, and a published email. Power Systems West: the nearest full-service shop and the only one with a Kohler factory name verified on the OEM's own directory, plus "all makes" and load bank in its agreements. Gen-Tech: all-brands claim, 24/7/365 line, load-bank trucks, five branches for bench depth.
- **What the RFP scores (quoted from the document in bids/parker-generator-maintenance/docs).** Experience and Qualifications, 20 points: "factory training or authorization, multi-brand capability, parts access, warranties, and references". Emergency Response Capability, 20 points: "Guaranteed response times, 24/7 staffing, proximity, diagnostic resources, escalation, and prioritization of critical facilities." §1.4 item 4 asks for "manufacturer authorizations, and local service coverage". Proximity and a factory name both score.
- **Factory dealers may bid direct** (flags above: Power Systems West, Cummins, Smith, Stewart & Stevenson, Wagner, Rocky Mountain Generator Supply; DVL is a Generac dealer). RFP §8 says submission certifies the proposal was prepared "without collusion, fraud, or improper coordination with another offeror", and §8 also bars subcontracting "material responsibilities without prior written Town approval". Worth asking each firm up front whether it is also submitting its own proposal.
- **Timing (CLAUDE.md rule 0b).** Pricing answers wanted 7+ days before the 2026-11-06 14:00 MT deadline, which is 2026-10-30. The Town's Q&A deadline is also 2026-10-30 at 10:00.
- **Emails.** Every address above was seen in raw page HTML at the URL given; none was guessed or built from a pattern. Cloudflare-obfuscated addresses were decoded from the page itself. I did not use data-broker sites (Apollo, ZoomInfo and similar). Firms marked "no public email" published none on the pages I could read.
- **Verification method.** I pulled raw HTML through the session proxy and searched it for emails, phones and key terms. Web-search summaries were used only to find leads, never as the source of a fact, except where a row says "search result" or "search summary".
- **Odd details to confirm on first contact.** Generator Source's load-bank page says "Local Colorado Springs experts" (probably template wording; confirm dispatch is from Brighton). Its pages also give different years in business (40+ vs 50+). The RFP lists the Town Hall set only as "Rolls-Royce LSA 49.3/L10" (no engine model), and the Detroit sets with no engine series, so ask each firm whether it can service MTU/Rolls-Royce and older Detroit engines and a Leroy-Somer alternator. RFP §4.6 requires annual resistive load-bank testing on every unit, up to the 750 kW Cummins, so ask about load-bank capacity.
