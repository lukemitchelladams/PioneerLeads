## Scope (quoted, RFP p.1)
"Services include turf care, irrigation system management, plant health care, weed control, debris and litter removal,
snow removal in designated areas, and landscape enhancement using Colorado-native and drought-tolerant materials."

## Required submittals (quoted, RFP p.1)
"Proposals must (1) include the proposer's name, address, telephone number, and primary contact, (2) include proof of
liability insurance, (3) describe the proposer's professional history and company background and identify the key
personnel assigned to the work, and (4) provide a lump-sum cost estimate for each basic service and a schedule showing
when each maintenance operation will occur."
