## Scope summary (quoted)

- Place: *"Pagosa Ranger District, 180 Pagosa Street, Pagosa Springs, Colorado 81147"* (RFQ p.10). SF-1449 block 15 prints
  "180 SECOND PAGOSA ST"; PWS 1.1/1.5 print "180 Pagosa Springs, Colorado". Same office; ambiguity noted below.
- Building (PWS 1.3): *"a single-story building consisting of approximately 5,437 square feet of interior floor space. The
  facility includes two enclosed offices with carpeted flooring, 28 carpeted work cubicles, four collaborative or shared-use
  rooms with carpet or vinyl tile flooring, and two separate restrooms, each containing one toilet and tile flooring.
  Hallways are finished with tile, and the front foyer and main entrance are finished with vinyl tile. Although the
  building is primarily single-story, the contractor may be required to use a ladder to access all exterior windows."*
  Attachment 3 floor plan lists lobby, conference room, training/break room, collaboration room, server/plotter room,
  janitor's closet, and an "addition second floor plan" (draft remodel drawing).
- Hours (PWS 1.6): *"Daily services shall be performed Monday through Friday between the hours of 1700 and 0630. Weekly
  services shall be performed at any time either on Saturday or Sunday. The annual service shall be performed at any
  time either on Saturday or Sunday; however, it shall not be conducted on the same day that weekly services are
  performed."* Federal holidays excluded (PWS 1.6.1, 11 holidays).
- Line items (Attachment 2, per year, same for base and each option year): 0001 Daily Janitorial Services, 260 EA;
  0002 Weekly Janitorial Services, 52 EA; 0003 Annual Cleaning, 1 EA. Unit price x qty = line total; totals roll up to
  "TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE WITH OPTIONS".
- Daily (PWS 3.0 "1. Daily Requirements", p.8): lobby and hallway doormats vacuumed; *"Sweep and mop all uncarpeted
  floors"*; drinking fountains and entrance door handles cleaned and disinfected; restroom floors, toilets, sinks,
  mirrors, fixtures, door handles; *"Maintain continuous supply of toilet paper, paper towels, and soap"*. PWS 3.2 adds
  *"Break room counters will be cleaned daily."*
- Weekly (p.8): vacuum all carpet, carpeted stairs and indoor mats; disinfect high-touch areas; *"Empty all trash cans into
  designated outside receptacles whenever trash is present"*; clean liners/cans.
- Annual (p.8): *"Clean and vacuum all vents and air intakes"*; *"Steam clean all carpeted floors once per year"*; *"Power
  wash building exterior to remove cobwebs and clean exterior window screens"*; *"Deep clean bathroom floors, including
  grout and tile."* Carpet cleaning on weekend days with ~30 days COR notice (PWS 3.3).
- Quarterly task with no line item (PWS 3.2): *"High cleaning surfaces above 70 inches shall be cleaned free of obvious
  dust and cobwebs no less than quarterly."*
- Government furnishes (PWS 6.1): key card/keys, *"Plastics bags, toilet paper, paper towels, toilet seat covers, and hand
  soap refills"*, janitor closet with sink, dumpster, water/power, parking. Contractor furnishes *"ALL supplies,
  equipment (ex. steam cleaner, vacuum cleaner, cleaning supplies, etc.)"* (PWS 6.2). Bio-based products *"to the
  maximum extent practical"* (PWS 1.6.3); product list + SDS to COR within 10 days of award.
- Payment: monthly invoice in IPP, *"Payments shall be made for janitorial services which have been satisfactorily provided
  during the month"* (PWS 5.0). QASP: *"1% deduction of monthly invoice if below 90%"* (95% for restrooms) per
  performance line (Attachment 5, surveillance matrix).
- Corrections *"within 48 hours after notification by the Government"* (PWS 6.0 Inspection and Acceptance).

  local janitorial firm small under NAICS 561720 ($22M) is similarly situated, so a 100%-sub crew should comply. Confirm
  against the actual 52.219-6 Nov 2025 text (or ask the CO; draft in `vendor-questions.md`) before the quote goes in.

## Go / no-go (2026-11-05)

**GO.** Janitorial is one of our trades (CLAUDE.md rule 8). No mandatory walk. LPTA with "neutral" past performance
acceptable, so Brown Bison's thin federal record does not disqualify. One small building, simple scope, firm fixed price,
up to 5.5 years. Pagosa Springs is ~280 miles from Broomfield, so a local sub (Durango 60 mi, Bayfield 45 mi, Pagosa
itself) does the cleaning and serves as alternate Contract Manager; Brown Bison prices, submits, holds the contract,
manages the sub. Risks: (1) the subcontracting-limit text of 52.219-6 Nov 2025 (above); (2) LPTA means a local
owner-operator can underbid a prime-plus-sub stack; our markup must stay thin; (3) submission address not stated.

## Ambiguities (ask the CO by 2026-11-10 10:00 MT, or resolve before pricing)
1. **Where/how to submit the quote.** The RFQ never names an email address or portal for quotes; p.11 only says to submit
   "by the solicitation closing date and time identified in the SF-1449" in PDF/Word/Excel. The subject-line sentence on
   p.17 follows the Questions section. Assume email to stephen.holly@usda.gov with solicitation number in the subject;
   confirm with the CO.
2. **Subcontracting limit.** 52.219-14 absent; 52.219-6 Nov 2025 text not in package (above).
3. **"Parts A, B, and C of the Performance Work Statement"** (p.8): the PWS is numbered 1.0-11.0 with no Parts A/B/C.
   Treat as General Information (1.0-2.0), Technical Requirements (3.0-4.x), Deliverables/Personnel/Security (5.0-11.0).
4. **Weekly definition.** PWS 2.8.2 says *"Weekly. Services performed twice during each 12-month period"* (typo) while
   Attachment 2 prices 52 weekly services per year. Price 52.
5. **Quarterly high dusting** (PWS 3.2) has no CLIN; fold into the weekly or daily unit price.
6. **Address**: "180 Pagosa Street" (RFQ p.10) vs "180 SECOND PAGOSA ST" (SF-1449 block 15) vs "180 Pagosa Springs" (PWS).
7. **Health & welfare line**: $5.92 standard vs $5.42 + paid sick leave under EO 13706 (52.222-62 is listed).
8. **Attachment 7 Experience Questionnaire**: attached but not on the required-submittal list. Submit it.
9. **Daily delivery dates** on SF-1449 CLIN 2002 ("Delivery: 01/01/2029") and 3001 ("01/01/2030") look like typos for
   12/31; the periods of performance are stated correctly.
10. PWS 1.6.5 cites "FAR 52.246-4 ... (see Section I)"; there is no Section I in this commercial-item RFQ and 52.246-4 is
    not in the clause list.
