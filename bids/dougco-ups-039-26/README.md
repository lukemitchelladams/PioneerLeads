## Scope summary
Semi-annual preventive maintenance (with parts) on the County's UPS and lighting-inverter plant at 14 "MAINTENANCE LOCATIONS"
(Philip S. Miller Building, Wilcox Building, Parker Yard Crew Building, Covenant Community Tower and others; full list in the
IFB). Units named in the IFB include Liebert EXM 30/40 kVA, Gamatronic 50 kVA, Liebert Npower 130 kVA, EvenLite lighting
inverters, Powerware 93E 30 kVA, Toshiba G9000 160 kVA, APC (2.2 to 20 kVA), Tripplite SU30K 30 kVA, BEST 4.3 kVA, GE GT5000
5 kVA (x3), Eaton 9PXM 20 kVA and 93PM 50 kVA, MGE EPS6000 150 kVA and Galaxy 5000 50 kVA, Schneider GVSUPS 150 kVA,
Emerson/Liebert APS 20 kVA. Scope of Work pages list the UPS and VRLA battery PM steps ("Verify/calibrate proper float and
equalize setting for installed battery plant", "Perform load tests on each unit (open circuit), if appropriate", etc.).

## Key requirements (quote the document, cite section)
- Technicians: "All bid responses shall include resumes of the licensed/certified Colorado state technicians that would be" assigned, "based within seventy-five (75) miles of Douglas County, Colorado." (Special Conditions item 2)
- Narrative (item 4): address "Normal business hours, after hours, weekends & holidays", "Response time for emergency and non-emergency services", "Stocking of parts; ordering of parts; and how parts are shipped."
- References: "furnish a list of three (3) current references to include name, title, address, telephone/fax numbers, and e-mail address".
- Access: "BADGE ACCESS TO COUNTY FACILITIES" — "law enforcement and judicial buildings that may contain Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) material"; background checks before work.
- Insurance: "proof of and the required limits of liability insurance, including Workers' Compensation" by Certificate of Insurance; limits are in Exhibit A (sample contract, not yet quoted here).
- Exceptions: deviations must be listed on separate paper "boldly marked 'EXCEPTIONS TO IFB SPECIFICATIONS'".
- Payment "Net 30"; cooperative-purchasing clause (other agencies may use the award).
