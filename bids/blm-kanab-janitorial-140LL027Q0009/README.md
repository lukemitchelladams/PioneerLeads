## Scope summary (quoted)

- Building (PWS A.1): *"The cleanable space, excluding the pavilion and grounds, is estimated to be approximately 13,000
  square feet of space, consisting of various types of flooring."* Place (PWS A.4): *"669 S Highway 89A Kanab, Utah 84741 in
  Kane County"*. The building is single-story (Attachment 7 layout). The District building *"provides visitation from the
  public and workspaces for staff from the District Office, Grand Staircase Escalante National Monument and the Kanab Field
  Office"* (A.1).
- Areas (PWS E.1): lobby *"approximately 850 square feet of concrete flooring"* + conference rooms *"approximately 1100 square
  feet of carpeted space"*. Public restrooms *"approximately 400 square feet of tile"*: men's *"2 flush toilet, 2 flush urinal,
  2 sinks"*, women's *"3 flush toilet, 3 feminine hygiene receptacles, 2 sinks"*, a baby changing station in each. West staff
  *"approximately 2100 square feet, 1400 square feet of carpet, 700 square feet of stained concrete"*. Breakroom *"approximately
  290 square feet"* with *"3 fridges, 1 double sink"*. Closet *"100 square feet"*. East staff *"approximately 7400 square feet,
  620 square feet of carpet, 6800 square feet of stained concrete"*. East staff restrooms (2 x single toilet). Breezeway/
  pavilion picnic tables. Warehouse *"two restrooms, shower/locker room, hallway and a stainless-steel water fountain"*,
  *"approximately 170 square feet of tile and approximately 220 square feet of stained concrete"*.
- Frequency (PWS A.5.1): every area *"Monday, Wednesday, Friday"* (PRS column D "MWF" on every routine task).
- Hours: *"between the hours of 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. during the week"* (A.5). Once cleared, staff *"shall schedule all cleaning
  operations to be completed between 6 a.m. and 10 p.m., during non-business hours"*. Uncleared staff *"will need to schedule
  work with the COR during business hours"* (A.5.1). Public hours *"8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m."* (A.5). Holidays: *"The Contractor is
  not expected to work on federal holidays"* (A.5.3.1). The Attachment 4 calendars show which cleanings move.
- Concrete floors: *"Conditioner shall be applied bi-weekly"* (E.2.1.3 and the other concrete paragraphs). The named product is
  *"L&M Construction Chemicals Inc, FGS Concrete Conditioner ... applied bi-weekly by use of wet mop or brush and semi-annually
  by use of mechanical walk-behind scrubber with soft scrubbing pads"* (E.2.10.2).
- Semi-annual (E.2.10, *"twice during the contract base year and each option year ... between January/February and
  July/August"*): interior glass, floor conditioning, exterior glass, public-area carpet extraction (*"cottonwood and Ponderosa
  conference rooms"*), walls and ceilings. Annual (E.2.11, *"July/August"*): staff-area carpet extraction, detail grout
  cleaning. As needed on COR request: E.3 initial tile and grout, E.4 optional task services.
- Contractor furnishes (PWS Section D): *"'Green' cleaning/disinfectant liquids, hand soap, toilet tissue, paper towels,
  trash/waste receptacle liners, feminine hygiene receptacle liners, disposable toilet seat covers, stainless steel cleaner"*.
  Government furnishes (C.2/C.3) only *"Recycling bags and Safety Data Sheet (SDS) binder"*, plus *"electricity, fresh water,
  dumpster"* and one storage area (C.1).
- Personnel (A.6): *"A fully qualified workforce shall be on board no later than the second week of the contract."* A.6.1:
  contact the COR *"at least once a month"*. A.6.2: a *"Project Supervisor or Manager"*, with name and telephone to the COR
  *"at the onset of this contract"*.
- Security (A.8): a Government PIV card before unsupervised access. *"The BLM will be responsible for the costs incurred to
  acquire the initial 5 personnel security clearances."* After that, costs are the contractor's. SF-85/85P, OF-306, credit
  release and a PIV application *"before the start of the of the contract"*. This *"must be incorporated into any
  subcontracts"*. Lost cards are *"replaced at the Contractor's expense"* (A.8, A.10).
- Quality (PWS G.2): PRS AQL is *"No more than 2 'less than acceptable' findings per a month"* for most routine tasks. *"a
  percentage of the monthly payment will be withheld based on the remediation %, at a minimum of 1%."* Inspections use
  Attachment 6 (G.3). Invoices are monthly in IPP (PWS H.1; DOI-AAAP-0028, Sol p.8).
- Line items (Attachment 1 Bid Schedule, 22 lines per year): lines 1-12 *"January - General Cleaning A.5.1."* ... *"December
  ..."* priced per cleaning x *"# of Cleanings"* (base year 147: 11, 11, 14, 13, 12, 12, 12, 13, 12, 12, 13, 12; OY1 148, OY2
  149, OY3 148, OY4 149). Lines 13-17 semi-annual, qty 2. Lines 18-19 annual, qty 1. Lines 20-22 *"Upon COR's Request"*, qty 2
  each, *"*Requires COR approval"*. J8 *"=J7*0.5"* (52.217-8) and J9 *"TOTAL PRICE"* *"=SUM(J3:J8)"*. The SF-1449 CLINs
  00100-00500 (Sol p.2) carry no quantities.

## Go / no-go (2026-11-18)

**GO.** Janitorial is a core trade (CLAUDE.md rule 8). There is no walk and no bond, license or insurance clause, and the
evaluation does not penalise a non-local prime: both recent incumbents were out-of-state firms. The price is built from the
documents (`private/PRICING-WORKSHEET.md`: hours from the PWS square footage and frequencies, WD 2015-5495 Rev. 30, the same
markup method as Pagosa and BGNDRF). The Bid Schedule is filled, and its arithmetic check passes (`private/`). That makes it
submittable the week of 2026-11-23 and meets the weekly floor. Risks:
1. Past performance (Factor 2) is thin. Only the sub's projects can fill it (`RESPONSE-DRAFT.md` Part 2).
2. Price vs recent history: see `private/PRICING-WORKSHEET.md` section (h) before Luke approves the number.
3. Kanab is remote (about 80 road miles from St. George). The crew must live in Kanab or Fredonia. That is the main technical
   factor, and the whole of RESPONSE-DRAFT Part 1.
4. Questions close 11/25 and answers come later as an amendment. If we submit before the answers, any Amendment 0002 is
   acknowledged by email before 12/09 (SF-30 block 11 method (c) allows *"separate letter or electronic communication"*), and
   the quote is revised if the answers change scope or price.
