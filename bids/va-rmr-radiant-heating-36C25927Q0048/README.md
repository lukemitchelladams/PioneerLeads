## Limitations on subcontracting: what applies and how Brown Bison complies
- **In the documents after Amd 0001: nothing.** 52.219-14 is not marked, and the VAAR 852.219-75 certificate and Volume IV are
  dropped. **Do not sign or attach the VAAR form anyway**: as written it certifies Brown Bison *"will not pay more than 50% ...
  to firms that are not certified SDVOSBs ... or certified VOSBs"* under an 18 U.S.C. 1001 warning. Brown Bison is not an SDVOSB
  or VOSB and its sub does the field work, so signing it would be a false certification.
- **Regulation (not in the solicitation):** 13 CFR 125.6(a) applies the limit to *"a full or partial small business set-aside
  contract with a value greater than the simplified acquisition threshold"*; for services, *"it will not pay more than 50% of
  the amount paid by the government to it to firms that are not similarly situated."* (eCFR, read 2026-11-20). For a small
  business set-aside, a similarly situated entity is a small-business sub that is small under the NAICS code assigned to the
  subcontract (13 CFR 125.1, quoted in the 11/17 folder). Our documents-based price is below the threshold, so the rule does not
  reach this award.
- **How Brown Bison stays compliant either way:** use a sub that is small under NAICS 238220 (asked in the first email). Its work
  then counts as Brown Bison's for the 50% test, so Brown Bison plus one small-business sub meets the limit even if the CO later
  reads 52.219-14 in. A large mechanical sub would not.
