OVERVIEW:

   Through this Invitation for Bid (IFB), the Department of Facilities & Fleet Services of Douglas County
   Government, hereinafter referred to as the County, respectfully requests bids from responsible and highly-
   qualified companies for the provision of generator service and sales to include: Preventative Maintenance (PM)

   Load Bank testing for all generators and associated equipment at various Douglas County facilities located within
   Douglas County, Colorado beginning January 2027.

   approximately January 1, 2027 to and including December 31, 2027. The County shall have the option to renew
   the award for four (4) additional periods of one (1) year each, with the same terms and conditions. All pricing
   shall be firm and fixed for the initial agreement/contract period and may be renegotiated thereafter. This
   agreement/contract and any extension to the original period of a subject agreement/contract shall be contingent
   upon annual funding being appropriated, budgeted, and otherwise made available for such purposes and subject
   to the County’s unanimous satisfaction with the products and services received during the preceding
   agreement/contract period.

                                BID SPECIFICATIONS

1) Preventative Maintenance (PM) Inspection:

        •    Lubricating Oil System: Change oil and replace oil filters.
        •    Coolant System: Inspect the cooling system and top off fluids.
        •    Fuel Systems: Replace fuel filters, inspect breathers, and fuel regulation components.
        •    Belts and Hoses: Inspect and adjust engine belts, hoses, and clamps. Inspect the fan guard for
             condition and security.
        •    Exhaust System: Inspect, clean, and/or replace air filters.
        •    Battery System: Load test battery and record VDC readings. Service batteries add water to
             electrolytes and clean posts. Check and record battery static and running charging rates.
        •    Starter System: Check the unit for proper start initialization.
        •    Gauges and Instrumentation: Check, calibrate, and record AC output voltage and frequency.
        •    Generator: Inspect engine, alternator, control wiring, electrical safety shutdown devices, and
             block/pan heaters. Wipe down the outside of the generator and engine.
        •    Leaks: Inspect engine gaskets, breathers, and fuel regulation components for any fuel or oil leakage.
        •    Vents: Inspect dampers, louvers, and air intakes for restrictions and proper operation.
        •    Vibrations: Inspect the unit for excessive noise or vibrations. Inspect mounting bolts, brackets, and
             coupling connections.

2) Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS):
      a) Operation: Inspect for overheated power terminals and lugs. Inspect control board wiring and
         connections. Inspect mechanical switchgear mechanisms. Check and adjust exercise clock settings
         (as needed). Check time delays (as needed). Perform a power outage simulation with the owner's
         permission.

3) Load Bank Test:

        a) Procedure: Annual and thirty-six (36) month Load Bank Test, NFPA 110, 8.4.9. The generator
           will be loaded to a load factor of not less than 50% of the EPS kW nameplate rating for 30 minutes,
           then increase to a load factor of not less than 75% of the EPS kW nameplate rating for 60 minutes.
           For the remaining 2.5 hours, the generator will be loaded to a load factor of not less than 30% of the
           EPS kW rating for a total run time of 4 continuous hours.
        b) Justice Center is every four (4) years.

4) Fuel, Coolant, Oil Testing
       a) Fuel, coolant, and oil shall be tested based on NFPA 110 and ASTM standards.
