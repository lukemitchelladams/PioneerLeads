#### 1.c.1 Written safety plan for the Aims account

> **Standards.** OSHA general industry rules, including 29 CFR 1910 Subpart D (walking-working surfaces: 1910.23 ladders,
> 1910.27 scaffolds and rope descent systems, 1910.28 duty to have fall protection, 1910.30 training), 1910.67 (vehicle-mounted
> elevating and rotating work platforms, where it applies), 1910.140 (personal fall protection systems) and 1910.1200
> (hazard communication); ANSI/IWCA I-14.1, Window Cleaning Safety Standard; ANSI A92.22 and A92.24 (safe use of and training
> for mobile elevating work platforms); equipment manufacturers' instructions; and the College's access and security rules.
>
> **Program.** Field work runs under [SUB: window-cleaning sub]'s written safety and fall-protection program [SUB: title and
> date; attach]. This Aims plan adds the site rules below. Brown Bison flows both into the subcontract and checks compliance
> through the records in 1.c.3 and 1.c.4 and the completion reports in TAB 1.d.
>
> **Rules on every Aims building:**
> 1. **Ground first.** Glass is cleaned from the ground with pure water-fed poles wherever reach and glass type allow. On
>    these one- to three-story buildings that keeps most exterior work off ladders, lifts and ropes.
> 2. **Written work plan before any elevated work**: access method for each elevation, lift positions or anchorages,
>    hazards, exclusion zones and the rescue method (hazard assessment, 1.c.2).
> 3. **Rope descent only from certified anchorages.** A rope descent system is used only where the College has informed us in
>    writing that each anchorage has been identified, tested, certified and maintained, as OSHA 1910.27(b)(1) requires, and
>    each worker on it also uses a separate, independent personal fall arrest system. Where no certified anchorage exists, the
>    glass is reached by lift, pole or ladder instead. [QUESTIONS-DRAFT Q4 asks which buildings have certified anchorages.]
> 4. **Lifts.** Operated only by trained, authorized operators; boom-lift occupants tied off to the manufacturer's anchor point;
>    ground checked before setup (turf, irrigation boxes, slopes, curbs); outriggers on pads; overhead power lines identified
>    and kept clear; barricaded exclusion zone below.
> 5. **Ladders** per 1910.23: short tasks only, on a firm and level base, never on wet or icy surfaces.
> 6. **Never alone at height.** At least two trained workers are on site whenever lift, ladder or rope work is under way, one
>    of them able to start the rescue in 1.c.5.
> 7. **Weather.** Elevated work stops in high wind, lightning, ice or snow. Exterior water-fed pole work is scheduled when
>    temperatures keep water from freezing on glass, frames and walks. [SUB: wind and temperature limits from the
>    subcontractor's program and ANSI/IWCA I-14.1.]
> 8. **Public and occupant protection.** Cones, signs and barricades at each work zone; entries kept usable or an alternate
>    route marked; no lift or rope work over an occupied doorway or walkway; drop cloths and floor protection inside;
>    landscaping, furnishings and finishes protected (p.3).
> 9. **Chemicals.** Pure water first. Any cleaning product is used per its Safety Data Sheet, with the SDS on site, and is
>    safe for the glass coatings, seals and frames. [SUB: product list.]
> 10. **Electrical.** No water-fed pole work near overhead lines or exterior electrical equipment; corded tools on GFCI
>     protection.
> 11. **Incidents.** Any injury, near miss, property damage or unsafe condition is reported to the College's designated site
>     contact and to Brown Bison the same day, with a written report within one business day [HQ default].
> 12. **Airport.** Workers at the Flight Training Center hold TSA clearance and follow the airport's security and badge rules
>     (p.2) [College: any airside or escort rules, QUESTIONS-DRAFT Q5].
