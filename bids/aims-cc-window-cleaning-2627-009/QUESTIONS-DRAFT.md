**Q1. "Bi-Annual Cost" column (Attachment C, pp.13-14).** The scope of work (p.3) requires interior and exterior glass to be
cleaned "annually on a rotating basis." Attachment C asks for an "Exterior Cost," an "Interior Cost" and a "Bi-Annual Cost"
for each building. Please define the Bi-Annual Cost: (a) the price for cleaning the building twice in the same contract
year, (b) the price for one additional cleaning in a year, or (c) a cleaning every two years. Should the Bi-Annual Cost
include both interior and exterior glass, or exterior only? Should the Exterior Cost and Interior Cost each be the price of
one cleaning per contract year? How will the three columns be used in the cost evaluation?

**Q2. Number of campuses (p.2).** Page 2 states that Aims "currently consists of five area campuses" and "currently operates
five campuses," but lists four locations: Greeley, Loveland/Fort Collins Airport, Fort Lupton and Windsor. Please identify
the fifth campus, confirm whether it is in the scope of this RFP, and if it is, provide its buildings, building heights and
a pricing line on Attachment C.

**Q3. Buildings to be priced (Greeley map, p.2, Attachment C).** (a) The Greeley campus map shows Aviation Technology (AVT),
5109, which is not on Attachment C. Is it in scope? (b) Page 2 lists the "Flight Training and Aircraft Maintenance Training
Centers" at the airport, but Attachment C lists only the Flight Training Center. Is the Aircraft Maintenance Training Center
in scope? (c) Summit, Student Health and Workforce Innovation are on Attachment C but not on the Greeley map. Please provide
their locations. (d) Please provide the street addresses of the Windsor, Fort Lupton and airport buildings. (e) For
Facilities Shops ("Bay doors") and Purchasing ("Single Story & Bay doors"), is the glass in the bay doors the only glass to be
cleaned, or all windows on those buildings? (f) What does "Optional Services" (p.3) include, and should optional services be
priced on an additional page?

**Q4. Bidder site visits (p.2) and building access.** Page 2 makes each bidder responsible for site visits "across all
campuses to confirm dimensions before submitting pricing," and page 4 names Purchasing as the sole contact. How should a
bidder schedule its own visits, including interior access and access to the airport building? Will the College hold escorted
site visits at Windsor, Fort Lupton and the airport, or provide dates? Is per-building glass square footage available, or
the breakdown behind the "approximately 150,000 square feet of exterior glazing/windows"? Which buildings, if any, have roof
anchorages that the College has identified, tested and certified for rope descent work, and will the College provide that
certification in writing? Are there buildings where the College requires or prohibits lifts on turf, walks or plazas?

**Q5. TSA clearance at the airport (p.2).** Page 2 states "The awarded contractor must obtain TSA clearance for Fort Collins
Airport. Badging fees range from $50 to $125." Which office issues the badge, how long does clearance usually take, and must
each worker who enters the Flight Training Center hold a badge, or may workers be escorted by College staff? Is the
building inside the airport's secured area? Should the badging fee be included in the price on Attachment C?

**Q6. Firm pricing over the full term (p.7, p.2).** Page 7 states "Submitted pricing is firm and cannot be increased," and
page 2 sets "an initial one (1) year term, with the option to renew for four (4) additional one-year terms." Please confirm
whether Attachment C prices must hold for all five years, or whether a proposer may state a price adjustment for each renewal
year (for example a fixed percentage or an index) for the College to consider at renewal. How will buildings added under
"Additional locations may be added throughout the term" (p.2) be priced?

**Q7. Experience, references and safety record (p.3, p.5, p.15).** Page 3 requires a "Minimum of (1) year in commercial
multi-site window maintenance" and "At least 3 comparable client references." May a proposer meet the experience minimum and
the reference requirement through its proposed team, including a disclosed subcontractor that will perform the window
cleaning, with references labeled as the subcontractor's? For TAB 1.c ("OSHA compliance history (EMR rate)") and Attachment D
("a three (3) year history of workmen's compensation experience"), how should a proposer with less than three years of
history complete the form, and may it also submit the subcontractor's EMR and loss history?

**Q8. Insurance, digital accessibility, withdrawal and dates (pp.5-8, p.1).** (a) The insurance section (pp.5-6) uses the
terms "Subcontractor," "Contractor" and "Owner," and requires that "Contractor and Owner shall be listed as an additional
insured." Please name the entities to be listed as additional insureds and certificate holder, and confirm whether the "Per
Project Aggregate" and the completed-operations coverage to the statute of repose apply to this services contract. Is a
certificate of insurance required with the proposal or only at award? (b) Does the Digital Accessibility section (p.8),
including the VPAT and HECVAT, apply to a window-cleaning contract with no software or digital deliverables? (c) Page 8 allows
withdrawal "by written facsimile request." May a proposer instead withdraw or replace its response through BidNet before the
deadline? (d) Please confirm the "Anticipated Award Notification" date, which reads "January 2076" on pages 1 and 4.

**Q9. Licensing and evaluation (p.7, p.3, p.5).** (a) Minimum requirement 3 on page 7 reads "Required licensing, if any,"
and page 3 asks proposers to confirm "that required permits, licenses, and building-access approvals are obtained." Does
the College require any specific license, registration or certification for this work, at the state or municipal level
or from an industry body? (b) Page 7 states that proposals "will be reviewed and scored based on the example score sheet."
Please post the example score sheet. (c) TAB 1.a (page 5) asks for "years of experience in door services." Please confirm
this means window-cleaning services.
