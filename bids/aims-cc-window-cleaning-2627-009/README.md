## Scope summary (RFP pp.2-3, Attachment C pp.13-14)

"Aims Community College (the "College") is seeking proposals from qualified vendors to provide window cleaning
services." "The vendor must have the capacity to provide services, as requested, to all the locations: 1. Greeley
(Main Campus) 2. Loveland/Fort Collins Airport (Flight Training and Aircraft Maintenance Training Centers) 3. Fort
Lupton 4. Windsor" (p.2). (The same page says "five area campuses located across Greeley, Loveland, Ft Lupton, and
Windsor" but lists four.)

**Quantity:** "Estimated approximately 150,000 square feet of exterior glazing/windows across all campuses and
facilities. This quantity is provided for bidding purposes only and is not guaranteed." (p.2). No per-building square
footage is given.

**Glass and work types (p.2-3):**
- "Annual/Rotating Interior and Exterior Cleaning: Full glass surfaces, glass doors, transoms, sidelights, and atrium glass."
- "Detailing: Cleaning of window frames, sills, tracks, and removal of cobwebs, paint overspray, or mineral deposits."
- "Specialized Glass: Lower high-rise exterior glass requiring rope access, boom lifts, or suspended scaffolds, water-fed poles and lifts."
- "Optional Services" (listed with no detail).
- Contractor provides "all labor, supervision, equipment, materials, transportation, and safety measures" to: "Clean exterior glass annually on a rotating basis"; "Clean interior glass annually on a rotating basis"; "Clean frames, sill, ledges, and mullions"; "Remove any ordinary dirt, dust, fingerprints, and water marks"; "Protect occupants, landscaping furnishings, finishes, and equipment"; "Report damaged glass, failed seals, unsafe access points, or other site issues promptly"; "Complete work during approved hours and coordinate access with the designated site contract".

**Heights / access:** tallest buildings listed are "Three Story" (Applied Technology, Welcome Center, Workforce
Innovation, Gateway). Access methods named: "rope access, boom lifts, or suspended scaffolds, water-fed poles and
lifts" (p.3); TAB 1.e asks for "equipment utilization (e.g. pure water-fed poles, lifts, etc.)" (p.5).

**Frequency:** the scope says interior and exterior glass are cleaned "annually on a rotating basis" (p.3), i.e.
once a year per the text. Attachment C adds a third price column, "Bi-Annual Cost", for every building. The RFP does
not define "Bi-Annual" (twice a year or every two years) or say which frequency is ordered. Q&A item.
