1.   SCOPE OF WORK
     The Contractor shall be responsible for providing custodial services, graffiti removal,
     and snow removal at RTD Shelters on North and South Federal,“Group 3 shelters,
     Federal and Colfax transfer center and Federal and Evans transfer Center. It is the responsibility
     of the Contractor that all pedestrian areas are free from all dangerous conditions at
     all times. A dangerous condition means a physical condition of a facility or the use
     thereof that constitutes an unreasonable risk to the health or safety of the public,
     which is known to exist or which in the exercise of reasonable care should have
     been known to exist. The Contractor will be legally responsible for any
     dangerous condition that it causes or if it fails to remove a dangerous condition
     as required by these technical specifications. Proper insurance coverage will be
     required to cover any claims resulting from accidents and the Contractor shall
     defend any lawsuits addressing that issue.
     The intent of this Contract is to provide Janitorial, Graffiti Removal and Snow
     Removal services to the RTD facilities listed in this specification, all inclusive, in one
     lump sum invoiced and paid monthly.
     The main objective of these custodial services is to maintain RTD sites and its
     surrounding areas to be as aesthetically pleasing as possible for patrons using RTD
     transportation services. A Custodial Plan, Graffiti Plan, Snow Removal Plan, Quality
     Control Plan, and a Staffing Plan are required to be submitted with your proposal that
     addresses the following requirements:

Pressure Washing
A.    Shelters, trash cans, and the area immediately around the shelter (10’ from
      each side minimum) will require power washing. The minimum frequency for
      power washing will be weekly on shelters receiving service five or more times
      per week and monthly for shelters receiving service less than five times per
      week. Personnel servicing shelters will utilize a vehicle equipped with the
      required pressure washing equipment at all times. Pressure washing will be
      performed as part of the standard shelter service anytime an unacceptable
      condition is discovered. When pressure washing is required, use a high-
      pressure low volume hot water washer unit capable of producing a minimum
      3000 PSI. Water consumption shall be kept to a minimum to prevent
      wastewater from running into storm sewer drains. Water is not available on
      site for Contractor use! Contractor is to transport water to each location as
      needed.
B.    The requirements of the Clean Water Act shall be observed. No additives may
      be added to pressure washer water that may run into a storm sewer.
      Absorbent tubes “socks” shall be used to keep any water from entering the
      storm sewer drainage system. Guidelines and requirements of the Colorado
      Department of Public Health and Environment or any other Relevant Authority
      regarding pressure washing shall be followed at all times.

6.   GRAFFITI REMOVAL
     The Contractor shall be responsible for removing all graffiti on RTD property. Graffiti
     shall be removed upon discovery from shelters, trash receptacles, and concrete
     surfaces maintained under this specification.
     Graffiti is to be removed in a professional manner and the surface restored to its pre
     graffiti condition. Graffiti shall be removed as soon as possible to discourage
     additional activity. In no instance shall graffiti remain for more than 72 hours unless
     written approval is received from RTD FM Manager. It is a requirement of this
     Contract to maintain all property, in a professional manner, and maintain an as close
     to new appearance as possible.
     When removing graffiti on concrete, removal, rather than cover, is the first priority
     and the preferred method. When painting over graffiti, paint should match as close
     as industry color matching will allow. On various occasions it will be necessary to
     paint from a parting line or seam to provide a uniform appearance when covering
     graffiti.
     Graffiti Removal Plan: A Graffiti Removal plan is required to be submitted with your
     proposal and should include the following:
          A. Equipment to be used for graffiti removal.
          B. Training to be provided to employees performing graffiti removal.
          C. Summary of techniques used for graffiti removal on concrete, concrete block,
             glass, and polycarbonate plastic used on schedule boards,
          D. Graffiti removal and surface restoration on glass and polycarbonate glass
             appear to be particularly challenging. Provide detailed information on the
             process for removing graffiti and restoring surfaces of these materials in your
             graffiti removal plan.

General Snow Removal Requirements
It is the responsibility of the Contractor that all pedestrian and vehicle surfaces are
free from all Dangerous Conditions at all times. The Contractor will be legally
responsible for all snow and ice removal and the pooling of water on all sites listed
in this Contract. Proper insurance coverage (listing RTD as an "additional insured")
will be required to cover any claims resulting from accidents and the Contractor shall
defend any lawsuits addressing those issues. Snow removal and ice control services
on all pedestrian and vehicle surfaces located on RTD property as well as property
RTD is legally required to maintain, such as perimeter city sidewalks, are included as
a requirement of this specification. Vehicle surfaces include, but are not limited to,
bus lanes, streets, and drive ways. Pedestrian surfaces include, but are not limited
to: sidewalks, walkways, ramps, stairs, crosswalks, platforms and plaza areas. RTD's
facilities are continuously relied upon by the elderly and disabled for safe reliable
transportation. Snow removal services are expected to be the highest level of service
provided in the industry.
Please note that RTD uses security cameras at all light rail stations to monitor
passenger areas for safety, including looking for Dangerous Conditions caused by
snow and ice and monitoring Contractor performance.
   A. Ice Control Material: All ice control material must be approved by the RTD
      Contracted Public Facilities Manager prior to use. Currently approved RTD ice
      control material is industry standard ice slicer or magnesium chloride in bus
      lanes and vehicle surfaces and magnesium chloride on pedestrian surfaces.


                                       7
B. Ice Control Material Application: Except for areas that have "new concrete"
   as listed in paragraph "J" (New Concrete Surfaces) below, all surfaces should
   be treated with an anti-icing material prior to the start of any event that could
   potentially cause ice to build on pedestrian or vehicle surfaces. Ice control
   material should then be reapplied as needed after shoveling or plowing to
   ensure the surfaces are acceptably maintained. Contractor shall use best
   industry practices and common sense when applying ice control materials. At
   a minimum, all ice control material must be applied at the suppliers listed
   application rates. Minimum application rate for Ice Slicer shall be at a rate of
   one ton per 10,000 square feet.
C. Pedestrian Surfaces: Anti-icing material shall be applied to pedestrian surfaces
   prior to the start of a snow or ice event. Snow should be removed from
   pedestrian surfaces as soon as accumulation begins. Ice control material shall
   be applied after snow removal to ensure an acceptable, clear, ice-free surface
   is maintained. Snow removal from pedestrian surfaces will continue during the
   storm to ensure acceptable conditions are experienced at all times.
   Snow may NOT be stored on pedestrian or vehicle surfaces without prior
   written approval. Snow may not be stored on platforms with the exception of
   temporary snow storage locations pre-approved by RTD. Snow will be required
   to be relocated from platforms to acceptable snow storage locations at the
   end of each storm and prior to crews leaving site. Snow may NOT be stored
   in an area that will potentially damage landscape. Snow may NOT be stored
   in an area that, when melting, will cause water to run across a maintained
   surface. This may require snow to be moved to alternate onsite locations via
   truck, wheelbarrow or other means at no additional cost to RTD.
D. Bus and Traffic Lanes: Anti-icing material shall be applied to bus lane surfaces
   prior to the start of a snow or ice event. Bus lanes will be plowed and ice
   slicer applied to maintain an acceptable surface at all times. Contractor shall
   use their best efforts to prevent packed snow/ice from accumulating in bus or
   traffic lanes, and apply ice-melting materials and/or scrape areas of
   accumulation as quickly as possible.
E. Vehicle Surfaces: Anti-icing material shall be applied to all vehicle surfaces
   prior to the start of a snow or ice event. Vehicle surfaces include, but are not
   limited to, driveways, access lanes, and bus lanes. Vehicle surfaces will be
   plowed and treated with ice control material to maintain an acceptable surface
   at all times. Contractor must use their best efforts to prevent packed snow/ice
   from accumulating on vehicle surfaces, and apply ice melting materials and/or
   scrape areas of accumulation as quickly as possible.
F. Snow Removal from site: Snow removal and hauling from various Contracted
   locations may be required during heavy accumulations. Snow removal and
   hauling from Contracted locations will be performed on a reimbursable basis
   utilizing the hourly rates submitted by the Contractor only with the approval
   of RTD. At locations with available onsite snow storage the Contractor shall
   include in the base bid the cost for moving this snow onsite as needed. Moving
   snow to onsite snow storage is expected at no additional cost to RTD.


                                   8
G. Sweeping: Any solid material/product used for ice control, traction, etc., must
   be swept up at no additional cost and removed from RTD sidewalks, stairs,
   drive lanes, and plazas within twenty-four (24) hours of the snow event in
   which it was first laid down. This requirement may be waived by the RTD
   Contracted Public Facilities Manager if snow is expected in the next several
   days. Ice and snow control product must be swept up and disposed of
   properly. At no time shall the Contractor place ice control products in the tree
   pits, flower beds, or sod areas. The Contractor must notify RTD when
   sweeping operations begin and end.
H. Pressure Washing: It may be necessary to pressure-wash the areas that have
   been treated by the Contractor with any substance used for snow and ice
   control. Ice melt shall be washed from platforms and all other steel structures
   within 72 hours following a snow event to prevent corrosion. During extreme
   temperatures this may be extended by the RTD FM Manager.
I.   Appropriately Sized Equipment: Walk behind snow blowers or other
     mechanical devices or hand shoveling may be necessary wherever vehicular
     equipment is prohibited or cannot adequately perform the snow removal tasks.
     Snow brushes are the preferred method of removal when conditions allow.
     Snow brushes clean the surface of snow much better than shovels or blowers
     and can reduce the amount of ice melt material required.
J. New Concrete Surfaces: Melting agents shall not be applied on new concrete
   surfaces. Sand mix only will be applied to all new concrete areas in place of
   chemicals and will need to be swept up within twenty-four (24) hours after a
   snow event. RTD shall specify any "new concrete surfaces" for special
   handling. This requirement will last for one year after the new concrete has
   been installed.
K. Snow Removal Performance Standard
     Contractor is legally responsible to make sure that all areas are free of any
     Dangerous Condition caused by an accumulation of snow or ice. The
     Contractor shall place special emphasis to ensure pedestrian and vehicle
     surfaces are in acceptable condition prior to the 6 a.m. and 3 p.m. peak
     periods Monday-Friday , however, the Contractor is required to ensure
     Acceptable Conditions are present at all times and that no Dangerous
     Conditions exist (including all off-peak hours and weekends and RTD
     recognized holidays). The Contractor shall have adequate equipment and
     personnel to prepare all surfaces receiving less than 8" accumulation in non-
     blizzard conditions within three (3) hours.
     An Acceptable Condition (surface) is defined as follows:
        1. The surfaces are free of snow, melting snow and ice, and dry; or if
           wet, there is low probability for additional snowfall or of freezing to
           occur within the next 12 hours;
        2. Pedestrian walks, crosswalks, boarding platforms, and drive through
           lanes have been shoveled or plowed, ice melt chemicals have been
           applied and slip hazards are not present.
