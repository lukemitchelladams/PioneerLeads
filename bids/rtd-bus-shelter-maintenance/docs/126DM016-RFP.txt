2.      INSTRUCTIONS TO PROPOSERS

2.1.     Work
RTD will accept proposals from qualified companies until 2:00 pm on Monday,
November 16, 2026 via RTD's online procurement portal, OpenGov. This Request for
Proposal (“RFP”) is for Bus Shelter Maintenance Services (15% SBE Goal), per the
enclosed specifications and requirements. RTD may award multiple contracts from this
RFP.

2.2.     Cost Type
Payments are anticipated to be made on a Fixed Price basis.

2.3.     Award of Contract
Award of a Contract from this RFP will be based upon Best Value competitive proposal
procedures.

2.4.     Period of Performance
Subject to the Termination provision of the Contract, performance shall commence as
of the Effective Date (or in the event that RTD provides a Notice to Proceed to the
Contractor, the Period of Performance shall commence on the date of the Notice to
Proceed) and shall continue for 3 years (subject to the exercise of any option periods).

2.5.     Options for this Contract
This Contract has the potential for two (2) 1-year options to extend the Period of
Performance.

2.6.     Small Business Requirement for Contracts with an SBE Goal
RTD has specified a SBE participation goal of 15% for the Work to be performed under
the Contract. Proposers must make adequate good faith efforts to meet this goal in
order to be deemed as a responsive and responsible proposer. Award of the Contract
will be conditioned on meeting the requirements of this section. 49 CFR Part 26.53 and
Appendix A of 49 CFR Part 26 shall serve as the criteria for evaluating compliance with
the good faith efforts requirements. Additionally, proposers are required to solicit the
support and assistance of RTD’s Small Business Opportunity Office if they are unable to
meet the SBE participation goal assigned to this Contract. The proposers can meet this
requirement in one of two ways:
First, the proposer can meet this goal by demonstrating and documenting its
commitments for participation by SBEs for at least the total percentage identified above
or a percentage that exceeds the goal for the project. The SBE Contract Requirements

ability to successfully respond to project SBE participation goals and compliance with
