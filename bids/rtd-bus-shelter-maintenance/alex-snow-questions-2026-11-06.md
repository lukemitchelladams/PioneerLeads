## What Luke tells Alex (paste-ready, no prices)

RTD's question window closed 11/2, so these come straight from their scope of work, not from RTD:
1. Peak windows are not deadlines. They want surfaces clear before AND during 6-9 AM and 3-6 PM, and "acceptable" at all times, nights, weekends and holidays included. Anything under 8 inches (non-blizzard) has to be cleared within 3 hours. Keep crews working through the storm.
2. No accumulation threshold. Start as soon as snow accumulates and keep clearing during the storm, as often as it takes. RTD only pays for two services a day per shelter (AM and PM), so price the per-service number knowing a long storm is two paid visits a day. The transfer centers are one flat price per month for 8 months, every visit included.
3. Yes, pretreat before any event that could put ice down, snow or no snow. Mag chloride on walks; Ice Slicer or mag chloride in bus lanes and drive areas; anything else needs RTD sign-off first. Minimum rate is the supplier's listed rate, and Ice Slicer is at least 1 ton per 10,000 sq ft. Sand only on any new concrete RTD flags, swept up within 24 hours.
4. No pile locations are given. Rules: no snow on walks, platforms, bus lanes, landscaping, or anywhere melt runs back across a walk; nothing pushed into the street or gutter; platforms cleared of piles at the end of each storm before you leave. Moving snow around on site is in the base price. Hauling off site only when RTD approves it, paid hourly, and that hourly rate has to be in our proposal, so send me a truck, loader and labor hourly rate instead of leaving it open.
5. Yes. All granular product gets swept up and removed from walks, stairs, drive lanes and plazas within 24 hours of the event (RTD can waive it if more snow is coming), and RTD gets told when sweeping starts and ends. Washing ice melt off platforms and steel within 72 hours is the pressure-wash crew, not you.
Also price in: GPS on every truck that RTD can watch live, a snow log per storm (location, time in and out, conditions, actions, equipment, crew) that RTD gets within 48 hours, photos of each visit, and RTD's insurance list applies to subs ($1M/$2M GL, $1M auto, $5M umbrella, RTD as additional insured). Your scope assumptions match the RFP, with #1 widening the timing one.

## Alex's questions

### 1. Service timing: strict completion deadlines, or active maintenance through the peaks during ongoing snowfall? ANSWERED: both, and at all times

- SOW §7 Snow Removal Plan A, pp. 6-7: the plan must give "Detailed information on how the Contractor plans to service
  several locations simultaneously to adequately prepare and maintain facilities entirely free of snow, ice and pooling
  water prior to and during the 6:00 a.m.to 9:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m.to 6:00 p.m. Peak Periods - and then to service and
  maintain all surfaces to be acceptable and free of Dangerous Conditions at all other times including during and after any
  snow event."
- SOW §7.C Pedestrian Surfaces, p. 8: "Snow should be removed from pedestrian surfaces as soon as accumulation begins.
  ... Snow removal from pedestrian surfaces will continue during the storm to ensure acceptable conditions are experienced
  at all times."
- SOW §7.K, p. 9: the 3-hour rule, "prepare all surfaces receiving less than 8" accumulation in non-blizzard conditions
  within three (3) hours", and "Acceptable Conditions are present at all times".
- Blizzard relief, SOW §7 Plan F, p. 7: "During blizzard conditions, 42" paths on walkways and across plaza areas will be
  acceptable for a limited time. However, platforms should be clear of snow to the maximum extent possible during blizzard
  conditions".
- Definition, SOW §12.D, p. 12: "Peak Periods:6:00 a.m.to 9:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m.to 6:00 p.m."

### 2. Repeat service in extended storms: how does RTD decide another clearing is needed; accumulation thresholds? ANSWERED: no threshold; the Acceptable Condition standard at all times; invoicing capped at two services a day

- No accumulation threshold appears anywhere in the SOW, RFP or Form of Contract. The trigger is the condition standard:
  SOW §12.B, pp. 11-12: "Acceptable Conditions( for snow and ice management): the surfaces are free of snow, melting snow
  and ice, and dry; or if wet, there is low probability for additional snowfall or of freezing to occur within the next
  12 hours; or a melting agent has been uniformly applied; and/or Pedestrian walks, crosswalks, boarding platforms, and
  drive through lanes have been shoveled or plowed, ice melt chemicals have been applied and slip hazards are not present."
- SOW §4 Snow Removal, p. 5: "as often as necessary to properly manage the Contractors liability, however, the Contractor
  may only invoice for a maximum of two services (AM and PM) per day."
- SOW §7.B, p. 8: "Ice control material should then be reapplied as needed after shoveling or plowing to ensure the
  surfaces are acceptably maintained."
- SOW §7 Plan E, p. 7: the plan must say "how the Contractor proposes to conduct quality control during snow or ice events
  and effectively monitor conditions during snow storms, ice storms etc." SOW §7, p. 7: "RTD uses security cameras at all
  light rail stations to monitor passenger areas for safety, including looking for Dangerous Conditions caused by snow and
  ice and monitoring Contractor performance."
- Pricing assumption: every return trip in a storm is covered by two paid services per shelter per day (per-service price
  on the sheet) and by the flat monthly price at the transfer centers. RTD's own basis is 70 events per shelter per year
  (Pricing Sheet).

### 3. Ice control: pretreat every forecast freezing event with no snow? Application rates or product rules beyond the scope? ANSWERED: yes, pretreat any potential icing event; rates and products are in the SOW

- SOW §7.B Ice Control Material Application, p. 8: "Except for areas that have "new concrete" as listed in paragraph "J"
  (New Concrete Surfaces) below, all surfaces should be treated with an anti-icing material prior to the start of any
  event that could potentially cause ice to build on pedestrian or vehicle surfaces." §7.C: "Anti-icing material shall be
  applied to pedestrian surfaces prior to the start of a snow or ice event." §7.D and §7.E say the same for bus lanes and
  vehicle surfaces. An ice event with no snow is an event.
- Rates, §7.B, p. 8: "At a minimum, all ice control material must be applied at the suppliers listed application rates.
  Minimum application rate for Ice Slicer shall be at a rate of one ton per 10,000 square feet."
- Products, §7.A Ice Control Material, p. 7: "All ice control material must be approved by the RTD Contracted Public
  Facilities Manager prior to use. Currently approved RTD ice control material is industry standard ice slicer or
  magnesium chloride in bus lanes and vehicle surfaces and magnesium chloride on pedestrian surfaces."
- New concrete, §7.J, p. 9: "Melting agents shall not be applied on new concrete surfaces. Sand mix only will be applied to
  all new concrete areas in place of chemicals and will need to be swept up within twenty-four (24) hours after a snow
  event. RTD shall specify any "new concrete surfaces" for special handling. This requirement will last for one year after
  the new concrete has been installed."
- Not stated: how a pretreatment-only trip (ice forecast, no snow) is paid. The Pricing Sheet has no pretreatment line;
  "service" is not defined. Pricing assumption: a pretreatment run counts as one of the two invoiceable daily services at
  the shelters and is inside the monthly price at the transfer centers; price material (mag chloride, Ice Slicer) into the
  per-service number. The sweep-up after an ice-only event (§7.G) is also at no additional cost.

### 4. Snow storage: designated pile locations at the transfer centers and shelters; restrictions beyond the RFP? PARTLY: rules yes, locations no

- No designated snow storage locations are named anywhere in the package. They are set through the Snow Removal Plan,
  which RTD pre-approves: SOW §7, p. 6: "A final version of this Plan will need to be pre-approved by RTD".
- Rules, SOW §7.C, p. 8: "Snow may NOT be stored on pedestrian or vehicle surfaces without prior written approval. Snow may
  not be stored on platforms with the exception of temporary snow storage locations pre-approved by RTD. Snow will be
  required to be relocated from platforms to acceptable snow storage locations at the end of each storm and prior to crews
  leaving site. Snow may NOT be stored in an area that will potentially damage landscape. Snow may NOT be stored in an area
  that, when melting, will cause water to run across a maintained surface. This may require snow to be moved to alternate
  onsite locations via truck, wheelbarrow or other means at no additional cost to RTD."
- SOW §4 Snow Removal, p. 5: "snow may not be pushed from the sidewalk into the street or gutter area."
- SOW §7.F, p. 8: on-site storage moves are in the base bid; hauling off site only with RTD approval, at the hourly rate.
- Pricing assumption: each location has some on-site storage off the walks and lanes (tree-lawn or a corner of the plaza
  that does not drain across a walk); no routine off-site hauling in the base; relocation off platforms at the end of each
  storm in the base. Nothing more can be asked of RTD before award; the storage locations go into the Snow Removal Plan.

### 5. Post-storm cleanup: does the 24-hour cleanup include removal of all residual granular de-icer from pedestrian and vehicle surfaces? ANSWERED: yes, at no additional cost

- SOW §7.G Sweeping, p. 9: "Any solid material/product used for ice control, traction, etc., must be swept up at no
  additional cost and removed from RTD sidewalks, stairs, drive lanes, and plazas within twenty-four (24) hours of the snow
  event in which it was first laid down. This requirement may be waived by the RTD Contracted Public Facilities Manager if
  snow is expected in the next several days. Ice and snow control product must be swept up and disposed of properly. At no
  time shall the Contractor place ice control products in the tree pits, flower beds, or sod areas. The Contractor must
  notify RTD when sweeping operations begin and end."
- New-concrete sand, §7.J, p. 9: "swept up within twenty-four (24) hours after a snow event."
- Related but not the snow sub's: SOW §7.H Pressure Washing, p. 9: "Ice melt shall be washed from platforms and all other
  steel structures within 72 hours following a snow event to prevent corrosion." That is pressure washing (cleaning sub).
  Brown Bison must make sure the cleaning sub's price carries it.
