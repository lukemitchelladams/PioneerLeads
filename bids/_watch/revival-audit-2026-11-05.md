**Answer.** No. Not one of the 18 open or recently-closed solicitations below asks for a COI with the bid or proposal. Every one puts the COI at award / contract execution / before work starts. The only package that asked for a certificate with the proposal was CMC custodial ("for evidence only"), and that bid was already dead on 2026-10-12 (mandatory walk). The insurance assumption was wrong for all nine dropped bids. What actually kills bids in this set is (a) missed mandatory walks and (b) bid bonds at submission (no surety yet).
