## Colorado state bonding help (DHR / Statewide Equity Office)
- The program Luke named: "Construction Assistance Program", page https://dhr.colorado.gov/statewide-equity-office/supplier-diversity/construction-assistance-program (fetched OK 2026-10-29). It is a help desk and education page, NOT a bond guarantee. It points to: Construction Bond Readiness Education (free, English/Spanish, "an entry-level training to help you learn more about construction bonds in State procurement"); the US DOT Bonding Education Program ("includes one-on-one sessions with local surety bonding professionals"); the Contractor Bonding Education & Mentoring Program ("designed to help new and emerging construction businesses, particularly minority-owned and other under-represented businesses, learn how to qualify for surety bonds"); and the US SBA Surety Bond Guarantee Program.
- Contact on that page: Colorado Supplier Diversity Navigator, DPA_SupplierDiversityHelp@state.co.us, 303-866-5765, plus a "Request for Assistance Form". Services listed include "Construction contracting assistance". Free listing in the Colorado Supplier Diversity Directory is open to "any business registered and in good standing with the Colorado Secretary of State" (https://dhr.colorado.gov/statewide-equity-office/supplier-diversity).
- The collateral program (Statewide Bond Assistance Program, SB22-163, $2 million): its DHR pages (.../statewide-bond-assistance-program-eligibility-guidelines and ...-frequently-asked-questions) now return "Access denied" (HTTP 403, "You are not authorized to access this page"), so I could not read them. A web-search summary of those pages said: small business located in Colorado, annual gross revenue under $5 million, "at least two years of operations", must finish the Construction Bond Assistance Training, one open SBAP-guaranteed contract per firm; the program acts as collateral for the surety and does not write bonds. Treat these terms as unverified by me.
- Status: BillTrack50 (https://www.billtrack50.com/billdetail/1996502) shows HB26-1388 "Repeal Bond Assistance Program Fund Transfer", "Governor Signed, 06/01/2026": repeals the Bond Assistance Program, moves the remaining cash fund to the General Fund on 2026-06-30 (already-obligated money still honored), repeal effective 2027-12-01. Cross-checked on https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/HB26-1388 via WebFetch: "The governor signed it on June 1, 2026", Session Law Chapter 284; program "repealed effective December 1, 2027".
- Bottom line for Brown Bison: do not plan around state collateral help. The fund was swept 2026-06-30, and even before that the stated two-year-in-business test would have excluded an LLC formed 2026-05. The usable pieces are the free bond training and the Navigator help desk. The SBA guarantee through an agent is the real path.
