## 2. City of Longmont RFP-426089, On-Call Specialized Electrical for Treatment Operations (BidNet 0000441987): NO

**Document in hand:** `RFP-426089_OC_Spec_Electrical.pdf` (80 pp) = RFP (19 pp) + Attachment A Proposal Acknowledgement, B Contract
Exceptions, C RFP Pricing Submittal, D Sample Contract, E Construction Contract General Conditions (with bond forms). Complete per the
RFP's own list.

**Scope (quoted):** "general and specialty electrical work for the Treatment Operations Group in the City's Water and Waste Department",
18 listed items incl. "Create dedicated circuits", "UPS supply power installations", "Replace building supply transformers", "Electrical
panel upgrades", "Provide emergency power during planned and unplanned outages", **"Excavation to install or intercept conduits and
feeders"**, "Design and installation of generators up to 200kW in size, along with accompanying automatic transfer switches (ATS)",
motors and VFDs, "PLCs and RTUs", "Process automation systems", "Installation and calibration of flow meters, pressure, level, and
temperature sensors". Sites: "Wastewater Treatment Plant, Nelson-Flanders Drinking Water Treatment Plant, Wade-Gaddis Drinking Water
Treatment Plant, Drinking Water Distribution System, Sanitary Collection System and Raw Water Supply System." "The Contractor must send
a journeyman or master electrician to every job." Task orders: NTE estimate "within 48 hours under normal circumstances and within 24
hours in emergency situations", start "within five business days". "Individual projects performed under this agreement must not
exceed $500,000.00". "The City does not guarantee any quantities of work". Term: 1 year + "four (4) additional one-year periods".
May award to multiple contractors.

**Due / method (quoted):** "Proposals must be received electronically in the Rocky Mountain E-Purchasing System no later than November
18, 2026 prior to 2:00 p.m. MT." "The City will only accept proposals submitted in the Rocky Mountain E-Purchasing System". One original
PDF (+ optional "FOR PUBLIC VIEWING" PDF). Contact: Michelle Davis, michelle.davis@longmontcolorado.gov, 303-651-8343.

**Pre-bid:** none in the RFP. Inquiry deadline "November 5, 2026 prior to 4:00 p.m."; final addendum "November 10, 2026".

**Licenses/certifications (quoted):** Criterion 1.e "Is your company listed and in good standing with the State's electrical board?
Provide a copy of the license for verification purposes." 1.f staff "including licensure (Journeyman or Master electrician)". GC 2.4.1:
"the Contractor will have a Contractor's License with the City by the time of Notice of Award ... Subcontractors shall also have a City
of Longmont Contractor's License". Colorado law (C.R.S. 12-115-103, "Electrical contractor" means any ... firm ... "THAT undertakes or
offers to undertake for another the planning, laying out, supervising, and installing ... of wiring apparatus and equipment for electric
light, heat, and power"; and C.R.S. 12-115-110(5)(a) "No person, firm ... shall engage in the business of an electrical contractor without
having first registered with the board", which under (5)(b)/(c) needs a master-licensed owner or "at least one licensed master
electrician" employee; source: Colorado Title 12 recodification text, content.leg.colorado.gov, article 115). No general-contractor exemption in the exemptions section.
This is my reading of the statute, not legal advice.

**Subcontracting (quoted):** allowed with City approval. RFP III.B.3: "There may be areas for use of subcontractors or partners ... The
City will contract solely with your company". GC 7.2.1 "The Contractor may utilize the services of specialty Subcontractors on those
parts of the Work which, under normal contracting practices, are performed by specialty Subcontractors"; 7.2.2 no subletting "until
approval of such action has been obtained from the City". No self-perform percentage. But on an electrical contract the electrical
work IS the whole job, and RFP IV.F.2 lets the sub bid against us: "A party quoting prices to a proposer is not thereby disqualified
from ... submitting a direct proposal on the proposer's own behalf."

**Bonding (quoted):** none at proposal. Task orders "$50,000 to $500,000": "Performance and payment bonds will be required from the
Contractor and must be submitted on the City's forms" (GC 5.1.1: each "in a penal sum equal to the amount of the Contract Price").
Retainage 5% over $150,000; "ten percent may be withheld" under $150,000.

**Insurance (quoted, RFP IV.G):** GL "at least $1,000,000 per each occurrence plus an additional amount adequate to pay related
attorney's fees and defense cost"; Auto "at least $1,000,000 per each occurrence"; Workers' Comp + "Employer's Liability with limits of at
least $500,000" (GC 5.3.1.3 says "$1,000,000 per occurrence", conflict); City additional insured; 1, 3 and 4 kept "for two additional
years". Livable wage "$26.18 per hour".

**Experience asked of the BIDDER (quoted):** "at least three projects/contracts of similar scope including wastewater treatment plants,
drinking water treatment plants, water distribution system pump stations, and/or other industrial process or manufacturing environments
within the last 5 years"; "Identify the year in which your company was established and began providing the services"; "Describe the
safety program for your firm, and attach a copy of your Experience Modification Rate (EMR) as well as the last three years of your OSHA
300 form"; mobile generators "3-phase, 480 V ... minimum of 180 kW within 2 hours of notification and 500 kW within 24 hours".

**Evaluation (quoted):** "Company and Personnel Qualifications (Weight: 40%)", "Approach to Scope of Work (Weight: 35%)", "Value/Cost of
Efforts (Weight: 25%)". Local 5% + small local 2.5% preference (needs Longmont sales tax license and a staffed location in city limits;
Brown Bison has neither).

**Call: NO.** The license gate is firm-level, and a sub cannot lend it to us: the RFP asks for OUR state electrical board license, and
state law requires the firm offering electrical work to be a registered electrical contractor. 40% of the score is company
qualifications (EMR, 3 years of OSHA 300 logs, year established), and Brown Bison has none of that history as an LLC formed May 2026.
Any Longmont industrial electrician we asked to price could bid direct and win on its own license. The excavation item (#10) is minor,
but the work is also water/wastewater-plant heavy. Reverse only if Luke wants to register Brown Bison as an electrical contractor by
hiring a master electrician.
