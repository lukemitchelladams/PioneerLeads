## Limitations on subcontracting: how Brown Bison + a small-business sub could comply
- **52.219-14 alone would be met.** It is not in the draft. For reference only (the FAR text, not this draft), its
  services rule is that the prime *"will not pay more than 50 percent of the amount paid by the Government for contract
  performance to subcontractors that are not similarly situated entities"*, and work a similarly situated entity further
  subcontracts counts toward that 50%. A sub that is small under NAICS 811310 is similarly situated, so dollars paid to it
  do not count against the cap; only large-business subs do. Here the large-business subs are the factory-certified PMs
  (York, Ingersoll Rand, Carrier, Liebert), possibly the water-treatment firm, a large Fort Collins sub, and a large Kauai
  sub: by the private estimate well under half of the price. The sub-outreach email asks each firm whether it is small
  under 811310.
- **The draft's own rules are stricter and decide it.** PWS 2 puts the whole 24/7 crew (project manager, project engineer,
  stationary engineers, HVAC mechanics) on Brown Bison's payroll, and Section L asks how we *"complete 51% of the contract
  tasks as the prime contractor"*. Compliance would mean Brown Bison hiring the plant crew itself (about 11 FTE by the
  private estimate, likely the incumbent's IUOE Local 1 staff under SCA section 4(c) CBA wages) and subbing only the
  specialty PM, Fort Collins and Kekaha work. A small-business sub's share still counts as ours under 52.219-14, but it
  cannot fill a key-personnel seat under PWS 2. Plus Factor 3 asks for the company's own 5-year plant record. That is the
  NO-BID.

- PWS 1.1: FFP lines for *"24/7/365 Operation and Maintenance Services at the Central Utility Plant (CUP) in Boulder,
  CO"*, *"Preventative and Scheduled Maintenance at the WWV/WWVB Field Site in Ft. Collins, CO"* and *"... at the
  WWV/WWVH Field Site in Kekaha, HI"*, plus T&M repair lines.
- PWS 3: the CUP (Bldg 42 and its tunnel) *"Shall provide steam, laboratory-grade compressed air, and chilled water to
  buildings 1, 2, 3, 24 and 81 and chilled water only to building 92"*; staffed 24/7/365; one stationary engineer always in
  Bldg 42 (3.1.5) and one HVAC mechanic always on the Boulder site (3.1.6); after-hours alarm response *"no more than 15
  minutes"* with *"4 hours of support per call"* (3.1.10).

  no PM), *"Four (4) York YZ 1500 ton capacity each"*, *"Four (4) Ingersoll Rand Air compressors 100 h.p. each"*, *"Four
  (4) Ingersoll Rand Desiccant air dryer systems"*. Attachment 1 also lists 3 steam boilers (Superior Boiler Works,
  BLR-C-017/018/019), 2 DA tanks, 4 CUP cooling towers, 4 emergency generators (1 Cummins, 3 Generac; PWS Att 11), heat

- 3.1.4: *"One boiler operating and one boiler warmed up, on standby."* *"There should never be more than one boiler, one
  chiller, one air compressor, or one air drier in need of repair or down for preventive maintenance at one time."*
- Chemicals are ours: all steam, condensate, DA, chilled, condenser and air-compressor loop treatment, testing every 8 hours
  (PWS 3.1.18, 3.2.1, 3.2.4; Monthly Report "Chemical Testing" tab).

  Annual 193 / 191.6, 4-year 5 / 1.25, 5-year 2 / 0.4. Nesting rule: *"only the longest-interval PM is performed"*.
