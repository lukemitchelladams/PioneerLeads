## Scope summary (Schedule B)

"Weld County is requesting cabling at the Weld County Southeast Weld facility in Fort Lupton, Colorado. The project includes a total of 235 Uniprise category 6 cables (Color BLUE), 24 strand Corning single mode fiber cable and associated new data closet build out including a 2-post rack and patch panels. All pathways are to be verified and provided by the contractor. The build out of one (1) new data closet, the extending of fiber from the existing MDF data closet in the basement to the new data closet on the 1st floor ... The project is considered an all-inclusive cabling turnkey solution." (Overview)

- **Copper (235 drops):** "CommScope CS37P Cat 6 Blue Cable UN874043014/10"; "Commscope High Density Modular Patch Panels P/N 760207282 / CPP-UDDM-M-2U-48"; "Commscope Jack 760237777 | UNJ600-BK" in the closet, "UNJ 600 GY" at the work area; faceplates M12L-262 / M14L-262; "All patch panel Jacks must be left populated even if a cable is not terminated"; "All cable runs will be home run from the distribution frame to each work area"; "Bridge taps and splices are not permitted"; "90 Meter maximum horizontal cable length"; "All terminations must be tested to 250 MHZ"; J-hooks / Arlington loops, sleeved pathways, core drills "should be included".
- **Patch cables (green):** 235 x 1 ft UNC6-GR-1F, 120 x 3 ft, 120 x 5 ft, 120 x 7 ft, 60 x 10 ft (655 total), "required prior to signoff".
- **Fiber:** "24 Strand Single Mode fiber cable as specified is required (Between data closets)"; Corning 024ZU4-T4F22D20; housings CCH-02U; panels CCH-CP12-A9; "All fiber must be Corning Fiber and all terminations will be LC connections"; "A slack loop of 25' shall be left in each Data Closet"; "Install, splice, terminate, and test"; test equipment "certified and calibrated within the previous 365 days"; testing "Corning certified for fiber" with company-owned equipment.
- **New 1st-floor data closet (West wing, Phase 1):** Chatsworth 7 ft 2-post rack 66353-703; 6" vertical cable managers 30095-703; 10" middle managers 30096-703; "2" metal 3 ring horizontal cable managers between each 48-port patch panels"; ladder rack, anchoring, fire caulking; Corning patch panel at the top of the rack.
- **PDUs:** "Four (4) additional Tripp Lite metered horizontal PDU's model PDUMH15-ISO are to be supplied. (NOT TO BE INSTALLED)".
- **Demolition:** "demolition and removal of all existing old low voltage networking cable that currently terminate in the basement MDF data closet"; Phase 2 (East wing) cables stay live until Phase 2 starts.
- **Deliverables:** "All cabling test results, and as-built engineering documents are to be provided in both physical hard copy and electronically 30 days from project completion"; labeling both ends "per the specified Weld County standard. This will be provided after the bid award"; one-year labor and materials warranty after acceptance.
- **Site:** work "can be completed during normal business hours ... Monday through Friday from 7 AM until 5:00 PM"; "Access into county facilities will be granted via escort and facility sign-in will be required"; staff badged and in company clothing; daily clean-up.
- **Quantities and routes come from the walk only:** "Copper cable locations and other requirements will be handed out at the mandatory pre-bid meeting"; "Building electrical diagrams will be provided at the mandatory walk-through"; "The Contractor is required and responsible for all measurements of all cables and other materials."

- "Performance bond is required. Contractor's Bond should be for one-year warranty."

## Risks / why NO
1. **No subcontracting.** Brown Bison's model (prime + certified cabling sub) is expressly barred: "sub-contracting the work will not be permitted"; project manager must be "a full-time employee of the award company". Same reason Douglas County 036-26 went NO.
2. **Company-level certifications Brown Bison does not hold:** Uniprise Certified Installer and Corning Certified Installer (company, "not just the individual"), BICSI-certified technician, 25-year Uniprise/Corning assurance warranty (only a certified installer can register it).
3. **100% performance and payment bonds** (Bond Total on the bid form); first bonded job.
4. Mandatory walk 11/19 10:00, full attendance with ID; pricing quantities exist only in the hand-outs from that walk.
5. Schedule C date/location conflict (1:00 PM, Greeley) vs the rest of the IFB (10:00 AM, Fort Lupton).
6. E&O policy required for three years after completion with no limit stated.
7. Weld County resident preference on competitive bids.
