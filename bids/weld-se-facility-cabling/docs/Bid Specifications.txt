 Schedule B - Scope of Work

Project Overview

Weld County is seeking bids for a vendor to provide Southeast Weld Facility Cabling.

Method of Procurement

Invitation for Bid (IFB): is a procurement method often referred to as a sealed bid solicitation. When issued, the bid package is considered complete for bidding purposes. Responsiveness to the solicitation’s terms and conditions are required. Incomplete or non-responsive bids will be eliminated from consideration. Low price is the key consideration by Weld County in awarding an IFB contract.

Pricing Method

Lump Sum Price: The lump sum price is the simplest and easiest price. Based on specifications, the contractor estimates their cost to provide the work, adds a profit margin, then proposes the sum as a price of the project. Lump Sum requires the proposals to include a fixed, not-to-exceed lump sum, which shall include all costs that may be paid to the contractor. The solicitation may include an itemized list of costs, in which case the proposals shall include itemized costs.

Specific Requirements and Responsibilities

The scope of this project is for Southeast Weld Facility Cabling.

Weld County is accepting bids from qualified contractors to furnish all labor, equipment, supplies, and materials necessary for the Weld County Southeast Weld facility cabling project as stated in this Invitation to Bid. Weld County will award the bid to the contractor/vendor with the best offering deemed most advantageous to the County, price/cost being the primary consideration. The determination of whether the bid is acceptable and meets the minimum requirements of the solicitation is solely within the discretion of the County.

Overview:

Weld County is requesting cabling at the Weld County Southeast Weld facility in Fort Lupton, Colorado. The project includes a total of 235 Uniprise category 6 cables (Color BLUE), 24 strand Corning single mode fiber cable and associated new data closet build out including a 2-post rack and patch panels. All pathways are to be verified and provided by the contractor. The build out of one (1) new data closet, the extending of fiber from the existing MDF data closet in the basement to the new data closet on the 1^(st) floor and installation of new fiber optics to this new IDF data closet from the existing MDF data closet. All fiber components including patch panels or the use of the existing patch panel or if required the installation of a new patch panel in the MDF must be included in the proposal. All fiber terminations shall be included. The new 1^(st) floor data closet requires one (1) 19’ rack, ladder rack, and two 6” cable managers on the sides of the rack. All other associated materials as required are to be included in this turnkey solution. This includes anchoring of the rack, ladder rack and all other parts and installation required. Additional patch panels and all other data closet equipment as specified are required to be installed. Patch cables (Color GREEN) at the specified amount in the details section are required prior to signoff. Specific details will be provided during the mandatory walk-through. The project is considered an all-inclusive cabling turnkey solution. Building electrical diagrams will be provided at the mandatory walk-through. The new data closet will be terminated with new patch panels. Four (4) additional Tripp Lite metered horizontal PDU’s model PDUMH15-ISO are to be supplied, but not to be installed.

The project will include the demolition and removal of all existing old low voltage networking cable that currently terminate in the basement MDF data closet. This will be reviewed with particulars detailed during the mandatory walkthrough.

The project will be conducted in 2 phases. Tentative timelines will be discussed at the mandatory walkthrough.

Phase 1 consists of cabling the WEST wing including the build out of the new 1^(st) floor data closet. This includes the fiber optics component which extends connectivity from the existing basement MDF data closet to this new 1^(st) floor data closet. Demolition and removal of existing cables for this phase will occur; however existing cables providing connectivity for locations included in Phase 2 must not be removed and left in place until phase 2 is kicked off.

Phase 2 consists of cabling the EAST wing. Demolition and removal of existing cables for this phase will occur.

New data closet build-outs. Build a new 1^(st) floor data closet in the WEST wing during the 1^(st) phase of the project. The data closet will consist of one (1) new 19” rack, associated ladder rack, and requested vertical cable managers. Rack will consist of 2” metal 3 ring horizontal cable managers between each 48-port patch panels. Two 6” cable managers on the end racks. Ladder rack will stabilize the rack and be installed as specified. Fiber optics from Main MDF data closet in the basement with corning patch panel to be placed at the top of the rack.

Contractors are to furnish all labor, equipment, supplies, and material as necessary for the copper, fiber, data closet and all other components of the installation project. The project is to include the installation of specified cabling per the identified materials in the specification sheet. The installation will use industry standards in addition to requirements listed in this bid. Access into county facilities will be granted via escort and facility sign-in will be required. All cabling test results, and as-built engineering documents are to be provided in both physical hard copy and electronically 30 days from project completion. There shall be a warranty for all materials and labor for a period of not less than one full year after the work has been accepted by the County. This in no way limits or reduces the warranty offered by any manufacturer of material or equipment used in maintenance or repairs.

All dry wall and drop ceiling penetrations are to be conducted by the contractor. Core drills if required will be performed by the contractor. Surface mounts will be provided and cabling in surface mounts will be pulled, terminated and tested by the contractor. J boxes, Arlington loops, and additional associated materials are to be installed by the contractor. Jack termination labelling per Weld County requirements is to be completed and test results provided prior to signoff. All modular RJ’s to fully populate a patch panel must be supplied, meaning if a jack is not punched down, the patch panel should not be left empty, and a RJ should be placed in all slots.
