                     DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS – Eastern Colorado Healthcare

                            Hydronic Radiant Heating Panels at RMR Entrances


                                    PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT
1. Background

       The Rocky Mountain Regional VA Medical Center (RMRVAMC) has hydronic radiant
       heating panels at all the main entrances that have corroded and failed creating potential slip
       hazards and not keeping up with the heating of the spaces at our Colfax entrance, South
       entrance, North entrance and employee breeze way coming from employee garage.

       The contractor shall remove and replace the existing failed radiant panels and shall comply
       with this Performance Work Statement (PWS), all VA, local, state, and Federal policies, and
       regulations.

2. Objective:

Radiant Heating Panels Replacement- One time request

          The objective of this service contract is to have a contractor to furnish all labor,
           materials, supplies, tools, equipment, and qualified personnel to remove all existing
           heating panels and replace for new approved like heating panels at all current locations.
3. Scope:

          Remove and dispose of failed heating panels – roughly 238’ of radiators
          Prep existing locations to ensure no failure points that may leak
          Install new heating panels at the same length and pressure as existing and connection
           points (see pictures below)
          Verify that required pressure is met
          Verify that there are no leaks on the new panels and connections
          Test and ensure full operation is restored
