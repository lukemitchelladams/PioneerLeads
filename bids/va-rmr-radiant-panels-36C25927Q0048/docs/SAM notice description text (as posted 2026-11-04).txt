 Description of Requirements for the items to be acquired; 

 DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS – Eastern Colorado Healthcare 

 Hydronic Radiant Heating Panels at RMR Entrances

 PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT

 1. Background 

 The Rocky Mountain Regional VA Medical Center (RMRVAMC) has hydronic radiant heating panels at all the main entrances that have corroded and failed creating potential slip hazards and not keeping up with the heating of the spaces at our Colfax entrance, South entrance, North entrance and employee breeze way coming from employee garage.

 The contractor shall remove and replace the existing failed radiant panels and shall comply with this Performance Work Statement (PWS), all VA, local, state, and Federal policies, and regulations.

 2. Objective: 

 Radiant Heating Panels Replacement- One time request

 The objective of this service contract is to have a contractor to furnish all labor, materials, supplies, tools, equipment, and qualified personnel to remove all existing heating panels and replace for new approved like heating panels at all current locations.

 3. Scope: 

 Remove and dispose of failed heating panels – roughly 238’ of radiators

 Prep existing locations to ensure no failure points that may leak

 Install new heating panels at the same length and pressure as existing and connection points (see pictures below)

 Verify that required pressure is met

 Verify that there are no leaks on the new panels and connections

 Test and ensure full operation is restored

 Locations pictured:

 4. Site Specific Equipment Information: 

 Model: PRF

 Radiators to be rate for low, medium and high pressure for a working pressure of 74 PSIG

 Radiators should be supplied with all mounting backets for noise free operation

 Radiators to be supplied with standard ½” female tapered NPT female connections and 1/8” female tapered NPT air vent connections

 Radiator expansion not to exceed 0.016 inches at 215°F for Piping allowance for expansion and contraction

 5. Contractor Qualifications and Responsibilities: 

 Contractors shall be qualified and has skilled employees with previous work experience in removal and installation of all hydronic heating panels

 Contractors must demonstrate having performed labor, start-up and commissioning of these units

 Contractor shall take all precautions necessary to protect people and property from injury or damage during the performance of this contract. Contractor shall be responsible for any injury to himself/herself, his/her employees, or others, as well as for any damage to personal or public property that occurs during the performance of this contract that is caused by him/her or his/her employees’ fault or negligence. Contractors shall maintain personal liability and property damage insurance prescribed by the laws of the Federal Government.

 The Government reserves the right to accept or reject Contractor’s employees for the rendering of services. Complaints concerning Contract Personnel’s performance or conduct will be dealt with by the Contractor and POC with the final decision being made by the Contracting Officer.

 6. Report Requirements: 

 Furnish report within one week of each space completion in “Microsoft Word” format by email to the FMS POC.

 7. Resources: 

 The Government will not furnish any resources for the fulfillment of the services required under this contract. The contractor must provide all tools, equipment, materials, transportation, professional expertise, personnel, and supervision for maintenance services required under this contract. The contractor shall remove all debris and trash from facility upon completion of work.

 Scheduling

 The work will be performed Monday through Friday from 7:30am to 4:00pm. The majority of the work will have minimal impact on the operation of the VA Medical Center.

 Any potential disruption to ongoing medical center operations shall be coordinated 2 weeks in advance and shall be subject to review and approval by the CO/POC.

 Any other disruption of operations in any building shall be scheduled at least one week in advance with the CO/POC. Work shall be scheduled to allow continuous operation of all facilities to the greatest extent possible.
