## Scope summary
- PWS sec. 1 (Desc p.2): panels "at all the main entrances that have corroded and failed creating potential slip hazards
  and not keeping up with the heating of the spaces at our Colfax entrance, South entrance, North entrance and employee
  breeze way coming from employee garage."
- PWS sec. 2: "furnish all labor, materials, supplies, tools, equipment, and qualified personnel to remove all existing
  heating panels and replace for new approved like heating panels at all current locations." The PWS calls it "this
  service contract".
- PWS sec. 3 (Desc p.2): "Remove and dispose of failed heating panels – roughly 238' of radiators"; "Prep existing
  locations to ensure no failure points that may leak"; "Install new heating panels at the same length and pressure as
  existing and connection points"; verify pressure, verify no leaks, "Test and ensure full operation is restored".
- Photo captions (Desc pp.2-4; images in `docs/photos/`): Cross Bridge North 88" "divided into 4 panels with 3 flow
  compartments"; North Entrance 730" "Multiple divisions with 2 flow compartments"; Chappel 1256" (same); South Entrance
  560" (same); Colfax Entrance 220" "divided into 4 panels with 3 flow compartments". Sum 2,854 in = 237.8 ft, which ties
  to "roughly 238'".
- PWS sec. 4 (Desc pp.4-5): "Model: PRF"; "Radiators to be rate for low, medium and high pressure for a working pressure
  of 74 PSIG"; "supplied with all mounting backets for noise free operation"; "standard ½" female tapered NPT female
  connections and 1/8" female tapered NPT air vent connections"; "Radiator expansion not to exceed 0.016 inches at 215°F".
  The wording matches the Sterling (Mestek) PRF panel radiator sheet (`reference/`, manufacturer literature, not part of
  the solicitation). No model number (tube count/height), BTU, color, grille or bracket type is given.
- Piping/valves/controls: only "connection points" and "Prep existing locations"; no valve, control or piping
  replacement is listed. Ceiling work: none mentioned. **Asbestos, ICRA/infection control: not mentioned anywhere.**
- Hours (PWS sec. 8, Desc p.5): "Monday through Friday from 7:30am to 4:00pm"; disruptions "coordinated 2 weeks in
  advance and shall be subject to review and approval by the CO/POC"; other building disruptions "at least one week in
  advance". **No after-hours work.** Check in/out daily "with the POC located in Building L (ENC)" (Desc p.6).
- Deliverable (PWS sec. 6): "Furnish report within one week of each space completion in "Microsoft Word" format by
  email to the FMS POC."
- Payment (PWS sec. 10): in arrears; "Payments also must be submitted through Tungsten to be paid".

## Key requirements (quoted, with section)
1. **Limitations on subcontracting (the deciding rule for our model).** FAR 52.219-14 is listed but **not marked**
   (Desc p.11). Instead the quote must include the signed VAAR 852.219-75 certificate, which has the **Services** box
   checked: "(i) [X] Services. In the case of a contract for services (except construction), the contractor will not pay
   more than 50% of the amount paid by the government to it to firms that are not certified SDVOSBs ... or certified
   VOSBs ... Other direct costs may be excluded to the extent they are not the principal purpose of the acquisition and
   small business concerns do not provide the service as set forth in 13 CFR 125.6." (VAAR attachment para. (a)(1)(i)).
   The form carries an 18 U.S.C. 1001 warning (para. (a)(2)) and "The Government will not consider offers for award from
   offerors that do not provide the certification" (para. (d)). Factor IV: failure to complete it means the quote "will
   be determined ineligible for award" (Desc p.9).
   - Mismatch: the form's wording is the SDVOSB/VOSB version, but this is a total small business set-aside. Under
     13 CFR 125.1 a "similarly situated entity" for "a small business set-aside" is "a subcontractor that is a small
     business concern" that is "small for the NAICS code" assigned to the subcontract. The CO asks for exactly that:
     "Offeror must list any/all subcontractors (and their size standard IAW NAICS 238220) ... AND what they will be
     performing. How is your firm going to comply with Limitations on Subcontracting requirements?" (Desc p.8).
   - Meaning for Brown Bison: at least 50% of what VA pays must stay with Brown Bison or go to small-business subs under
     $19.0M. A large mechanical sub is out. Panel material bought from a large manufacturer conservatively counts against
     the 50%, because the panels are arguably "the principal purpose of the acquisition".
2. **Technical (Factor I).** PWS sec. 5 (Desc p.5): "Contractors shall be qualified and has skilled employees with
   previous work experience in removal and installation of all hydronic heating panels"; "Contractors must demonstrate
   having performed labor, start-up and commissioning of these units". 52.212-2 addendum (Desc p.9): "Offers that do not
   meet or exceed the technical capability or quality ... shall not be selected regardless of price." "The Government
   reserves the right to accept or reject Contractor's employees" (PWS sec. 5).
3. **Past performance (Volume III, Desc p.8).** "a maximum of three (3) recent and relevant past efforts"; CPARS if
   available, else "contract numbers, amount of contract award, point of contact with email address, telephone numbers
   ... and a description"; relevant = "similar in nature of work, size, magnitude, complexity, and scope"; recent = "not
   more than five years". No record: "may not be evaluated favorably or unfavorably" (Desc p.9). Not disqualifying.
4. **Signature.** "This RFQ shall be completed in its entirety, and signed and dated, failure shall not be considered for
   award." (Desc p.1)
5. **Labor standards.** SCA WD 2015-5419 Rev 34 (WD p.3/7): 23410 HVAC Mechanic $34.38, 23790 Pipefitter, Maintenance
   $32.85, 23810 Plumber, Maintenance $30.92, 23470 Laborer $22.25; (WD p.5/7) "HEALTH & WELFARE: $5.92 per hour" ($5.42
   where EO 13706 applies), vacation 2 weeks after 1 year, 11 paid holidays. 52.222-62 Paid Sick Leave marked X (Desc p.12).
6. **Buy American.** 52.225-1 Buy American-Supplies marked X (Desc p.12): the panels must qualify as domestic end
   products.
7. **Flowdown.** PWS sec. 13 (Desc p.7): incorporate the clause "in all subcontracts under this contract, and require
   written subcontractor acknowledgement of same."
8. **Contracting vehicle statement.** "State if quoted items are available and priced through offerors existing
   Government-wide Acquisition Contract (GWAC), GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) contract, or VA-wide Acquisition
   Contract." (Desc p.7). Brown Bison: none.

Performance; and (IV) Completion of VAAR 852.219-75". "All information shall be confined to the appropriate file" (one
file per volume), all in one email under 4 MB.

  referenced in 1449", Desc p.10); no SF-1449 blocks to fill.
