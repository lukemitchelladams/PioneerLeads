## Scope summary (IFB pp.5-6)

The County builds a 2027 on-call list for 38 trades (p.6). Jobs are "small projects ranging from $10,000 to $50,000" (p.5),
though "Some jobs would be relatively small and entail an hour or two of work. Others might be more detailed or lengthy, such as
a small construction project." "All work locations are within La Plata County." Each job is quoted on request from the awarded
hourly rates plus estimated hours and materials, then authorized by a written work order (Exhibit D, PDF pp.34-36, not-to-exceed
amount, "Maximum amount per work order: $50,000"). Contractors must "respond to inquiries from County staff within
seventy-two (72) business hours (standard business hours of Mon-Fri. 8:00 am to 5:00 pm). This expectation does not obligate an
On-Call Contractor to accept the assignment" (p.5). After-hours work: "the employees assigned to the work will be subject to a
background check prior to being allowed on site after hours." (p.5). Prices exclude taxes: "La Plata County is exempt from all
state taxation including state sales and use tax." (p.4 item 6). Bids held "sixty (60) days after the date of the submittal
deadline" (p.4 item 4), i.e. through 2027-01-16.

## Key requirements (quoted, with source)

- Bid Sheet rates: "Complete the Proposed Services and Rates table below for Years 1 – 3. Do not exceed a 3% increase in rates
  from one year to the next." "You may submit more than one Services and Rates Sheet if necessary." (p.10). Columns: Trade/Service
  from List; Hourly Rate Year 1; Year 2; Year 3. No trip-charge or materials-markup field is stated in the solicitation.
- References: "Provide three (3) valid references for similar projects completed within the last three (3) years. (Note: do not
  provide La Plata County staff as a reference.)" Fields: Entity Name, Contact Name, Contact Email, Contact Phone (pp.11-12).
- Addenda: "Respondents shall acknowledge receipt of each Addendum in their submission ... Failure to acknowledge receipt of any
  Addendum issued may invalidate a submission as incomplete." (p.4 item 2). "Please include signed Addenda, if applicable, in your
  submittal." (p.12).
- Certifications on the Bid Sheet: conflict-of-interest disclosure (two initials), debarment (one initial) (p.12); bidder
  certification incl. "Bidder has the expertise, desire, licensing and means to provide the required product/services described
  herein" and signature (pp.12-13).
- CORA form: "Bids and proposals submitted without a duly executed CORA Agreement will be deemed incomplete and will not be
  evaluated or considered for award of the contract." (p.15). Three initials plus signature.
- Confidential marking: "Submittals marked confidential, proprietary or which otherwise propose to restrict or deny the public
  access ... may be disqualified or rejected by the County" (GC 6, p.7). Mark nothing confidential; rates become public.
- Subcontractors are contemplated: "All agreements between «ShortName» and any subcontractors shall contain sufficient terms to
  ensure subcontractor's compliance with all provisions of this Agreement. All agreements between «ShortName» and any
  subcontractors shall specifically note that the County is a third-party beneficiary to each agreement" (Agreement sec. 23,
  PDF p.27). Also p.9 (subcontractors' workers' comp), sec. 11 (audit of "any subcontractor's" records), sec. 13 (indemnity covers
  "independent subcontractors").
- Licenses: "«ShortName» shall be responsible for obtaining all licenses and permits necessary to perform the Services, at
  «ShortName»'s expense" (Agreement sec. 9, PDF p.21). The only trade license named in the IFB is "2. Arborist – Licensed in La
  Plata County" (p.6).
- Payment: monthly, "subject to Contractor providing County with an itemized statement of Services", "by checks payable to the
  trade or business name of Contractor and not to any individual" (Exhibit B, PDF p.31).
