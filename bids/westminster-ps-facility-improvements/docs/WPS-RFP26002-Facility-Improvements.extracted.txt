Section 1: Introduction and Overview 
 
District Profile 
Westminster Public Schools is a Colorado public school district serving students and families 
throughout portions of Adams County in the Denver metropolitan area.  The District covers a 
geographical area of 17.5 square miles and serves the communities of Westminster, Arvada, 
Denver, and Federal Heights, along with certain unincorporated areas of Adams County.  The 
District consists of 17 schools serving approximately 7,500 students and employs approximately 
900 staff members.  Westminster Public Schools is recognized for its innovative approach to  
personalized learning and operates the largest districtwide Competency  B ased System in the 
nation.   
 
Purpose 
Westminster Public Schools (the District) is soliciting proposals from qualified general contractors 
to provide construction services for the District’s Summer 2027 Facility Improvements at multiple 
school locations.  The Work generally consists of renovation, repair, replacement, and facility 
improvement projects and includes, as applicable, electrical improvements; classroom casework 
and associated plumbing; flooring replacement; window replacement; lighting improvements; 
Voice Lift and technology- related improvements; waterproofing and exterio r improvements; 
classroom modifications; drinking fountain replacement; masonry improvements; and other 
associated construction Work as shown and specified in the applicable Contract Documents. 
 
The Work is divided into six (6) separate Project Packages by school/location.  Respondents may 
submit proposals for one (1), multiple, or all Project Packages in accordance with the requirements 
of this solicitation.  The Work shall be performed in accordance with the Drawings, Specifications, 
WPS General Conditions of the Construction Agreement, addenda, and other Contract Documents 
applicable to each Project Package.  Certain additional design information, including Civil 
documents for identified Project  Packages, is anticipated to be issued by addendum during the 
solicitation period as further described in Section 3.

Project Package Proposal and Award Structure 
Respondents may submit a proposal for any one (1), multiple, or all Project Packages.  A 
Respondent is not required to submit a proposal for every Project Package in order to be considered 
for award.  Each Project Package shall be separately priced and may be separately evaluated and 
awarded.  When a Respondent proposes on multiple Project Packages, the District may 
additionally evaluate the Respondent’s demonstrated staffing, resources, capacity, scheduling, and 
ability to successfully perform the proposed Project Packages concurrently.  
 
The District reserves the right to award any Project Package individually or to award multiple 
Project Packages to the same Respondent; make awards to one or multiple Respondents; accept or 
reject any Alternate; and determine the award or combination of awards determined to be the most 
advantageous to the District based upon the evaluation criteria established in this solicitation.  
 
Award of one Project Package shall not entitle a Respondent to award of any other Project Package.  
The District is under no obligation to award all Project Packages included in this solicitation.  
 
Contingent Project Package – Fairview PK-8 
Package E – Fairview PK-8 is included in this solicitation for competitive pricing and evaluation 
purposes; however, the District has not made a final determination to proceed with this Project 
Package.  Any award of Package E is contingent upon availability of funding, required District 
approvals, and the District's determination to authorize the Work.  The District reserves the right 
to award or decline to award Package E independently of the other Project Packages.   No 
Respondent shall be entitled to compensation or reimbursement of proposal costs if the District  
elects not to proceed with Package E.   If the District has not authorized an award of Package E 
within the established proposal validity period, the District may request a written extension of the 
applicable proposal and pricing validity, subject to the Respondent’s agreement and applicable 
procurement requirements.

Project Schedule 
The Work is required to be performed during the District's summer 2027 construction period.  The 
District anticipates on -site construction beginning on or about May 28, 2027, subject to 
coordination of building access, furniture and equipment relocation, District-provided abatement, 
and other preconstruction and site-readiness activities.  
 
All awarded Project Packages shall achieve Substantial Completion no later than July 23, 2027,  
unless otherwise modified in writing by the District.  For purposes of this Project, the July 23, 
2027 Substantial Completion date is intended to provide the District sufficient time to begin 
returning furniture, equipment, instructional materials, and other District property to the buildings 
and preparing the facilities for the 2027-2028 school year. 
 
The precise commencement date and sequencing of Work may vary by Project Package based 
upon building availability, furniture relocation, regulated-material abatement, existing conditions, 
and coordination with District operations and separate contractors.  Detailed sequencing will be 
developed with the awarded Contractor following award. 
 
Respondents shall plan procurement, submittals, fabrication, mobilization, construction, 
inspections, testing, final cleaning, punch- list activities, and closeout to achieve the required 
completion dates.  Respondents shall identify anticipated long- lead materials and equipment and 
describe the actions proposed to ensure timely procurement.  Respondents proposing on multiple 
Project Packages shall demonstrate their ability to concurrently perform the proposed Work and 
shall identify the personnel, subcontractors, supervision, equipment, and other resources proposed 
for each location.

3.10 Regulated-Material Abatement and Coordination 
Regulated-material abatement required in connection with the Work will be performed by a 
separate contractor retained by the District unless expressly identified otherwise in the Contract 
Documents.  The Contractor shall not include the cost of District -provided abatement in its 
Contract Sum. 
 
Anticipated abatement may include, as applicable to the Project Package, materials associated with 
window replacement, existing flooring, pipe insulation, casework removal, and other materials 
encountered or identified in connection with the Work. 
 
The sequencing of abatement and construction activities has not been predetermined and may vary 
by Project Package, location, and existing conditions.  Following award, the Contractor shall work 
collaboratively with the District, Design Professional, and D istrict's abatement contractor to 
develop and maintain an appropriate sequencing and coordination plan for the Work.  Depending 
upon the conditions and requirements of a particular location, abatement may occur before 
Contractor activities, after initial C ontractor demolition or enabling Work, between phases of 
Contractor Work, or in another sequence approved by the District. 
 
The Contractor shall incorporate District -provided abatement activities and associated access, 
clearance, sequencing, and coordination requirements into its construction schedule and shall 
provide reasonable cooperation necessary to facilitate such Work.

3.11 Project Coordination and Site Operations 
The Contractor shall coordinate all construction activities with the District's Project Manager and 
Design Professional and shall perform the Work in a manner that protects District property and 
supports timely reopening of District facilities for the 2027-2028 school year. 
 
Contractor access, staging, deliveries, material storage, temporary facilities, site security, 
construction fencing, utility interruptions, shutdowns, abatement coordination, and other site 
logistics shall be coordinated with and subject to approval by the District. 
 
When awarded more than one Project Package, the Contractor shall provide sufficient supervision, 
staffing, subcontractor capacity, equipment, and other resources to perform the awarded Work 
concurrently without compromising the required completion date of any Project Package. 
 
3.12 Protection of Existing and Completed Work 
The Contractor shall protect existing District property and all existing and newly completed 
finishes, flooring, casework, fixtures, equipment, and other surfaces from damage resulting from 
construction activities, personnel, equipment, tools, material movement, deliveries, debris, and 
other operations associated with the Work.  The Contractor shall determine and provide appropriate 
temporary protection necessary for its means, methods, sequencing, and operations.  The District 
does not require a specific f loor-protection product or system unless otherwise identified in the 
Contract Documents; however, the absence or inadequacy of temporary protection shall not relieve 
the Contractor of responsibility for damage. 
 
Existing or newly installed flooring and other finished surfaces damaged, scratched, stained, 
gouged, chipped, broken, or otherwise adversely affected by the Contractor, its Subcontractors, 
suppliers, employees, equipment, or operations shall be repaired, refinished, or replaced by the 
Contractor, as appropriate, to restore the affected material to a condition acceptable to the District 
and Design Professional. 
 
When repair or refinishing will not restore the affected material to an acceptable condition or 
would result in a visibly inconsistent finish, the Contractor shall replace the damaged material at 
no additional cost to the District.  Required repair or replacement shall be completed promptly and 
shall not delay Substantial Completion, District furniture and equipment move-in, building setup, 
or the opening of school.
