## Scope summary (Exhibit 4 Statement of Work, pp.64-86)

"The City of Colorado Springs is seeking an experienced and qualified vendor to deliver quality professional janitorial
services to the CAB, City Hall, Municipal Court, and the Center for Sustainability offices." (p.64). Building list (pp.64-65):
City Hall 107 N Nevada; City Administration Building (CAB) 30 S Nevada; Municipal Court 224 E Kiowa; "Fire Department –
Various Addresses"; Police Operations Center 705 S Nevada; POC Annex 224 E Rio Grande; POC Parking Garage 720 S Weber;
Stetson Hills Substation 4110 Tutt Blvd; Falcon Substation 7850 Goddard; SED 4125 Center Park Dr; Impound Lot 2725 E Las
Vegas; Airport Hangar 950 Aviation Way; Training Academy 725 N Murray; Gold Hill Substation 955 W Moreno; Quartermaster Unit
1050 S Academy Blvd Ste 124; "Leased site ( E. Bijou )"; Sand Creek 950 Academy Park Loop.

Sizes (estimates, p.65-67): CAB 136,312 sf / 18 restrooms; City Hall 32,550 sf / 7; Municipal Court 90,000 sf / 22; POC
133,000 sf, 336 daily headcount, 6 holding cells; Sand Creek 37,800 sf; Gold Hill 33,000; Training Center 31,200; Stetson
Hills 31,000; Leased Site 19,932; SED 17,600; Falcon 17,500; Hangar 14,000; Quartermaster 3,906; Impound 2,188; POC Garage
1,200 (elevator, 4 levels of stairs); POC Annex 935; fire: FDC 43,500 sf (cleanable area printed "??"), FDCW 10,668, CPH 4,200.

Days of service (p.79): CAB, City Hall, Municipal Court "Five days per week"; Falcon, Sand Creek, Stetson Hills, Gold Hill
substations and Police Operation Center "Seven days per week"; Police Training Center "Five days per week"; FDC, FDW, Police
Annex, Hangar, Supply Building, SED "Two days per week, Tuesday and Thursday"; Fire Downtown "One day per week, Tuesday";
Impound Lot and Leased Site "Three days per week: Monday, Wednesday, Friday". "There are some sites that will require service
365 days per year." Hours: "between the hours of 1:00 P.M. and 10:00 P.M. seven days per week unless otherwise noted" (p.78);
secure areas close earlier (Crime Lab before 4:00 p.m.; SED before 3:00 p.m.; "Supply Building-1-3 p.m. only"; "Leased Site-
8 a.m. to noon", pp.79-80). Holidays: "the Contractor is required to continue cleaning tasks as regularly scheduled" (p.79).
Quarterly tasks "first week of January, April, July, and October"; semi-annual April and October; annual October (p.78).

As-needed and emergency: "The Contractor shall provide additional services on an as-needed basis" and "emergency call-out
services as-needed" (p.68). "After hours and on weekend responses to emergencies shall not exceed two (2) hours from
notification", with "a reasonable inventory placed at strategic location of wet/dry vacuums, extractors, floor fans,
extension cords, brooms, squeegees, etc." (pp.82-83). Call-out covers overflowed fixtures, pathogen spills, muddy/wet
entrances, broken glass (p.83).

Other duties: "certified green seal chemicals and HEPA vacuums" (p.68); consumables: Municipal Court supplies furnished by the
Contractor (toilet tissue, hand soap, paper towels, sanitary products, all trash can liners, air fresheners), City buys
supplies for City Hall, CAB and Center for Sustainability (p.84); police and fire sites are not named either way (not stated
in the RFP), while the general rule is "The Contractor shall furnish all necessary equipment, material, and supplies required
in performing services of the Contract except as listed in the attached Exhibits" (p.84) and "The Contractor shall acquire and
dispense City approved consumables" (p.68 C); the City pays only for consumables "mutually inventoried and accepted" and not more
than "three months of total inventory usage" (p.78); monthly QA inspections with the vendor's "manager or site
supervisor" and "Bi-Weekly Quality Assurance Inspection Forms" (p.81); deductions for non-performance (daily pro-rated;
weekly 10%; monthly 10%; periodic 5%; quality inspections 5%; second offense adds an administration fee "not to exceed $50 per
occurrence", pp.83-84); bloodborne-pathogen program (29 CFR 1910.1030, Exposure Control Plan within 15 working days, BBP
training, cleanup kits, p.81); relief staff "at no cost to the City" (p.83).

## Risks and gaps (RFP silent or inconsistent; Q&A is closed, so each is an assumption stated in the response)

1. **No live sub.** Sergeant Suds and Environment Control were both stood down 2026-11-03 (Gmail thread 1a183668e7d49de9)
   and neither was re-engaged; the other five 10/28 firms never replied. No price = no bid.
2. **Insurance at award:** $1M per-loss employee crime with third-party fidelity extension, CGL master-key and
   care-custody-control endorsements (p.39). Nothing is bound as of this pre-build (CLAUDE.md: nothing is bought before Fri
   11/13); only Luke can say what the policies he buys will cover, and HQ does not contact the broker. Exceptions are
   scored Pass/Fail (p.62). Contract §4 (p.43) also mentions "Professional Errors and Omissions coverage" but p.39 states no
   E&O limit (not stated in the RFP).
3. **Pricing format not specified:** Appendix A is only "on company letterhead" (p.93). Price Area requires "a detailed
   breakdown of the price for each year of performance" and, for T&M, "labor categories, labor rates, separated profit, and
   estimated material costs" (p.13). The deduction clause works off a "monthly invoice" (pp.83-84). Profit must be shown
   separately, so the price sheet discloses our margin to the City.
4. **Materials at cost:** "no additional profit, fee, overhead, handling, or General and Administrative (G&A) costs" (p.54).
   Margin can only sit in labor rates. City sales and use tax "should be included in all proposals" (p.8).
5. **No overtime:** "In no event shall the City be responsible for overtime pay." (p.51); holidays cleaned as scheduled
   (p.79; a holiday premium is not stated in the RFP); relief staff "at no cost to the City" (p.83).
6. **Documents referenced but not in the package:** the "frequency schedule" (p.68 I), "Consumable History table" (p.78),
   "attached Quality Inspection Form" (p.81), "Exposure Control Plan. (Appendix C)" (p.81; Section VI lists only Appendix A,
   p.92), a bloodborne-pathogen "Form shall be signed by contractor and submitted with RFP response or proposal" (p.81, no form
   attached), and a "Liquidated Damages Clause in the contract" (p.82; the sample contract has none).
7. **Site list inconsistencies:** Center for Sustainability is in the Purpose (p.64) and supply clause (p.84) but not in the
   building list or days of service; "Fire Department – Various Addresses" (p.64) vs. FDC/FDCW/CPH (p.67) vs. FDC/FDW/
   Downtown (p.79); "Quartermaster Unit" (p.65) vs. "Supply Building" (p.79); FDC cleanable area "??" (p.67). Pricing assumes
   the p.79 day schedule and the p.65-67 square footage.
8. **Experience:** Brown Bison Holdings LLC was formed 2026-05-04; Exhibit 1 asks years in business, three similar projects
   in the last five years, three current projects, and a bank reference (pp.31-36). References must come from the sub, labeled
   as the sub's. Luke's prior PM roles go only in his resume, never as Brown Bison contracts.
9. **Exhibit 1 checkboxes the City left blank:** financial statements required / not required (p.23) and additional
   qualification requirements (p.37) are both unchecked in the posted PDF. 2.4 says financial information "may be requested
   during the proposal evaluation" (p.10). Default: do not include financials; state they are available on request.
10. **No appointments under Luke's name:** monthly QA inspections are attended by the vendor's "manager or site supervisor"
    (p.81) and daily coordination "Before 4:00 P.M." with the CAB/OCH Facility Supervisor (p.78) is the sub's site supervisor.
    The response draft never commits Luke to a meeting or walk.
11. **Consumables at police and fire sites are unassigned** (not stated in the RFP): p.84 names only Municipal Court
    (Contractor) and City Hall, CAB, Center for Sustainability (City), but its general rule and p.68 C put supplies on the
    Contractor. The draft prices police/fire consumables as a separate at-cost materials line, used only if the City asks.
    Bulk orders are at our risk: the City pays only for consumables "mutually inventoried and accepted", capped at "three
    months of total inventory usage" (p.78).
12. **Interviews "TBD"** (p.4) and oral presentations or best and final offers at the committee's choice (pp.16-17). An
    interview is a meeting: HQ does not accept one under Luke's name; it goes to Luke as a decision with a brief, and the
    sub's operations manager can attend.
13. **City documents referenced by link, not attached:** City Contractor Safety Program (Rep. & Cert. #11, p.29) and
    Environmentally Preferred Purchasing policy (#12, p.29), both at coloradosprings.gov/finance/page/procurement-regulations-
    and-documents; Procurement Rules and Regulations (1.17, p.8, "can be reviewed and/or downloaded from the City website
    https://www.coloradosprings.gov"). Download into docs/ before Luke initials #11 and #12. Not in hand yet.
