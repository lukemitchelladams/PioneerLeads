### 3.B Project approach (2.5.1.B, p.11: "The Offeror must at least address the following areas")

**B.1 Schedule management.** A master calendar for all sites (days per p.79, windows per pp.78-80, periodic weeks per p.78),
issued to the City before the January start and updated monthly. Daily: site supervisor confirms every scheduled site was
cleaned (sign-in book, crew checklist). Weekly: Brown Bison reviews the completion report against the calendar; any miss is
re-worked inside 24 hours and reported in writing. Holidays: "continue cleaning tasks as regularly scheduled" (p.79). Weather:
"every reasonable effort ... regardless of snow"; if the City closes a facility, service resumes "the next day the facility is
open" (p.83).

**B.2 Quality control.** Three layers. (1) Crew checklist per site per shift, signed by the crew lead. (2) Site supervisor
inspection of every five- and seven-day site at least weekly and every other site every two weeks, scored on the City's 1-5
QA scale (p.82) [SUB: confirm frequency]. (3) Brown Bison review of every inspection and every Bi-Weekly Quality Assurance
Inspection Form before it goes to the City (p.81: forms "are to be emailed to the Contracting Specialist or Facilities
Representative"). Any score of 2 gets a written corrective action within 24 hours; the target is 4 ("Target") or better. The
vendor's "manager or site supervisor" joins the City's monthly QA inspections at each location (p.81): that is [SUB: site
supervisor name]. Deductions for non-performance (pp.83-84) are tracked by us so the monthly invoice already reflects any
missed service.

**B.3 Safety.** OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard 29 CFR 1910.1030 (p.81): written Exposure Control Plan delivered "within
the first fifteen (15) working days"; BBP training for every assigned employee before the first shift, records kept; BBP
cleanup kits at every site; Hazard Communication Program with named contacts (p.81). PPE per task; wet-floor signs at every
mopped area; no high-pressure cleaning or machine scrubbing "on a regular basis" in restrooms (p.70); areas exposed to bodily
fluids or bed bugs cleaned with the right method and PPE (p.68 O). Conduct rules: no alcohol, drugs or firearms on City
premises (p.80, p.85); courtesy to staff and public (p.80). Safety of City staff and public: work zones marked; cords taped;
furniture returned to position (p.68 M). [SUB: safety program summary, training records, EMR if any.] Brown Bison and the sub
