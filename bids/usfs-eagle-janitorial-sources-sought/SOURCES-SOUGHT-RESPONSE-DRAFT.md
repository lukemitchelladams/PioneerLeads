**6. Set-aside recommendation: Total Small Business Set-Aside**
Rationale:
1. Janitorial firms in the Eagle Valley are mostly small, so two or more responsible small businesses should offer at

2. The labor market is tight. A narrower set-aside (WOSB, HUBZone, SDVOSB or 8(a)) would cut the number of offerors
   who can actually staff an Eagle site, which raises the risk of missed service and higher prices.
3. A Total Small Business set-aside keeps every small-business category eligible to compete, including the ones listed
   in RFI section 3.
