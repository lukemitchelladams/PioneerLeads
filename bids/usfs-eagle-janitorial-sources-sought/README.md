## Scope (quoted, notice sec. 2)
*"routine and periodic janitorial services for a federal facility located in Eagle, Colorado. Services may include, but
are not limited to: Daily and weekly custodial cleaning; Restroom sanitation and replenishment; Trash collection and
disposal; Floor maintenance; Carpet cleaning; Window cleaning; High-touch surface disinfection; Supply management;
Emergency cleaning services. The anticipated service area is Eagle County, Colorado."* Building size, frequency and
period are not stated.

## What the response must cover (notice sec. 4-5)
- Geographic: *"Physical office location(s)"*, *"Service coverage within Eagle County"*, *"Distance from Eagle,
  Colorado"*, *"Existing contracts currently performed in western Colorado or mountain communities"*, *"Ability to
  recruit and retain janitorial personnel in the Eagle Valley region."*
- Company information: name, UEI, CAGE, address, POC, telephone, email, *"Business size status under NAICS 561720"*.
- Socioeconomic status checkboxes.
- Experience: *"at least three similar contracts performed within the last five years"* with customer, contract
  number, period, annual dollar value, scope, location.
- Staffing: *"Number of current employees"*, *"Availability of staff within 50 miles of Eagle, Colorado"*, recruitment
  strategy, management approach.
- Set-aside recommendation with rationale.
