
1. Purpose
The Government is conducting market research to identify qualified small business concerns capable 
of providing comprehensive janitorial services in Eagle, Colorado. The information collected will 
be used to determine the availability and capability of small businesses and specific socioeconomic 
categories for a potential set-aside acquisition.
This RFI is issued solely for planning and market research purposes and does not constitute a 
solicitation, request for proposal (RFP), invitation for bid (IFB), or a commitment by the 
Government to award a contract.
2. Background
The Government is seeking information from contractors capable of providing routine and periodic 
janitorial services for a federal facility located in Eagle, Colorado. Services may include, but 
are not limited to:
•  Daily and weekly custodial cleaning
•  Restroom sanitation and replenishment
•  Trash collection and disposal
•  Floor maintenance
•  Carpet cleaning
•  Window cleaning
•  High-touch surface disinfection
•  Supply management
•  Emergency cleaning services
The anticipated service area is Eagle County, Colorado.
3. Socioeconomic Categories of Interest
The Government is particularly interested in responses from firms in the following categories:
•  Small Business (SB)
•  Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB)
•  Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB)
•  Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB)
•  Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB)
•  HUBZone Small Business
•  Small Disadvantaged Business (SDB)
•  SBA Certified 8(a) Business Development Program Participants
Contractors should indicate all applicable socioeconomic certifications and provide supporting 
documentation if available.
4. Geographic Capability
Respondents should address:
1. Physical office location(s).
2. Service coverage within Eagle County and surrounding areas.
3. Distance from Eagle, Colorado.
4. Existing contracts currently performed in western Colorado or mountain communities.
5. Ability to recruit and retain janitorial personnel in the Eagle Valley region.
