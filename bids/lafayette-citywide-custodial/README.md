## Key requirements (quoted)

- **Supplies, two conflicting statements.** Appendix B: "Through the City of Lafayette's approved process, The City of Lafayette will furnish supplies to carry out the typical cleaning processes and to fill typical dispensers. The CONTRACTOR will provide administrative time to create orders through The City of Lafayette approved ordering platform" (HD Supply quotes approved and paid by the Superintendent of Facilities). RFP sec. 3 criterion 1: "Pricing must also include all equipment, chemicals, consumables, and personal protective equipment." Ask which governs before pricing (Q5).
- **Equipment:** "The CONTRACTOR will furnish all equipment for accomplishment of all work ... shall operate from existing sources of electrical power and shall have low noise level of operations." "All consumable supplies must conform to manufacturer requirements as defined in the O&Ms. See Exhibit C." (Appendix B 1-05; Exhibit C is not posted, Q6.) "Any item with potentially flammable or otherwise harmful qualities shall not be used."
- **Staff and security:** "CONTRACTOR's employees must have security background checks and be approved by The City of Lafayette prior to working on-site." "no person shall be employed whose loyalty to the United States is questionable." PD: CJIS background inspection for everyone inside. Evaluators score "the vendor's process for conducting security background checks and submitting employees for approval by the City before they work on-site", "employee training on confidentiality, key control, and access control".
- **Supervision:** "The CONTRACTOR shall at all times during hours specified for service, provide a CONTRACTOR janitorial supervisor who can efficiently and effectively communicate, in written and verbal forms, with both The City of Lafayette and to their subordinate janitorial staff." Reachable 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.; monthly meeting with the City's contract point of contact; "Within 24 hours, bring to Company's attention any issues that could affect the timing or delivery of Services".
- **Inspection:** City staff log deficiencies in Asset Essentials; "CONTRACTOR is responsible to complete approved work requests through this ERP to include calling the work request 'complete'". Frontline staff "will not work to the direction of anyone other than the CONTRACTOR'S supervisors."
- **Safety and housekeeping:** PPE per OSHA 1910.132 at contractor cost; wet floor signage and barricades; combustible rags not stored in the building; "Janitorial staff shall be responsible to turn off interior lights after the conclusion of their nightly operations"; lost articles turned in to the location's Manager of Operations; City not liable for contractor equipment stored in janitor closets.
- **Periodic work methods** are defined (hot water extraction at ~180 degrees F with IICRC practice, bonnet, or rotary shampoo; floor scrubbing followed by one coat of finish; stripping then two coats; high dusting above six feet).
- **Subcontracting:** allowed in substance. The staffing plan must detail "all personnel and subconsultants proposed for assignment to the Project team"; the City "reserves the right to obtain financial data or other supplemental information concerning the firm and/or its subcontractors"; workers' comp flows down to subcontractors (RFP sec. 2; Appendix A sec. 8). Nothing in the RFP or sample contract bars a janitorial sub. Assignment of the contract needs written consent (Appendix A sec. 13). Confirm the sub model openly at the conference (Q8).
- **Contract exceptions:** "Any exceptions to this RFP or the City of Lafayette Sample Contract (Attachment A), no matter how slight, must be detailed and provided as a redlined document. Significant exceptions may make your proposal nonresponsive." Keep Jobs in Colorado Act applies (Appendix A sec. 11). Protests within seven working days to the Finance Department.

## Risks / open questions
1. **Mandatory conference 2026-11-19 10:00.** Nobody from Brown Bison = no bid. Put it on Luke's calendar now; take the sub along.
2. **Experience scoring (20 points)** wants "comparable custodial services across multiple municipal or public sector facilities", "secure facilities such as police stations, day porter programs", and "at least three verifiable references for contracts of similar size and complexity". Brown Bison has none in janitorial; the sub's municipal references and staff carry this section. Pick a sub that already cleans a city or county building.
3. **Labor scale.** Three 7-day buildings (Rec Center, Library, Golf) plus day porters at two of them, four park restrooms 7x/week, PD 5x/week with a daytime secured-area crew, Senior Center and City Hall 5x/week. Rough count: 6-10 cleaners plus porters and a working supervisor present "during all service hours". "Proposals with labor hours too low to perform the Master Cleaning Schedule ... will score lower."
4. **Supplies conflict** (Appendix B: City furnishes; RFP criterion 1: price includes consumables). Pricing swings by thousands a year; get the written answer by 11/27.
5. **No square footage** for 12 of 13 buildings; the 11/19 walk and Q3 are the only sources before pricing.
6. **Loss run and OSHA 300A** are required submittals; Brown Bison's and the sub's.
7. **CJIS** background inspection for every person entering the PD; turnover means re-clearing staff.
8. **Interviews week of 12/28** (between Christmas and New Year); Luke should be available.
9. The waterpark line is seasonal (Memorial Day-Labor Day) but Appendix C prices it "Per Specifications" like the others; ask how to show it (Q11).
