Section 1 - PROJECT DETAILS  
A. BACKGROUND:   
The City’s portfolio includes approximately 38 structures averaging 75 years in age, all of which 
are in various conditions and feature some degree of antiquated equipment. As such, the City 
has a need for on-call plumbing maintenance and repair services to be performed on a scheduled 
and unscheduled basis, for preventative, remedial, and emergency work across all City-owned 
facilities. Facilities owned by the City include, but are not limited to: City Center, Police Station, 
Fire Station, Bellview Service Center, Littleton  Museum, Bemis Library, Littleton Municipal 
Courthouse, and some residential buildings. 
 
The City seeks to establish a group of qualified on-call plumbing contractors who will be 
responsible for delivering professional, Code-compliant services on a continuing on-call basis.  
 
B. WORK ISSUANCE :  
The City anticipates awarding a two (2) year contract (with an option to renew for one additional 
year at the City’s sole discretion) to multiple contractors, with work issued on an as-needed basis. 
The contractors’ compensation will vary depending upon the number of contracts awarded. No 
minimum amount of work is guaranteed. The City’s anticipated budget for all services combined 
is $100,000 per year.  
 
When services are needed, documents describing the nature of the work will be issued to the 
pool of on-call contractors for preparation of quotes. The selected on-call contractor will receive 
a Work Order specifying a scope of work, cost, and timing expectations as the need arises.  
 
A Performance and Payment Bond shall be furnished covering all Work Orders performed 
hereunder. An initial bond in the amount of $100,000.00 shall be provided at the time of contract 
execution. In the event the dollar amount of Work to be performed exceeds this amount on any 
given Work Order, the Contractor shall provide properly executed bond Change Riders, in the 
amount(s) not less than 100% of all Work Orders issued.  
 
The City aims to ensure that work is distributed equitably among all awarded contractors during 
the term of the contract. It is anticipated that work will be assigned on a rotational basis, and/or 
by requesting quotes from all contractors for work t hat is over a specific project cost threshold. 
For emergency services, work will be distributed on a rotational basis based on the contractor’s 
immediate availability and spend capacity remaining on their on-call contract, regardless of 
project cost. The City reserves the right to request quotes or services from multiple contractors, 
or from a single contractor, based on the City’s need.

Section 2 - SCOPE OF WORK 
A. SERVICES: 
The Contractor shall provide services to support the plumbing system functional operation, 
maintenance, repair, and/or replacement at City-owned facilities, including testing, 
verification, and diagnostics. 
 
Non-Emergency Work : Scheduled and unscheduled services shall be performed during 
normal business hours of Monday through Friday 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM. 
 
Emergency Work : Emergency services shall be performed at the City’s request. The 
Contractor shall be available to provide services “on call” on a 24/7 basis for any emergency 
that may occur, including weekends and holidays. The Contractor shall respond to an 
emergency within two (2) hours of notification by the City. 
 
Preventative and remedial maintenance and emergency repair services include, but are not 
limited to: 
o Assessment and Evaluation of Plumbing Systems 
 Assess and evaluate plumbing systems to determine if systems are 
serviceable, including identification of obstructions, leaks, failures, or other 
areas of concern within internal and external plumbing infrastructure. 
• Assessment and evaluation shall include video scoping and inspection 
of plumbing lines to identify blockages, deterioration, or structural 
issues. 
 Ensure City plumbing systems are operational and within manufacturer 
specifications and applicable safety codes. 
 Identify and develop a report detailing the Contractor’s assessment and 
evaluation of the plumbing systems, detailing any maintenance and/or repairs 
recommended, documenting deficiencies and upgrade recommendations, and 
adding any manufacturer specific items that may not be addressed. 
• The service report shall include corrective actions beyond preventative 
maintenance, recommendations that will improve and/or lower 
maintenance and operation costs, and estimated costs per 
recommendation  
 
o Preventative Maintenance 
 Inspect and provide preventative services on plumbing systems and 
components including valves, piping, drainage, and fixtures on a scheduled 
basis. 
 Perform preventative services including: 
• mechanical cleaning and clearing of lines using jetting, snaking, and 
augering methods 
• maintenance and inspection of plumbing vents to ensure proper air 
flow and drainage performance; and 
• service of sanitary waste lines, vent lines, cleanouts, toilets, sinks, roof 
drains, and associated fixtures 
 
o Remedial Maintenance and Repairs 
 Troubleshoot and provide immediate response and services for leaks, 
blockages, and other plumbing failures. 
 Prepare written cost estimates for any installation, modification, and/or

upgrade of the plumbing systems or any components to match Original 
Equipment Manufacturer (“OEM”) specif ications and City requirements. 
Services may include repair and/or replacement of piping, drains, fixtures,

 Provide a warranty on all replacement parts and repair services for a minimum

o Emergency Repairs 
As requested by the City in writing, perform emergency maintenance and repairs to 
ensure continuous and safe operation of all plumbing systems. A two (2) hour time 
window is required from City notification to the Contractor’s expected response time. 
At the City’s discretion, the City may elect to contact a separate contractor to respond 
in an emergency if the Contractor is unable to respond within the two (2) hour window.  
 
Emergency repairs may include, but are not limited to, major water leaks, broken 
water mains, sewer back-ups, flooding, burst or frozen pipes, and hot water loss.  
 
B. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS: 
The Contractor shall provide all labor, materials, equipment, tools, vehicles and 
transportation, permits (when required), and supervision necessary to successfully perform 
services. Hourly rates shall be inclusive of on-site time, travel time and mileage, and portal-
to-portal labor. The City shall not pay separately for any travel expenses not included in the 
hourly rates. 
 
Many City-owned facilities are secured; the Contractor shall coordinate access, shutdowns, 
and restoration of service as needed in advance with City staff. Access to secured sites will 
need to be scheduled with the City for each day that the Contractor is working at secured 
sites. 
 
Per Title 3, Chapter 16 of the Littleton Municipal Code, a license or certificate of registration 
is required for contractors (individuals, busi ness entities, or any combination thereof) 
performing plumbing work within City limits. Additionally, the Contractor shall have current 
licensing as required by the Colorado State Plumbing Board, that is appropriate for the type 
of services being conducted (whether Master Plumber, Journeyman Plumber, etc.)  
 
Work shall comply with the City of Littleton Municipal Code (“Code”), and applicable 
International Council Codes, as adopted in Title 4 of the Code, including but not limited to the 
International Building Code, the International Plumbing Code, and International Mechanical 
Code, as they may be amended. Work shall also be performed according to the best trade 
practices of the industry. 
 
Manufacturers, types, sizes, outputs, controls, and other elements of the City’s existing 
services components may vary. For any replacements, all manufacturer warranties shall be 
transferred to the City. The Contractor shall use OEM parts unless unavailable; substitutes 
shall require prior written approval from the City. All materials must comply with OEM and 
EPA requirements.  
 
The Contractor shall maintain clean, organized, and hazard-free work zones, and shall be 
responsible for clean-up and compliant disposal of waste offsite. Handling and disposal of all 
waste (including hazardous materials and hazardous waste) shall be conducted by the

Section 5 - SELECTION PROCESS  
A. EVALUATION CRITERIA: 
Respondents ranked highest based on qualifications and technical elements, as evaluated by 
the City's selection committee, will be short-listed and move to the next round of City 
evaluations during which the City will evaluate pricing as part of its final award decision.  
 
Responses may be evaluated based on other relevant considerations as determined by the 
selection committee. Responses shall also be evaluated to confirm the respondent has 
conformed with the requirements of the RFQ (signed forms, etc.), and to confirm the 
respondent is not in arrears to the City, in default with the City, or otherwise subject to award 
disqualification. The City may, if it deems necessary, request clarifications, conduct 
discussions or interviews, or request best and final offers.

cancel, suspend, or modify the solicitation; to reject any or all responses; and/or to waive 
informalities, irregularities, or omissions in any responses. As the City deems necessary and 
appropriate, and in its sole discretion, the City reserves the right to make any inquiries or other 
follow-up required to verify or clarify information provided by the respondent. Any one or more 
of the following causes may result in the disqualification of a respondent and the rejection of 
a response: 
i. Failure to provide complete information, pricing, and/or documentation as required. 
ii. Submission of more than one response for the same work from an individual, firm, 
partnership, or corporation under the same or different names. 
iii. Evidence of collusion among contractors; participants in such collusion may 
receive no consideration for award for any future work. 
iv. Default, breach, or termination of other contracts pertinent to respondent’s ability 
and willingness to perform contractual obligations.  
v. Breach or violation of terms and conditions  of existing City contracts, licenses, or 
permits. 
vi. Delinquent federal, state, or local tax obligations; or outstanding debts to the City. 
vii. Federal debarment or suspension. 
viii. Omissions or fraudulent statements. 
ix. Lack of competency as revealed by the respondent’s submission, or lack of ability 
to operate as required.  
x. Uncompleted work which, in the judgment of the City, might hinder or prevent the 
prompt completion of additional work if awarded. 
xi. Conviction or indictment of a respondent or respondent’s staff for a felony involving 
violence, including sexual offenses, and/or listing of same on the Colorado sex 
offender registry or a similar sexual offender registry of another state or the federal 
government. 
xii. Not being in good standing with the Colorado Secretary of State. 
xiii. Failure to disclose potential conflicts of interest.
