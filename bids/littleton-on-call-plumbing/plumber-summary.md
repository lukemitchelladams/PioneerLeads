# City of Littleton On-Call Plumbing — Plumber Summary (short version)

Prepared by Brown Bison Holdings LLC, 2026-10-19. Brown Bison bids, holds the contract, and handles all the City
paperwork, bonding, and invoicing. The plumber does the plumbing as our sub and gets paid on every job.
Luke's instruction (2026-10-19): keep it to the bare minimum, these are plumbers, don't overwhelm them.

## The job in plain terms
- City of Littleton, about 38 City buildings (City Hall, police, fire, library, museum, service center, courthouse).
  Older buildings, older plumbing.
- Fix-it and emergency plumbing: leaks, drains, backups, water heaters, burst pipes, fixtures.
- Emergencies: you have to be on site within 2 hours of the City's call.
- 2-year contract starting December 2026, plus a 1-year option. City spends about $100,000 a year total.
- Work comes as work orders. Emergencies are billed hourly. Hourly rate includes drive time. Parts billed on top.
- 12-month warranty on parts and labor. Before-and-after photos on each job.

## The four things we need from the plumber (send Littleton-Plumbing-Partner-Summary.pdf, same content)
1. **Can you be on site at one of those City buildings within 2 hours of a call, nights and weekends included?** Yes or no.
2. **Your hourly rates:** regular hours, after-hours/weekend, and holiday. Plus your parts markup if you charge one.
3. **Your Colorado plumbing license number** (Master or Journeyman) and the name on it. The City requires it.
4. **A copy of your insurance certificate** (general liability, auto, workers' comp). Whatever you already carry.

Nice to have if easy: one or two commercial customers we can list as references.
