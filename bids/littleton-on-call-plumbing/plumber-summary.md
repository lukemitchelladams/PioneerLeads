# City of Littleton On-Call Plumbing Contract — Summary for Plumbing Partners

Prepared by Brown Bison Holdings LLC, 2026-10-19. Brown Bison is the bidder and contract holder; the plumbing
partner performs the work as our subcontractor.

## The contract (City of Littleton RFQ #2126, On-Call Commercial Plumbing Services)
- **Client:** City of Littleton, Colorado.
- **Buildings:** about 38 City-owned structures averaging 75 years old: City Center, Police Station, Fire
  Station, Bellview Service Center, Littleton Museum, Bemis Library, Municipal Courthouse, and some residential
  buildings. Older equipment, mixed manufacturers.
- **Work:** preventative, remedial, and emergency plumbing. Repair and replacement of piping, drains, fixtures,
  water heaters, and safety devices, plus testing, verification, and diagnostics.
- **Emergency response:** on site within **2 hours** of the City's call. Emergencies include major leaks, broken
  water mains, sewer backups, flooding, burst or frozen pipes, and hot water loss. If we can't make 2 hours, the
  City can call someone else for that job.
- **Term:** 2 years starting December 2026, with a 1-year option.
- **Volume:** City budget about **$100,000 per year** across all awarded contractors. No minimum guaranteed.
  Work comes as Work Orders; routine jobs are quoted, emergencies are hourly.
- **Billing:** hourly rates are portal-to-portal and include travel and mileage. Parts at OEM unless unavailable.
- **Standards:** 12-month warranty on parts and labor; before-and-after photos on every job; Littleton Municipal
  Code and International Plumbing Code; OSHA; clean work zones; offsite disposal; secured buildings require
  access scheduled with City staff each day.
- **Licensing required:** Colorado State Plumbing Board license appropriate to the work (Master or Journeyman),
  and a City of Littleton contractor license/registration (Municipal Code Title 3, Chapter 16).

## How the partnership works
- Brown Bison submits the bid (due October 28), holds the contract, posts the $100,000 performance and payment
  bond, handles City paperwork, invoicing, and the relationship with Littleton Procurement.
- The plumbing partner is named in our submission as the licensed performer, takes the Work Orders, does the
  work, and is paid on every Work Order.
- Nothing is owed by the partner to be included.

## What we need from the plumber (to put in the bid)
1. Confirmation of interest and ability to meet the 2-hour emergency window in Littleton.
2. Master plumber's name and Colorado State Plumbing Board license number (and class).
3. Whether a City of Littleton contractor registration is already held.
4. Insurance carrier and limits (general liability, auto, workers' comp).
5. Hourly rates: regular hours, after-hours/weekend, holiday; parts markup; minimum call-out.
6. Crew and fleet: number of licensed plumbers, helpers, and service trucks.
7. Two or three commercial or municipal references (name, organization, phone).
8. EPA 608 and OSHA cards held by techs, if any.
