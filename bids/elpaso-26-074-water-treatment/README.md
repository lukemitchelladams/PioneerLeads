## Scope summary
"The Consultant shall furnish all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, transportation, technical expertise, laboratory
services, and incidentals necessary to provide a comprehensive water treatment chemical program". Chemical supply (orders
"fulfilled within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt", F.O.B. destination, SDS), "twelve (12) scheduled on-site service visits
annually" (Addendum 1 A28: "Monthly visits are required for TSB, CUP and RBD only"), on-site testing each visit, corrosion
coupon analysis (A27: 2 chilled coupons semi-annual, 2 condenser coupons quarterly), written service reports, lab services,
training on request. "It is the County's preference that the successful Consultant use the County's existing pump equipment.
We currently use Walchem Webmaster One and LMI Milton Roy pumps." Swamp coolers are NOT included (Addendum 1 A5).

Facilities (RFP "County Buildings" table): Central Utility Plant, 50 E Costilla (3 steam boilers, 2 cooling towers); Regional
Building, 2880 International Circle (3 hot water boilers, 1 cooling tower, 2 swamp coolers); Citizens Service Center, 1675
Garden of the Gods (3 hot water boilers); DMV East, 5650 Industrial Place (1 hot water boiler); Technical Support Building,
325 S Cascade Ave (2 hot water boilers, 2 cooling towers); CJC, 2739 E Las Vegas (8 hot water boilers, 4 swamp coolers).

Reference products and "Approximate Quantity" (annual, all facilities per Addendum 1 A9): NALCO 356 boiler corrosion
inhibitor 40 GAL; NALCO 22332 boiler water treatment 50 GAL; NALCO 22310 boiler water treatment 60 GAL; NALCO 1720 sulfite
oxygen scavenger 120 GAL; NALCO 2584 alkalinity booster 60 GAL; NALCO ST20 inhibitor 30 GAL; NALCO 247-30 TRASAR cooling
tower treatment 75 GAL; NALCO 7330 biocide 75 GAL; NALCO 8338 sodium nitrite "As Needed" (A9: "historically been 30 Gallons
per year"). "Proposers may offer equivalent or superior products". Rule 9 satisfied: quantities are stated.

## Key requirements (quote the document, cite section)
- Competency (Special Terms): "Bids will only be considered from Consultants which have been engaged in the business of performing the Work as described in this Solicitation. Consultants must be able to produce evidence that they have an established satisfactory record of performance for a minimum of three (3) years". Minimum Consultant Qualifications: "Consultant shall have a minimum of three (3) years of experience providing commercial or institutional water treatment services for boiler and cooling systems. The assigned service representative shall possess a minimum of three (3) years of water treatment experience." RISK: Brown Bison relies on H2 Global's record as the identified subconsultant; the County may read this as the prime's record.
- Subs allowed and must be named: "SUBCONSULTANTS OF WORK SHALL BE IDENTIFIED: As part of its Response, the Consultant shall be required to identify any and all subconsultants".
- Service levels: "a qualified service representative within three (3) hours of the County's downtown facilities during normal business hours. Routine chemical orders shall be fulfilled within 24 hours of receipt. Emergency technical support shall be available 24/7" (Addendum 1 A13: on-site within 3 hours; A24: "Response within 3 hours via telephone is acceptable"; A16: 24-hour fulfillment "applies to order confirmation").
- Emergency parts: "billed at actual cost plus a maximum markup of ten percent (10%)" (A20: includes freight and subcontracted work).
- Background check: "Consultant and all subconsultants entering the facility shall submit a list of all employees, within four (4) days prior to appearance on the work site including full name, date of birth, social security number, and driver's license number".
- Insurance (Exhibit 4): Commercial General Liability "$1,000,000 each occurrence; $2,000,000 general aggregate; $2,000,000 products and completed operations aggregate"; Automobile Liability "minimum limit of $1,000,000 each accident combined single limit"; Employers' Liability "$1,000,000 bodily injury for each accident, $1,000,000 each employee for disease, $1,000,000 disease aggregate"; Umbrella/Excess "$1,000,000 each occurrence and aggregate"; Pollution Liability "If Consultant's scope of work includes any pollution liability exposure ... no less than $1,000,000 each occurrence and aggregate" (chemical handling: assume required). Subconsultants must carry the same ("shall ensure that any subconsultants procure and maintain").
- Submittal (Addendum 1): Consultant Information Form; Proprietary/Confidential Statement; Subconsultant list; Exhibit 1 Exceptions; Exhibit 2 Lobbying Certification; Exhibit 3 Non-Collusion Affidavit; Exhibit 4 Minimum Insurance; signed Cover Sheet; Addendum acknowledgement; Documentation of Meeting Minimum Requirements; Fee Proposal (separate electronic envelope). 50-page limit; SDS do not count (A24). "We prefer no El Paso County's logos used within the proposal."
- Fee schedule format: per-gallon price x estimated yearly quantity for 9 products; hourly rates for normal and emergency/holiday service with minimum hours; lab testing/reports pricing; A18: "combine all services"; A19: state the hourly rate and that there are no trip or surcharges; A26: include non-oxidizing biocide pricing.
