## Scope summary
"The Consultant shall furnish all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, transportation, technical expertise, laboratory
services, and incidentals necessary to provide a comprehensive water treatment chemical program". Chemical supply: "Maintain
adequate inventory to fulfill routine orders within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt" and "Routine chemical orders shall be
fulfilled within 24 hours of receipt" (Addendum 1 A16: the 24 hours "applies to order confirmation"; A15: does not include
weekends and holidays; A17: "Deliveries must be shipped to EPC-FSIM, 325 S. Cascade Ave, Colorado Springs, CO 80903. No minimum
order quantities."). F.O.B. destination, SDS for every product. "Powdered products shall not be used unless specifically approved
by the County."

Service: "Perform twelve (12) scheduled on-site service visits annually. Monthly service visits, as requested by the County."
ADDENDUM 1 CONFLICTS WITH ITSELF on visit scope: A4 "Should each of the 12 annual service visits cover all six facilities and
all listed equipment?" — "Yes."; A1 "Field service required monthly. Corrosion coupons are pulled monthly."; A28 "Monthly visits
are required for TSB, CUP and RBD only." Price the 12-visit, all-six-buildings basis and show the three-building saving. Coupons:
RFP "Perform corrosion coupon analysis twice (2) annually"; A27/A10 "There are 2 coupons for the chilled and they are tested
semi-annually, and there are 2 for the condenser water that are tested quarterly." On-site testing each visit, written service
reports, lab services (water, coupon, deposit, microbiological, resin, metallurgical), training on request. "It is the County's
preference that the successful Consultant use the County's existing pump equipment. We currently use Walchem Webmaster One and
LMI Milton Roy pumps." "If the existing equipment is not compatible, the Consultant will supply any chemical feed or control
equipment which is required to assure reliable operation of our boiler and cooling systems at their cost." A12: repairs from
existing equipment condition or normal wear "will be the responsibility of the water treatment consultant." Swamp coolers are
NOT included (A5). Additional services at "the same or other locations within the proximity of the Project limits" must be
honored at the agreed rates "as a condition of contract award".

Facilities (RFP "County Buildings" table): Central Utility Plant, 50 E Costilla (3 steam boilers, 2 cooling towers); Regional
Building, 2880 International Circle (3 hot water boilers, 1 cooling tower, 2 swamp coolers); Citizens Service Center, 1675
Garden of the Gods (3 hot water boilers); DMV East, 5650 Industrial Place (1 hot water boiler); Technical Support Building,
325 S Cascade Ave (2 hot water boilers, 2 cooling towers); CJC, 2739 E Las Vegas (8 hot water boilers, 4 swamp coolers).
Totals: 20 boilers, 5 cooling towers, 6 swamp coolers (excluded).

Reference products and "Approximate Quantity" (annual, all facilities per Addendum 1 A9; "Proposers may offer equivalent or
superior products"): NALCO 356 "Boiler Corrosion Inhibitor" 40 GAL; NALCO 22332 "Boiler Water Treatment" 50 GAL; NALCO 22310
"Boiler Water Treatment" 60 GAL; NALCO 1720 "Sulfide, Oxygen Scavenger" (County's wording) 120 GAL; NALCO 2584 "Alkalinity
Booster" 60 GAL; NALCO ST20 "Inhibitor" 30 GAL; NALCO 247-30 TRASAR "Cooling Tower Water Treatment" 75 GAL; NALCO 7330
"Biocide" 75 GAL; NALCO 8338 "Sodium Nitrite" "As Needed" (A9: "historically been 30 Gallons per year"); A26: also price a
non-oxidizing biocide. Rule 9 satisfied: quantities are stated.

## Key requirements (quote the document, cite section)
- Competency (Special Terms): "Bids will only be considered from Consultants which have been engaged in the business of performing the Work as described in this Solicitation. Consultants must be able to produce evidence that they have an established satisfactory record of performance for a minimum of three (3) years and have sufficient financial support, equipment, and organization to ensure that they can satisfactorily execute the services if awarded a contract." Minimum Consultant Qualifications: "Consultant shall have a minimum of three (3) years of experience providing commercial or institutional water treatment services for boiler and cooling systems. The assigned service representative shall possess a minimum of three (3) years of water treatment experience. The Consultant shall employ or have access to qualified technical personnel with degrees in engineering, chemistry, or another related scientific discipline. Personnel performing on-site testing shall possess all applicable licenses and certifications required to perform the services."
