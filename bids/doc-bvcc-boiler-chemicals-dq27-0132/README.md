## Scope summary (Exhibit A)

- "CDOC is seeking quotes from qualified bidders that can provide Boiler Chemicals and Treatment Services on site at Buena Vista Correctional Facility (BVCC) on a monthly basis." Program: "maintaining the appropriate water chemical levels for optimal performance of steam boiler system, condenser, condensate, cooling tower, or chiller water of the boiler system. Implement a treatment program which protects boilers from water scales and corrosion ... Monthly testing of water chemical composition, adding or adjusting water concentration levels, inspection of boiler functions and controls." Plus "Consult with facility staff on operational practices, provide technical guidance and support of chemical handling, and make recommendations for system care." (Exhibit A sec. 2)
- "The awarded bidder(s) will be expected to provide all necessary chemicals, materials, equipment, testing supplies, and tools to perform treatment services on site."
- Schedule: "Services must be prescheduled, one day per month, based on CTCF - Physical Plant staff agreed upon date and time. Additional calls for testing and treatment may be requested prior to the regular monthly schedule." (CTCF looks carried over from another DQ; question 2 in vendor-questions.md.)
- Response: "Must be able and prepared to respond within 24 hours for urgent or emergency service calls." "24-Hour response time to service calls." (sec. 2, 4)
- Equipment (sec. 3): "three (3) E. Keeler Williams Sport steam boilers, built in 1992. Closed loop system, blowdown two – three times every 24 hours, condensate return, hydronics delivery steam around the facility. i. 1575 BHS, heating 492,408 square feet. ii. Boiler Tubes: Fire tubes are used in the boilers. iii. Natural gas is the primary fuel source, with Propane as a backup". Feedwater: "facility staff pre-treat water coming into the facility with water softening salt and de-alkalizer as needed." Cooling tower: "Water Tower chemicals are treated by facility staff ... Bidder(s) will not be treating the water in tower but will provide testing and treatment for the boilers." "Chemical Controls: Six (6) automatic pumps, LMI Colotro magnetic pumps that feed chemicals."
- Deliverables (sec. 4): four treatment products, each "55 Gallon Drum": "Boiler Water Treatment - RMA1226" (sodium hydroxide, polymethacrylate dispersants, alkalinity builders, oxygen scavengers); "Catalyzed Oxygen Scavenger – RMA1018" (potassium hydroxide, sodium bisulfite, cobalt sulfate); "Steam Condensate treatment – RMA2060" (cyclohexylamine, morpholine neutralizing amine); "Boiler Sludge Conditioner -RMA1284" (sodium hydroxide dispersant/anti-scalant). Ten reagents/test supplies (cresol red, KI/iodate R-0633-126, N/10 sulfuric R-06275-10, total alkalinity R-0645, polymer reagents Te-7438-J/Te-7439-J, conductivity R-7022, acid starch R-0725, hardness reagent, resin cleaner). "Brand names ... are intended to aid in establishing the desired standard of quality ... you may submit a bid for a product of equal specifications and quality." (sec. 9)
- Technical/training (sec. 4): reps "possess a technical degree(s), certifications, knowledge or experience"; "Provide formal training on the handling of chemicals, automatic or manual chemical controls, care, maintenance, testing, and operation"; "Regular consulting services upon request and at least monthly."
- Site (sec. 5): medium and high security; "Some interaction or verbal contact with offenders ... may occur." Facility staff escort technicians (sec. 6).
- Performance (sec. 5): "minimum of three (3) calendar days' written notice to the single point of contact ... if unable to meet delivery schedules"; three non-deliveries "may result in the Vendor being deemed a non-responsible bidder(s), leading to disqualification from participating in all future solicitations for a period of three (3) years or indefinitely."
- Invoicing (sec. 8): "All invoices will need to be submitted via email to:" (address left blank in the document); "sent by the 15th of each month".
