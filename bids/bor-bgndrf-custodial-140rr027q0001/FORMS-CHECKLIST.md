**Risks and ambiguities (HQ handling):**
- **Post-award conference:** WBR 1452.242-80(a) (Sol p.21): "The contractor's project manager shall attend the conference.
  If the contract involves subcontractors, a representative of each major subcontractor is also required to attend." It is
  held at BGNDRF, Alamogordo. Under the HARD RULE nothing is accepted under Luke's name; HQ default (RESPONSE-DRAFT QCP-10
  and Part E): Brown Bison's on-site representative, the Site Supervisor designated in writing under PWS 8.3, attends with the
  subcontractor's representative. Whether that person satisfies "project manager", and whether a remote attendee is allowed:
  not stated in the solicitation.
- **Limitation on subcontracting:** 52.219-14 is not in the clause list (Sol pp.6-7); not stated in the solicitation. HQ
  precaution: prefer a sub that is itself small under NAICS 561720.
- **Periodic scope wording differs three ways.** Sub-CLIN 3 windows: PWS 2.3.2 A (PWS p.6): "Clean all exterior glass windows
  in doors, fire extinguisher compartments and offices, four (4) times per year as requested by the COR"; Att. 2: "Exterior
  office and lobby windows (Four times per year)"; PRS periodic 3 (PWS p.15): "All Interior and exterior office, laboratory,
  and test bay windows", AQL "All windows along test bay (room 123) and all interior office windows and windowed doors".
  Sub-CLIN 1 deep clean: PWS 2.3.2 C and Att. 2 say tile floors and baseboards; PRS periodic 1 says "Deep clean lobby,
  hallway, and restroom Floors (Including Showers) and walls". Questions are closed. HQ default: describe and price the
  wider PRS scope (RESPONSE-DRAFT 1.5); the sub prices the same.
- **Sub past performance:** Demonstrated Experience says sub expertise "shall also be included" (Sol p.23); item 3 Past
  Performance does not say whether a sub's projects count. Sol p.25: "if no record of past performance is found in CPARS, the
  contractor shall not receive a favorable or unfavorable rating but shall receive a rating of neutral." HQ default: three sub
  projects, each labeled as the subcontractor's work, plus one line that Brown Bison has no CPARS record.
- **Evaluation:** comparative, not lowest-price: "All evaluation factors other than cost or price, when combined, are
  approximately equal to cost or price" (Sol p.24). "Information not contained in an offeror's quote will not be considered
  during the evaluation." (Sol p.25)
- **Price evaluation wording:** Att. 2: "Offers will be evaluated based on the total annual costs of the base year and all
  option year CLINs." Sol p.25 (52.212-2(b)) adds the 52.217-8 extension, "calculated by prorating the last option's CLIN price
  for a period of 6 months." Att. 2 has its own extension CLINs 00110/00120, which the SF-1449 (CLINs 00010-00100) does not
  list. HQ default: fill every Att. 2 line, extension included.
- **Remote QC and photos:** PWS 7.1: "if the Contractor or Supervisor are not a local company, the QCP must address how quality
  control will be maintained." WBR 1452.237-80 Alt III (e)(5)(xv) (Sol p.20): "The use of photographic equipment and taking of
  photographs shall only be allowed as authorized by the COR." The draft QCP uses photos only if the COR authorizes them.
  Several subs asked are in Las Cruces or El Paso, so the draft keeps a non-local subcontractor paragraph in QCP-5 until the
  sub's base is known.
- **Friday after-hours visit:** PWS 5.1 (PWS p.8): "The Government will require PIV credentials for the Contractor and
  Contractor's employees that will work on evenings or weekends when Government staff are not onsite"; WBR 1452.237-80 Alt III
  (e)(5)(vii) (Sol p.19): "No Contractor personnel shall be permitted access to the work site after the Government's
  established working hours without prior authorization from the Government." Missed-visit notice must come "during normal
  business hours but no later than two (2) hours after the scheduled start time" (PWS 2.1); normal hours end 4:30 pm (PWS
  pp.1-2). The draft (1.4, QCP-7, QCP-11) conditions the Friday visit on both and sends any cancellation before 4:30 pm.
- **Two correction windows:** QC-inspection deficiencies "no later than three workdays following the inspection" (PWS 7.1);
  Government-found nonconformance "remedied within seven (7) business days for routine cleaning tasks or within two weeks for
  those tasks that need to be performed quarterly or annually" (PWS 7.2.2). Make-up cleanings within "seven (7) calendar days
  of the missed cleaning" (PWS 2.1). The QCP answers all three.
- PRS 7.3 item 4 AQL reads "no more than 3 complaints received during." (text cut off in the PWS); read as "during calendar
  year" like the other rows.
