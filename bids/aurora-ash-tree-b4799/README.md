## Scope summary
"Emerald Ash Borer (EAB) was first detected in the City of Aurora in June 2025". "Aurora manages approximately 4,700 ash trees
across parks, streetscapes, and public facilities." Treatment A: "Emamectin Benzoate (minimum 4% active ingredient) Application:
Trunk injection at the root flare using manufacturer-approved closed-system injection equipment"; soil drenching as the second
method. "DBH measured at 4.5 feet above ground using standard measurement rules." "Contractor shall calculate dosage strictly
according to product label, based on DBH and soil conditions." Watering "10 gallons/dbh inch" after treatment; no soil drenching
in buffer zones near waterways. PROS-Forestry may observe applicators on site.

## Key requirements (quote the document, cite section)
- Licensing: "Applicators must have valid Qualified Supervisor (QS) license from Colorado" (CDA commercial applicator rules); "Applicators must attend and pass manufacturer training for the selected product and injection equipment; proof of training must be provided."
- Business license: "obtain an Aurora Business License ... within the first thirty (30) days of contract award."
- Insurance: Sample Service Agreement and Insurance Requirements (Form 410-33), Section VII — limits not yet quoted here.
- Funding: "Funds are currently not available for the award beyond this current calendar year"; renewals subject to appropriation.
