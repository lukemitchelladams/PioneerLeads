## Scope summary (Part 4)

"The City of Lakewood seeks qualified Contractor(s) to provide emergency board up, temporary fencing, and site security services for properties throughout the City following police-initiated events (including search warrants, forced entry, and crime scenes) that require immediate securing of a structure." (Part 4 p.1). "Site security" here means fencing and securing openings; no guard, patrol or alarm service is described anywhere in the RFP.

- **Board-up (base scope):** "Board-up of residential and commercial entry doors; Board-up of windows and other exterior glazing openings; Board-up of commercial storefronts and large glass openings; Board-up of garage doors and other oversized building openings; Temporary securing of other exterior openings identified by authorized City personnel" (sec. 1).
- **Temporary fencing:** "Furnish and install of temporary fencing to secure damaged structures, vacant properties, demolition sites, hazardous areas, or restricted sites; Providing temporary fencing gates, locks, bracing, sandbags, and other necessary security components; Relocation, maintenance, repair, and removal of temporary fencing when authorized by the City." Headed "(OPTIONAL) Temporary Fencing and Site Security Requirements": 6'-0" minimum galvanized chain-link panels, freestanding interlocking panels, heavy-duty bases "without permanent attachment unless otherwise approved", sandbags/ballast, diagonal bracing at corners, gates and long runs, lockable gates, chains/padlocks/clamps, privacy screening and warning signage "when specifically requested by the City", surface protection ("Ground penetration or permanent anchoring shall not be permitted without prior City authorization"), maintenance through the rental period, removal and clean-up. "(OPTIONAL) Temporary Fencing Rental and Maintenance": "Provide daily, weekly, and monthly rental options", prompt repair/reposition, relocation, removal on notice, restore disturbed areas; "shall not extend rental periods or perform additional chargeable services on the City's account without authorization".
- **Not in base scope:** "Debris removal, hazard mitigation (e.g., broken glass, exposed wiring), and related services are not included in the base scope but may be offered by the Contractor as a separate, optional service." (sec. 1).
- **Availability:** "Respondents providing emergency board-up services shall be able to provide these services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, including holidays." (Part 1 sec. I).
- **Response time:** no number is imposed; the respondent sets it and is scored on it. "Provide guaranteed response time (calculated from time of notification from the City to on-site arrival)." (Part 1 sec. II.B.3); "Proposed response time(s)" is an evaluation criterion (Part 2 sec. IV).
- **Materials (sec. 2):** "Board-up services shall primarily be performed using plywood. Alternative materials, including steel panels, may be utilized when plywood is insufficient ... and when approved by the City's authorized representative." "Utilize exterior-grade or other weather-resistant plywood of sufficient thickness (typically a minimum of 5/8 inch thickness)"; cut to cover openings with bearing and fastening surfaces; minimize damage to frames, masonry, siding; "Avoid unnecessary penetrations into structural members".
- **Site coordination (sec. 3):** coordinate with the responding Lakewood PD representative; "shall not enter restricted areas or begin work until authorized"; responsible for damage from its operations; at crime scenes "comply with instructions from law enforcement personnel regarding site access, evidence preservation, and restricted areas"; "shall not remove, disturb, or dispose of potential evidence without authorization"; responsibility "is limited to temporarily securing the designated openings or areas", not certifying structural condition or habitability.
- **Service reports (sec. 4):** one per dispatch, "submitted within two business days following completion of the initial work": address and date; requesting City representative; "Time of notification, dispatch acknowledgment, arrival, and completion"; openings secured with dimensions; materials; before-and-after photographs; for fencing the linear footage, gates, components, rental start/period/removal dates; itemized costs per the contract fee schedule; "Documentation of efforts to identify, contact, and obtain payment from the property owner, when applicable". Photos and reports "shall not be publicly distributed by the Contractor without authorization".
- **Pricing structure (sec. 6), Board-Up:** "1. Standard residential window board-up. 2. Standard exterior door board-up. 3. Commercial storefront and oversized openings. 4. Garage door openings. 5. Emergency mobilization. 6. After-hours, weekend, and holiday services. 7. Additional labor or specialty materials, if applicable." **Temporary Fencing:** "1. Emergency delivery and mobilization. 2. Installation and removal. 3. Daily, weekly, and monthly rental rates per linear foot. 4. Temporary gates and locking devices. 5. Additional bracing, anchoring, or stabilization beyond standard installation requirements. 6. Relocation or modification of installed fencing. 7. Emergency repairs or replacement. 8. Privacy screening or windscreen materials, when requested. 9. After-hours, weekend, and holiday charges, if applicable." "The standard temporary fencing unit price shall include 6'-0" chain-link fence panels, bases, clamps, sandbags, and stabilization materials necessary for a typical installation." "All pricing shall clearly identify whether labor, equipment, materials, transportation, installation, and cleanup are included." Fees for board-up are "inclusive of materials and disposal costs" (Part 1 sec. II.B.2). Only firm prices are considered (Part 2 sec. VI).

## Risks / why NO
1. **Payer of last resort.** Brown Bison would pay the board-up sub on every police-initiated job and then collect from a private owner whose door the police just forced; the City pays only when the owner is "confirmed deceased and no dependents can be located" or "cannot be identified or located after diligent, documented efforts" (Part 4 sec. 5). That is a collections business, not facilities maintenance.
2. **No volume stated:** no dispatch count, annual spend or NTE figure anywhere; the NTE is filled in at award (Agreement IV.A). Rule 9: do not rank GO without proven volume.
3. 24/7/365 availability and a scored "guaranteed response time" rest entirely on the sub; Brown Bison is a dispatch layer between Lakewood PD and the sub at 3 a.m.
4. Fencing is a rental vendor's product (per-linear-foot daily/weekly/monthly rates); our margin on a rental vendor's rate card is thin unless the City's fencing volume is real.
5. Service report with photos within two business days per dispatch and owner-contact documentation: administrative load per job.
6. Subcontractor consent is "not unreasonably withheld" but must be in writing (Agreement III.B); ask for it at award.
