## Scope summary (Exhibit A)

"Thornton requires one or more firms to perform re-surfacing with post-tensioned concrete, crack repair, lighting, fencing,
drainage, landscape/irrigation rehabilitation, and replacing posts, nets, or other necessary repairs and equipment." (p.3;
Agreement II.A). "The required services may include and be for, routine maintenance, a specific project or for urgent or
emergency services." (Exhibit A.A).

- **Sites (Exhibit A.A):** City Hall, Justice Center, New Public Safety Facility (13150 Quebec St), Maintenance Service Center,
  Active Adult Center, Arts and Culture Center, Infrastructure Maintenance Center, Fleet, Park Village Pool, Carpenter Rec
  Center, Thorncreek Golf Course, Community Pool, Community Center, Wes Brown Water Treatment Plant, Fire Stations 1-5,
  Police Shooting Range, "Other Thornton Facilities as needed".
- **Standards (B.1):** IBC, Mile High Flood District, ADA, IPEMA, CPSC, ASTM, ACI, Post-Tensioning Institute; "Design shall meet
  or exceed American Sports Builders Association guidelines" (B.5).
- **Design-side duties per project:** stamped engineering designs, drawings and specs (B.5); "The Engineer of Record shall be
  required to inspect any structural foundations such as the post-tensioned concrete and provide a certified letter" (B.5);
  Estimate of Probable Construction Cost by unit price "including a construction contingency allowance of at least ten
  percent (10%)" (B.6); field survey within 30 ft by a Colorado-licensed surveyor (B.7); geotechnical report with borings
  (B.8); laser grading (B.9); drainage study (B.4); lighting and site-furnishing evaluations (B.12, B.16); permits (B.13).
- **Construction:** post-tensioned concrete "a minimum of 6" thick" to ACI and PTI (B.14); "court surfacing, painting, and
  striping in accordance with Thornton Design Guidelines" (B.15); demolition haul-off incidental (B.3); landscape and irrigation
  "Repair and/or revegetation will be required only to adapt to new construction" (B.11); waste disposal (B.17).
- **Hours:** regular 7:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m. Mon-Fri; overtime rates all other times (Exhibit C).

## Rule 8 check (no excavation / earthwork / heavy civil)

Sport courts are not ponds, pump stations or roadwork, and most Task Assignments will be crack repair, resurfacing, striping,
nets, posts, fence and light repair. But Exhibit A also lists drainage, grading, geotech borings and 6-inch post-tensioned slab
reconstruction. The RFP lets the proposer define its "range of services offered" (V.B.5) and scores the "scope of services
offered" (IV.H.1), so Brown Bison offers court trades only (post-tensioned concrete through the PT sub) and writes grading,
drainage and earthwork as not offered. If the City answers that every Service Provider must take all of Exhibit A, this goes
NO-BID under rule 8.

## Pricing from documents (rate-sheet style, Exhibit C)

The proposal prices no project; it fills Exhibit C, "the sole basis of payment for the successful firm" (V.B.4):
1. Labor-burdened billable hourly rates, regular and overtime, per job title.
2. Owned-equipment hourly rates (else FHWA Blue Book).
3. Markup percentages on T&M costs: "Insurance costs ___%", "Bond costs ___%", "Overhead and Profit ___%". This is where Brown
   Bison's fee sits on top of the sub's bid costs.
4. The 12-line Unit Price Schedule, which the proposer writes. Lines to offer: crack repair (per LF), acrylic resurfacing (per
   SF and per court), line striping (per court, tennis / pickleball / basketball), net replacement (each), post replacement
   (pair), windscreen (per LF), chain-link fabric repair (per LF), court light lamp or fixture replacement (each),
   mobilization (each), emergency mobilization (each).
