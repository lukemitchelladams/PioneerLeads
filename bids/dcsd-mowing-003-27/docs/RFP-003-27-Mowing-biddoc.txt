General
      a. Furnish all supervision, labor, materials, equipment, and transportation required to
      maintain the landscape in an attractive condition throughout the school year as specified
      below.
      b. Check in with the site’s office before performing services.
      c. All work will be done to avoid interference with normal business at the buildings and
      grounds. Mowing of areas that are occupied must be scheduled around school sponsored activities.
      d. All workers will dress appropriately. T-shirts with company logo will be required at all
      times. As well as appropriate dress for safety.
      e. All workers will use appropriate safety equipment. Safety of the people on the property
      will be the first consideration for all activities. No equipment will be left running
      unattended. Parking and equipment space utilization will not impede traffic patterns or
      parking access by building/grounds occupants.
      f. Contract period will be determined by DCSD Grounds Supervisor and will run
      approximately from beginning of April (beginning of growing season) until
      approximately the end of October (end of growing season). Actual start and end
      dates will be determined by the DCSD Grounds Supervisor, and Supplier will be
      expected to meet these dates. No more than a 3% price increase will be
      considered upon renewals. No increase is preferred.
      g. A monthly meeting may be led by personnel of Supplier and DCSD Grounds
      Supervisor to discuss the status of contract and performance.
      h. Native Mow must be completed by the last day of every month. Otherwise,
      payment will be made upon completion of work.

Mowing Areas
     a. All sites will be mowed in their entirety. Quantity of mowing and areas to be
     mowed are the responsibility of the Supplier to verify.
     b. All questions and/or clarification of mowing areas must be addressed directly to
     DCSD Grounds Manager, James Korth (jpkorth@dcsdk12.org).

4
       c. All areas shall be mowed to a height of three (3) inches during each week of the
       contracted period. “A week” for the purpose of this scope of work is defined as
       Monday through Sunday. All mowing will occur according to the DCSD Grounds
       Manager-approved schedule each week. The schedule will be submitted ten
       days prior to the start of the mowing season. The owner must agree to variations
       to the specific mowing schedule when the mowing schedule is interrupted by
       adverse weather conditions (including, but not limited to, rain/snow).
       d. If there is inclement weather, adjustments to the mowing schedule are allowed
       within the same week it was scheduled to be mowed. However, the change of
       mowing date will need to be approved in advance with DCSD Grounds
       Manager. The Supplier will agree to the date/method most appropriate to the
       DCSD Grounds Manager, when an agreement cannot be reached between both
       parties.
       e. All native grass areas will be mowed once per month at all contracted sites. The
       standard native mow procedures include mowing the perimeter of the
       property/fence lines and all sidewalks adjacent to native grass areas with a 6-foot
       beauty band at a 3-inch height. Along with the 6-foot beauty band mow, some sites will be a full native
       mow once a month.

Trimming
     a. Trimming of all areas associated with the mowing areas will be done on a weekly
     basis.
     b. All trimming around structures, fences, trees, shrubs, baseball/football equipment
     areas, and/or near fields and tracks, etc., is to be included.
     c. Trimming will be done at the same height as the adjacent turf (three inches). Nonselective herbicides
     may be used as a substitute for trimming; however, prior
     approval by the DCSD Grounds Manager is required.
     d. All trimming shall be done with nylon weed whips. Tree trunks shall not be hit
     with the mowers or power weed whips. The Supplier will replace any trees
     that are damaged by trimming or mowing too close

Edging
      a. Edging of all sidewalks and asphalt walkways will be done one time per month.
      b. Residual debris from the edging process will be cleaned up and removed from the
      site.
      c. Edging with string trimmers is not acceptable.

Cleaning/Sweeping
      a. All clippings will be removed from surrounding surfaces, including but not
      limited to sidewalks and walkways. No visible accumulation of clippings is
      allowed in gutters, parking lots, etc.
      b. All fuel or oil spills of any type will be cleaned up appropriately and
      immediately.
      c. At the discretion of the DCSD Grounds Manager, the Supplier will remove
      clippings that accumulate on the turf. Sweeping of all turf is not necessary, but
      piles and swaths of excessive clippings shall be removed.
      d. All grass clippings must be removed from sites.
Weed Control
      a. An early pre-emergence spray is recommended to be applied in all rock areas,
5
          mulch and shrub beds, and hard surfaces (such as sidewalks and walk paths).
          b. On a weekly basis, the Supplier will inspect all rock areas, sports field weeds
          (shot put/discus areas and baseball/softball warning tracks), mulch and shrub
          beds, and hard surfaces and either spray or pull weeds to keep these areas weedfree.
          c. All chemicals used to spray for weeds must first be approved by DCSD Grounds
          Manager, James Korth.
          d. Supplier must email SDS sheets to DCSD Grounds Manager, James Korth, at
          jpkorth@dcsdk12.org prior to the start of the mowing season.

Inclement Weather
      a. Absolutely no mowing will be allowed while rain is falling.
      b. Grass will not be cut if the turf areas are saturated. DCSD officials will make the
      final determination if there is a concern.

Additional Mows
       a. In the event of heavy turf growth (in the months of May and August), DCSD may
       request an additional mow on athletic turf surfaces.
       b. The request may require one (1) additional mow per week to all baseball, soccer,
       lacrosse, and softball fields until it is determined by the DCSD Grounds Manager
       that additional work is no longer needed.

Additional Services
       a. Aeration of all yards around the building
       • Once a year in the spring
       • Clean/blow all aeration plugs on hard surfaces
       • Must avoid all valve boxes
       b. Fertilizations of all yards around the building
       • Once a year in the spring, on a no contact day.
       • Product called 30-2-12 One and Done Fertilization is compatible upon DCSD
       Grounds Manager approval
       • Blow all granules from hard surfaces
       • Prefer this to be completed on a non-contact day
       • Proof of fertilization is required and must be provided to the DCSD Grounds
       Manager. This can be in the form of an invoice for the purchase of the fertilizer.
       c. Broadleaf
       • Spraying of all blue grass. To be completed on a no contact day.
       DCSD Grounds Manager will determine dates to start this one-time application.

Billing
          a. DCSD shall be invoiced once per month in arrears (after services have been
          provided).
          4
          b. Each monthly invoice shall include the following details: location name, number
          of weeks serviced per location, fee per week, and total monthly cost per location.
          c. Invoices may not be submitted until all work has been completed. This includes
          Native Mow. Invoices shall be emailed to Jane Jenkins at
          Jane.Jenkins@dcsdk12.org in order to be paid on time.

      • School Activities
      • Athletic Events
      • Classroom Schedules
      • Student Activities
      • Student Lunch Periods, Recesses
      • Sprinkler System Watering Schedules
      • Fertilizing of Lawns
      • Striping of Athletic Fields
      • Time and dates will be provided to the Supplier for these functions at each site. It

      to beginning work when school is in session.
      b. All turf will be mowed at a height of three (3) inches, with blades sharpened
      weekly.
      c. All turf will be mowed once within a seven (7) day cycle.
      d. All sidewalk/curb areas are to be edged to street entrances and the main sidewalks
      one time each month.
      e. All areas along turfed fence lines, areas where mowers cannot reach, such as
      around trees, telephone poles, warning tracks, etc., are to be trimmed (nylon weed
      whips). Tree trunks shall not be hit with the mowers or power weed whips. The
      Supplier will replace any trees that are damaged by trimming or mowing too
      close.
      f. Excess mulch (from mowing) will be picked up and hauled away within one day
      (not disposed of in DCSD dumpsters). Excess mulch to be determined by
      DCSD Grounds Supervisor.
      g. All sidewalks, parking lots, and driveways where excessive amounts of mulch
      collect are to be blown, swept, or picked up, and hauled away.
      h. Any damage to school property (including turf, trees, shrubs, sprinkler heads,
      valve boxes, equipment ruts, excessive scalping) will be repaired or replaced at
      the Supplier’s cost in a timely manner.
      i. The mowing contract will begin and end on the dates established by the DCSD
      Grounds Supervisor. The final mowing will be scheduled to clean all debris and
      leaves from the site. Final payment will be made after inspection of all sites is
      completed by the DCSD Grounds Supervisor.
      j. The DCSD Grounds Supervisor will determine the mowing schedule during times
      of drought. There may be times when weekly mowing will be skipped.
