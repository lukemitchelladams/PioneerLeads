# Appendix A. Safety and Student Protection Plan

## Safety policy

Students, staff and the public come before the schedule at every site, on every visit. No mowing deadline justifies an
unsafe act. This plan applies to everyone Brown Bison assigns to the District, including YMS Landscaping's crews,
supervisors and substitutes. Brown Bison is accountable for it, audits it every month, and updates it whenever an incident,
a near miss or a District request shows it can be better.

Every worker has the authority, and the duty, to stop work when anything looks unsafe, with no penalty for using it.

Luke Adams, Owner, Brown Bison Holdings LLC

## 3. Working around students

1. **Check in, every visit.** The crew lead signs in at the main office on arrival and signs out on leaving. Crews wear
   company-logo shirts and carry photo ID at all times.
2. **Background checks before day one.** No one sets foot on District property until cleared: ⟪a national criminal search
   (Checkr), a Colorado Bureau of Investigation fingerprint check and a search of the national sex-offender registry, repeated
   every year⟫. Following RFP §12.7, no one with a felony or any offense involving unlawful sexual behavior or children is ever
   assigned. Brown Bison keeps a roster of cleared workers and provides a Verification of Background Check whenever the
   District asks. A substitute who is not on the roster is not sent.
3. **No contact with students.** Crews do not talk with, photograph or hand anything to students. Any student question goes
   to the school office.
4. **Stop zone.** Mower blades stop when anyone comes within 100 feet, trimmers within 50 feet. Discharge always points away
   from people, windows and vehicles. Areas in use are never mowed.
5. **Timing.** Playing fields are finished by 2:30 PM on school days. Work around buildings waits until classes end. Every
   school's recess, lunch, PE and athletics times are built into the weekly route.
6. **Emergencies.** Crews follow DCSD's Standard Response Protocol. On any announcement they shut equipment down and do what
   school staff direct.
7. **Vehicles.** Trucks and trailers park only where the office directs, never in fire lanes, bus loops or drop-off lanes, and
   do not move during arrival or dismissal. A spotter guides every trailer that backs up. Speed on campus is 5 mph.
8. **No tobacco, vaping, marijuana or alcohol** anywhere on school property, parking lots included.

## 4. Hazards and controls

| Hazard | Controls |
|---|---|
| Thrown objects | Walk each area for rocks and debris first; discharge chutes and guards in place; stop zone (Section 3.4); safety glasses |
| Slopes and rollover | Zero-turns run across slopes only where the operator's manual allows; rollover bar up and seat belt on; slopes beyond the limit are trimmed or walk-mowed |
| Lightning | Work stops at the first thunder or lightning; crews shelter in vehicles; work resumes 30 minutes after the last thunder |
| Heat | Water, shade and scheduled rest breaks; crews watch each other for signs of heat illness; work shifts earlier on extreme-heat days |
| Noise | Hearing protection for every operator of mowers, trimmers and blowers |
| Traffic and parking lots | High-visibility vests; cones around trailers; blowing directed away from cars and walkways |
| Fuel and chemicals | Refuel with engines off and cool, away from buildings and people; spill kit on every trailer; chemicals handled only by the licensed applicator |
| Sharp blades | Engines off and keys removed before clearing a deck or changing a blade; cut-resistant gloves |

## 5. Fertilizer and weed control

1. Every application is made by a Colorado Department of Agriculture licensed commercial applicator:
   Adam Perrigoue, license #34548. Brown Bison does not apply pesticides itself.
2. Every product is approved by the Grounds Manager before use, and its safety data sheet is emailed to him before the season.
3. Fertilization and the broadleaf application happen only on no-contact days, on dates the Grounds Manager sets.
4. Treated areas are posted with yellow signs at every entrance at the time of application. Areas stay closed until the
   product is dry or as the label requires. Registrants on the state's Pesticide-Sensitive Registry get the required notice.
5. Application records (product, EPA registration number, rate, area, weather and applicator) are kept for three years and
   given to the District on request.

## 6. Equipment and crew safety

1. **Daily pre-start check.** Every mower and trimmer is inspected before use: blade guards, discharge chutes, safety
   interlocks, rollover bars and seat belts. The crew lead records the check. Anything defective is tagged out until repaired.
2. **PPE.** Safety glasses, hearing protection, high-visibility vests and work boots. Each trailer carries a first-aid kit,
   a spill kit and a fire extinguisher. At least one person on every crew is trained in first aid and CPR.
3. **Daily briefing.** Before the first site, the crew lead covers the day's schools, events, weather and any hazard reported
   the day before.

## 8. Audits and enforcement

1. Brown Bison audits at least ⟪one site visit a month⟫ unannounced for check-in, PPE, stop-zone practice and equipment
   condition. Findings go to YMS in writing, with a date for each correction.
2. A first violation means retraining that day. A violation of the student-protection rules in Section 3 means immediate
   removal of that worker from all District sites.
3. Near misses are reported and reviewed the same way as incidents, so the cause is fixed before anyone is hurt.

## 9. Incident reporting

1. Call 911 for any emergency.
2. Tell the school office right away.
3. Notify the DCSD Grounds Manager and DCSD Security (303-387-9999) within the hour, then Brown Bison.
4. Send a written report to the District within 24 hours, with the cause and the corrective action taken.
5. Report any damage to District property the same day and repair it at our cost.
