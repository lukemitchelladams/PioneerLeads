# Appendix C. Crews, Equipment and Weekly Route

## Crews

- **Primary crew:** four people, including the foreman, running a six-day route (Monday through Saturday).
- **Second crew:** four people, including a foreman. It covers weather recovery, extra athletic-field mows (May and August),
  monthly native mowing and heavy weeks. Known extra work is scheduled in advance.
- **Field operations manager:** Alex Ditto, YMS Landscaping. He sets daily assignments and checks completed sites.
- **Crew leads:**
  - Greg Hitchcock: seven years with YMS, several as crew lead. Crew organization and mowing operations.
  - Luis Chavez: two years with YMS. Bilingual; runs large and small crews.
  - Garrett Sission: one year with YMS. Maintenance operations and daily crew coordination.

## Weekly route (subject to the District's approval)

YMS will keep the District's established route and schedule from previous years if the District confirms it has worked well.
Otherwise the route is:

| Day | Sites |
|---|---|
| Monday | Thunder Ridge HS, Ranch View MS |
| Tuesday | Highlands Ranch HS, Cresthill MS |
| Wednesday | Mountain Vista HS, Mountain Ridge MS, Rock Canyon HS, Rocky Heights MS |
| Thursday | Chaparral HS, VALE, Sierra MS, Cimarron MS, Legend HS |

| Saturday | Castle View HS, Castle Rock MS, Douglas County HS, Mesa MS ⟦ALEX: move to weekdays, or Saturdays only when no games or events are scheduled?⟧ |

- **Base:** crews start and return at 8187 W Brandon Dr, Unit 24, Littleton.
- **Daily order:** playing fields first, finished by 2:30 PM on school days; building areas after dismissal.
- **Monthly work:** edging and native mowing are scheduled by area and finished before month-end.
- **Spring work:** aeration, fertilization and broadleaf are booked on the District's no-contact days.
