## Competitiveness playbook for a Colorado school-district HVAC bid
These are the levers that decide school-district HVAC awards. Apply each against the actual document when it lands.

**Price structure**
- Districts usually score PM lump sum + hourly rates + parts markup. Keep the three internally consistent; evaluators flag unbalanced bids.
- Price PM per building from an actual equipment count (RTUs, boilers, chillers, split systems, exhaust fans, BAS). Do not price per square foot.
- Summer is when schools do the heavy work. Load your labor plan for June–August and price regular-hours labor accordingly; after-hours should be the exception.
- Quote parts at cost + a flat, stated markup. Districts compare the markup number directly.
- Offer a multi-year price hold or a capped escalator (e.g., CPI-U Denver, max 3%). Renewal-year pricing is scored.

**Hard qualifiers that eliminate bidders**
- Colorado school districts require fingerprint background checks for anyone on site (C.R.S. 22-32-109.8). Have the process ready and say so.
- Illegal alien affidavit / E-Verify (C.R.S. 8-17.5-102). Keystone Colorado public-contract form.
- Insurance: expect $1M/$2M GL, $1M auto, umbrella, WC, district as additional insured, waiver of subrogation.
- EPA 608 universal for every tech; NATE or manufacturer certs on the specific equipment (Trane, Carrier, York, Daikin) are scored.
- Mandatory pre-bid or site walk. Missing it is disqualifying.

**Scored narrative (where a smaller firm beats a big one)**
- Response time commitment: state a specific number (e.g., 2 hours emergency, 24 hours routine) that beats the RFP minimum.
- Named lead tech and backup, with certs. Districts want faces, not a dispatch board.
- Three references from K-12 or municipal facilities. Town of Parker, Estes Park, or any district work goes first.
- Local presence: Parker/Castle Rock/Highlands Ranch address or shop. DCSD likes vendors inside the county.
- Safety: EMR under 1.0, written safety program, OSHA 10/30 cards.
- Reporting: offer a per-visit digital PM report with photos and a quarterly deficiency list. Cheap to promise, heavily scored.
- Small/local/veteran/minority business status if Brown Bison qualifies. Say it on page one.

**Vendor leverage**
